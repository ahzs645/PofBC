# BC Parks Foundation visual identity studies

PofBC reconstructed these applications from the Foundation’s September 2025 launch. Each SVG opens independently. Photographic studies contain an embedded raster layer. The original board gives the most reliable comparison for layout and type.

## Fonts

Chi-Long Trieu designed Basel for Optimo. The Foundation playbook uses Basel Classic Black, Basel Grotesk Medium and Basel Grotesk Regular. Optimo supplies the trial and licensed font files through its [Basel family page](https://optimo.ch/typefaces/basel).

The three upright Aa samples preserve the foundry’s native paths. Classic Black comes from [Basel Classic, PDF page 3](https://optimo.ch/uploads/0423/91137346e49b25429d7ec666b1437e4236a35dae1554c253b710eb8a9420a3f2/Optimo-BaselClassic-Specimen.pdf#page=3). Grotesk Regular and Medium come from [Basel Grotesk, PDF page 2](https://optimo.ch/uploads/0423/df0e2e5bebacc61e61b17bc3969e16ae956619440784c24688abac596d164fec/Optimo-BaselGrotesk-Specimen.pdf#page=2). The typesetting template requests installed Basel Grotesk and includes a system fallback. The poster headlines preserve the fixed letter shapes visible on the published board.
