TeX Gyre Heros and TeX Gyre Termes are compatible substitutes for Helvetica and
Times, respectively. They are not authenticated historical printing cuts.

Original OpenType fonts and GUST Font License were obtained from the CTAN
TeX Gyre distribution: https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/tex-gyre.zip
Project: https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/tex-gyre

Copyright and authorship are retained in each original OpenType name table.
The included GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt applies to these font programs. Editable
document SVGs embed WOFF2 subsets derived from these faces so their lettering
does not depend on a viewer's local font installation. The subsets retain
Latin characters plus the existing page's additional characters.

TeX Gyre Cursor supplies compatible Courier lettering. Noto Sans and Noto Sans Math supply missing fraction, prime and mathematical glyphs under their accompanying open font licence.
