# Posters and signs to recreate next — candidates, 16 November 2026

A web search for more printed pieces and signs of the kind already in the **Posters** and **Signs**
views: issued by the Province, one of its branches or a B.C. Crown corporation, with a picture of it
viewable online, and preferably flat-colour work that can be redrawn as vectors.

How to read this:

- **Seen** means the page was loaded and the picture looked at during the search. **Record only**
  means the catalogue entry was read but no picture could be viewed. **Snippet** means a search
  result said so and the page itself could not be opened.
- Dates marked *guess* are read from the style or from which department is named, not from the source.
- No rights statement was found unless one is noted. Nothing here is a clearance to publish; it is a
  list of leads. Record what the holding institution says before a piece goes on the site.
- Nothing was downloaded into the repository. Four of the Forest Discovery Centre scans (posters 1 to 3
  and 11) were fetched once to confirm the links and descriptions below, and not kept.

### Where to look next, and what was out of reach

**Worth another pass**

- **BC Forest Discovery Centre catalogue.** Six new Forest Service posters and the Gobbler bag came
  from a handful of search terms; there are likely more.
- **UBC’s BC Historical Newspapers.** Items 7 and 8 are two advertisements from a blog post; the
  papers themselves will hold many more Forest Service advertisements, at high resolution.
- **BC Archives** (search-bcarchives.royalbcmuseum.bc.ca) could not be reached at all. Search results
  say it holds a Forest Service public-information series with fire-prevention posters, a “’71 is the
  year” piece and the official Expo 86 records. This is the largest gap, and worth a visit by hand.

**Nothing found this time:** ICBC and CounterAttack, Power Smart, BC Parks printed pieces beyond the
Gobbler bag’s back, more ferry decals, PGE and BC Rail, BCLC, the Fish and Wildlife Branch, Keep B.C.
Green as a campaign of its own.

**Blocked from the search environment** (they refused automated requests; all open in a browser):
City of Vancouver Archives, UNBC Northern BC Archives, UVic, David Rumsey, the Yukon archives,
Whistler and City of Victoria archives, Heritage Burnaby’s search page, eBay, Flickr, Wikimedia
Commons, tranbc.ca.

**Not tried:** UBC Open Collections’ own search, the Museum of Vancouver, Vancouver Public Library,
SFU, the Forest History Association of BC newsletters, the Royal BC Museum’s object database.
