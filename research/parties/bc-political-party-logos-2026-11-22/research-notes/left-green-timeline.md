# BC CCF, BC NDP and BC Greens: evidence timeline

Prepared 2026-11-22. Dates below distinguish dated use, file-history observations and organizational events. A dated specimen proves use on that date; it does not establish the first or last date of use. Colourways and file formats share a `design_id` in the manifest.

The visual observations establish several distinct arrangements. They do not provide a complete dated succession of every change. In particular, both upright and italic BC NDP sun-box designs appear in the evidence associated with 2017; upload chronology must not be used to invent a definitive order of adoption.

## Organizational and naming context

| Date | Event | Evidence and interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| 1961-10-27 to 1961-10-28 | Provincial NDP founding convention | The [BC NDP History Working Group timeline](https://bcndphistory.ca/timeline/) dates the provincial founding convention at the Bayshore in Vancouver. Its [1960s account](https://bcndphistory.ca/timeline/the-1960s/) describes CCF, labour and New Party clubs participating in the new party. This should be represented as the formation of a successor political organization through those partners, not reduced to a logo change. The independent history website states it is not sponsored by BC NDP. |
| February 1983 | BC Greens formed | The party's [About Us page](https://bcgreens.ca/about-us/) supplies this formation date. No formation-era artwork was recovered in this pass. |
| 2009-04-08 | Registered name changed from New Democratic Party of B.C. to BC NDP | [Elections BC, Electoral History of British Columbia supplement 2002–2013](https://elections.bc.ca/docs/rpt/2002-2013-ElectionHistory.pdf). This is a naming event; the evidence does not establish a simultaneous logo adoption. |
| Current constitution | Green Party Political Association of British Columbia | The [BC Greens constitution and bylaws](https://bcgreens.ca/constitution-and-bylaws/) gives this organizational name. Public-facing wordmarks “Green Party of BC”, “Greens of British Columbia” and “BC Greens” should not automatically be treated as three legally separate parties. |

No amalgamation is inferred merely from similarity between logos or changes in their wording.
