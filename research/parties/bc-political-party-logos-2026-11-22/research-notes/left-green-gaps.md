# Search coverage and gaps: CCF / NDP / Greens

## What is included

27 source assets across 10 design IDs: 18 official originals, 3 third-party vectors, 1 archival raster and 5 dated source extractions. There are 27 display previews. The ten IDs include one CCF campaign-lettering specimen, one NDP riding campaign lockup and one Greens leader-name lockup; they are not ten independent standardized party identities.

- BC NDP: four generic visible design arrangements and a local riding lockup, including dated 1983, 1996, 2005 and 2017 campaign specimens; modern official orange/white EPS and PNG; two historical Commons vectors.
- BC CCF: a dated 1949 campaign-lettering specimen, attributed in print to the CCF Campaign Committee.
- BC Greens: a sunflower logo, “greens / OF BRITISH COLUMBIA”, current “BC greens” suite, and Emily Lowan leader lockup. Different colours and formats share their design IDs.

## Unresolved coverage

1. **CCF standardized logos and early decades.** No securely attributable standalone BC CCF logo master was recovered. The 1949 specimen is distinctive campaign lettering, and is labelled that way. A 1956 CCF convention photograph from the official BC NDP history page shows event lettering but was excluded because it does not establish a party logo.
2. **BC NDP 1961–1995.** No verified provincial logo master from this interval was recovered. A bounded follow-up found the 1983 John Mika / Saanich and the Islands campaign lockup. It establishes local provincial campaign use in 1983, while no 1961–1982 specimen or province-wide early master was recovered. National NDP marks have not been substituted for BC use without provincial evidence.
3. **Exact adoption/retirement dates.** The collection establishes selected observations, not complete use intervals. Commons upload dates, Drive metadata and years inside filenames are explicitly separated from adoption dates.
4. **Upright versus italic NDP sun-box sequence.** A dated April 2017 campaign ad has the italic form. A Commons vector associated with July/August 2017 is upright. This evidence does not establish a precise transition sequence or exclude parallel use.
5. **Early Greens, 1983–2009.** The sunflower raster was uploaded to Commons in September 2010, but its design date is unknown. The earliest use, intervening versions and exact change to the later “greens” wording remain open.
6. **Historic leader/campaign lockups.** This pass does not claim every candidate or leader lockup. Emily Lowan's official suite is included because it is directly supplied in the current media kit. The current BC NDP media page retrieved in this pass exposed four generic party-logo downloads; no separate leader-logo download links were present in that page's HTML.

## Sources checked but not promoted to final assets

- [BC NDP official history / sixty](https://www.bcndp.ca/sixty): CCF convention, Tommy Douglas, Dave Barrett, Mike Harcourt and Carole James images. Candidate/event photos without a reliable isolated BC party mark were not included as logos. The Tommy Douglas “NDP Centre” photograph did not establish a provincial rather than national identity.
- [BC NDP History Working Group](https://bcndphistory.ca/): historical accounts and photographs. The site explicitly says it is not sponsored by BC NDP. Its own site branding was not treated as party artwork.
- [Poltext catalogue](https://www.poltext.org/en/catalogue/?type=plateforme): historical BC CCF/NDP platform PDFs were discovered, but source downloads returned HTTP 403 and a web fetch returned 502. No recovered asset is claimed from these files. Relevant candidates include `BC_PL_1953_CCF.pdf`, `BC_PL_1991_NDP_en.pdf`, `BC_PL_2009_NDP_en.pdf`, `BC_PL_2013_NDP_en.pdf` and `BC_PL_2017_NDP_en.pdf`. Some entries may be later transcriptions, so even a successful download would require checking the original design evidence.
- [SFU archival finding aid F-347](https://atom.archives.sfu.ca/downloads/f-347.pdf): the catalogue points to NDP promotional materials from 1972–1974, reference F-347-3-1-2-2, box 347-22. This is a concrete follow-up for early provincial artwork; it is not a downloaded logo.
- National NDP Commons logos were not included merely because the provincial party is affiliated. Provincial use needs a dated provincial specimen or official provincial source.
- White and black logo URLs on the Greens' older NationBuilder attachment host returned 403. Equivalent official originals were successfully obtained from the official media page's linked public Google Drive suite.

## Source fidelity and display notes

Original files are unmodified. Historical crops retain scan texture/background and carry page/date/crop-coordinate provenance. They were not redrawn, traced or generatively restored. The lengthy newspaper issues remain research intermediates and should be excluded from the downloadable final package.

The NDP EPS files render with a grey rectangle in the supplied source; their equivalent official PNGs have transparency and are better display assets. The Greens JPGs are CMYK with embedded ICC profiles: the display previews apply those profiles when converting to sRGB, so simple viewers may otherwise show incorrect bright green. White/reversed marks have dark backgrounds added only in previews. The Emily Lowan EPS is a multi-variant sheet, retained intact.

`manifest.json` contains the 27 primary assets and their `preview_path` values. `preview-manifest.json` separately documents all 27 derivative previews, so preview files do not inflate the source-asset count. `summary.json` supplies the counts. `included-assets-contact-sheet.png` is a visual QA sheet.
