# Remaining source requests

Prepared 30 October 2026. **Drafts only; no requests have been sent.** The source
downloads and processing completed in this pass do not require these drafts to

## PHSA: request the existing spreadsheet exports

Published route: [PHSA freedom of information requests](https://www.phsa.ca/about/accountability/freedom-of-information-requests),
**foi@phsa.ca**. The published email was checked on 30 October 2026.

Subject: Existing electronic employee remuneration schedules, 2015/16–2024/25

Hello,

We are assembling a sourced history of published British Columbia public-sector
remuneration disclosures. We have downloaded the ten employee remuneration and
expense reports for fiscal years 2015/16 through 2024/25 from PHSA's financial
reporting page. All ten PDFs contain scanned pages without extractable text.

Could you provide the existing Excel, CSV, or other electronic tabular files used
to produce those published employee schedules? We are seeking the same publicly
disclosed names, positions where published, remuneration and expenses, with the
original precision, annual reporting periods and scope notes. We are not asking
for additional personal information or for below-threshold individuals to be
identified.

Please include any published corrections, annual row counts and named-employee
subtotals. If the schedules include BC Emergency Health Services, please preserve
that scope and identify whether BCCSS is excluded or separately disclosed. We
want to avoid counting a payment twice through overlapping reporting entities.

If these existing electronic files can be released routinely, that would be
helpful. Otherwise, please advise the appropriate process and any fee estimate
before work incurring fees is undertaken. We would also appreciate links or
copies for any earlier employee schedules before 2015/16 that remain available.

Thank you.

### Why this remains open

The ten downloaded reports total 1,764 scanned pages and 277,510,838 bytes. The
exact source URLs, hashes and text-availability checks are retained in
`downloaded-health-sources-2026-10-30.json`. A spreadsheet reply would reduce the
transcription risk and work involved in OCR of every employee schedule. No PHSA
OCR rows have been silently accepted from this download pass.

Starting route: [City financial reports](https://vancouver.ca/your-government/financial-reports-and-information.aspx)
and the [official council open-data index](https://opendata.vancouver.ca/explore/dataset/council-budget-and-expenses/).
The open-data API was accessible; tested financial-report and council-file
downloads returned HTTP 403 in this environment.

Subject: Published SOFI employee and council remuneration schedules

Hello,

Could you provide accessible copies of the City of Vancouver Statements of
Financial Information for calendar years 2008–2025, or the employee remuneration
and council remuneration schedules from those reports in their existing
electronic format?

We have the City's official employee remuneration open-data export and would like
to reconcile it with the authoritative SOFI reports. The immediate priority is
the mayor and councillor schedules for 2020 and 2023, including their actual
reporting dates and expense definitions. We are keeping council records separate
from the employee disclosure.

Please include any revised schedules or explanatory notes on rounded figures,
missing expense values, and remuneration components. We are requesting published
professional disclosure records, not additional personal information.

Thank you.

Thank you.

[BC Pension publications](https://www.bcpensioncorp.ca/publications) provide the
reviewed executive disclosures. Their [BCI contact](https://www.bci.ca/contact/)

employee remuneration.
