# Recovered health employee histories

This batch contains 65 official employee disclosure originals and 207,001 payment observations. It adds earlier Fraser, Interior, Northern, Providence, Vancouver Coastal and Island histories. The original 29 grouped leads are accounted for in [lead-dispositions.json](lead-dispositions.json); several leads reference the same source, so their displayed counts must not be summed.

The main table, not a financial subtotal, determines which observations are present. Two native readers independently read every employee table. All differences, literal exceptions and every original-vs-declipped field-pixel change received independent original-image review: 88 initial targets and 264 additional targets. The complete 5,229-page census retains the monetary anchors on excluded aggregate, board, supplier and financial pages. All 93 native-reader flags and all 192 visibility diagnostics have explicit dispositions. Blank-tail exclusions include actual original raster checks; two nonblank pages with no visible native text are scanned Providence approval sheets, personally inspected separately.

The selected raw readers and source/readability manifest are explicitly `v2`, using isolated worker processes. Earlier threaded PyMuPDF diagnostics are retained but superseded. Each PDF is processed sequentially within one process; no source PDF bytes were changed. The clipping diagnostic removes only lexical PDF clipping operators in an in-memory derivative after proving the merged original content streams render identically. Actual unchanged source images, never the derivative, determine accepted field values.

The independent reviewer preserved immutable first transcripts. Three pay readings in the first 88 and one name reading in the next 264 were corrected by additive fresh original rereads with expected candidate values withheld. Source values were never adjusted to agree with controls. Exact reading selectors, root context acceptance, and the seven source-clipped-name adoptions are pinned in [source-verification-closure.json](source-verification-closure.json). The seven affected names remain unavailable; known amounts and original page citations remain, with visible fragments and clipping uncertainty in provenance. The literal `M.S.` observation is preserved without an invented full identity. Native Unicode hyphens are retained where an image establishes only the visible separator, not its code point.

Qualifications remain source specific. Island 2022/23 has 8,252 printed observations versus a narrative count of 8,264. Island 2023/24 known expenses are $45,772 below its printed named subtotal. Interior 2003/04 remuneration is $95,713.33 above its printed named subtotal; a numerically matching employee payment does not establish a cause or justify deletion. Other whole-dollar and cent residuals are retained. A separate Fraser 2010/11 contract analysis reports 4,458 employees, versus 4,439 observations in the main named schedule; both originals say March 31, 2011, but equivalence of counting scope is unproved. The main import does not invent nineteen records or infer positions from that separate analysis.

The complete [606-item catalogue audit](full606-catalogue-label-scope-audit.json) exposed a selection omission in the initial employee download list: salary labels and a literal `Remeneration` misspelling. Its finite 19-original extension consists of nine PHSA histories, six Northern histories, three Island histories, and one Fraser contract-detail context source. These are delegated separately and excluded from this batch's 65 originals and 207,001 rows. Nineteen separate board scopes have their own worker. Twenty-eight standalone financial originals remain explicitly unassessed for salary appendices until the separate source audit reports; a financial title never proves salary absence.

Current 2025/26 employee originals were not located in the refreshed issuer indexes, complete official serial pagination and focused current searches. This is not a claim that reports do not exist. Providence 2017/18, 2018/19 and 2019/20 official routes remain retrieval blocked. Previously accepted Providence 2023/24, VCH 2020/21 and PHSA employee schedules are not imported again. Existing compiled partitions remain intact; no complete-copy equivalence was inferred.

No outreach, forms, FOI requests, payments or messages to issuers were sent. The four earlier NIC, McBride, SD8 and SD10 requests remain on hold. The records describe disclosed payments, not unique people, complete workforces or annual base salary rates.

Reproduction uses the pinned original retrievals and exact library ORIGINAL locators, selected v2 native readers, parser, pixel audit, source-only review packets and explicit selectors. Run `python scripts/gap_health_employees_verification.py` and `python -m unittest discover -s scripts -p 'test_gap_health_employees.py' -v`. The collection's `sources.json`, final handoff and owned freeze identify the publication input selection once adopted.
