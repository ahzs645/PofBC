The inventory keeps these categories distinct:

- Unlocated employee publications: exact employer, period, searches performed and the missing scope.
- Missing original schedules: an identified report lacks the relevant pages or the complete preservable original.

- Known nonnumeric remuneration: names and supporting source cells are retained, but no numeric salary is invented. A dash or blank in an expense/title field likewise remains unknown and is not automatically a missing report.
