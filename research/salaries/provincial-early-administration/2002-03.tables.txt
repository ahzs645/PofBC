1
  Capital City Allowance is an allowance that can be claimed by a minister or MLA who lives outside the Capital Regional District (CRD) and is required to stay
overnight in the CRD while performing official duties. The amount of the allowance is $150.00 per day, which is meant to cover meals and accommodation. A
minister who lives within the CRD can claim a per diem of $51.50 per day to cover meals.
2
