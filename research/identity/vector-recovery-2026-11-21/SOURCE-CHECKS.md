## Date interpretation

Dates in URL folders describe the official source URL. They are not independently verified upload or
publication events. Report periods describe the documents. Neither is sufficient evidence for a logo's
adoption or retirement date. The Elections report's 21 September 2026 date is documented in its front matter.
