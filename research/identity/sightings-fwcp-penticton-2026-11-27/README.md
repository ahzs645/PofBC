# Two sightings of held logos (27 November 2026)

Sightings date the file they are found in, never a design's adoption (AGENTS.md, "Logo dates").
Both are recorded as `alsoSeen` on the held asset.

## FWCP Columbia Basin backgrounder, July 2006

- Document: "Backgrounder: An introduction to the Fish & Wildlife Compensation Program – Columbia
  Basin", 8 pages. It was found in the LAC web archive's CDX index, captured on 25 May 2010:
  <https://web.archive.org/web/20100525010649/http://fwcp.ca:80/version2/info/media/backgrounder-2006-july-24.pdf>.
- Date: the file name says 24 July 2006 and the PDF was created on 21 July 2006. The text names the
  2006-07 funding year but prints no date, so the sighting is recorded as "July 2006".
- Cover, middle: the "Conserving & Enhancing Fish & Wildlife · 10 Years · 1995–2005" badge. It matches the held
  `fwcp-columbia-ten-years` (from the Spring 2006 Program Update). Added to it in
  `src/gallery/documentSpecimens.js`; the card now reads "Seen Spring 2006 and July 2006".
- Cover, upper right: a blue fish and a green bear head over "FISH & WILDLIFE COMPENSATION PROGRAM /
  COLUMBIA BASIN". This is not the held `fwcp-columbia-2006`, which is the grey panel with the
  mountain and outlined name from the Spring 2006 update. It is a separate design that is not held,
  so it is recorded as the collection's next action, not as a sighting.
- The footer carries the BC logo with "Ministry of Environment" and the BC Hydro wordmark.
