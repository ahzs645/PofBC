## Tahsis reconstruction check

Each letter was authored directly in cubic paths and adjusted against a registered source/rebuild overlay. No contour-tracing algorithm or substitute typesetting was used. The source and a supersampled vector render were composited onto white and compared at 105 × 63 using a mean RGB threshold of 127. Ink-mask Jaccard overlap progressed from 82.59% to 86.30% to 86.86%. Both S curves are less certain than the straighter letters. These numbers describe resemblance to the tiny publisher specimen; they are not proofs of exact high-resolution geometry. The light version uses the drawn ink; the dark display copy changes only the ink for contrast. The downloadable artwork keeps the source-comparison colour.
