# Fishing BC and PSBSBC: print and current-site checks

## Fishing BC

The [Freshwater Fisheries Society of BC’s 2025–26 report, PDF p. 8](https://www.gofishbc.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2025-26-FFSBC-Annual-Report-digital.pdf#page=8) dates Fishing BC’s formation to 2016. The [May 2017 Destination BC newsletter](https://www.destinationbc.ca/content/uploads/2018/07/2990_Corporate_24May2017_Newsletter.pdf) dates the branded website and video announcement, not adoption of the exact framed mark. The contemporary and retrospective sources use different names for the industry association; they do not establish a formal name change.

## Prevent Shaken Baby Syndrome BC

The [issuer’s header](https://www.dontshake.ca/) serves a 594 × 61 monochrome PNG (SHA-256 `5745db734f294e9702b43f74c8ecfce12792b506c6fd14eac4aaa0051f96e3da`). It is not just a higher-resolution file of the 340 × 46 report version: the relative widths differ, and all six initials share the heavier weight, unlike the report’s lighter final “BC”. The first observed website header is not a dated identity launch. Keep the report artwork and link the issuer’s specimen separately.

The [official resource list](https://www.dontshake.ca/resources) links Tagalog and Arabic editions of the crying infographic. Each PDF page consists of a single 2,550 × 3,300 raster with no vector objects or live text. Both show the small PSBSBC partner signature in the footer; the large Period of PURPLE Crying title is a different mark. Neither file supplies reusable vector geometry. No new artwork is hosted from this check.
