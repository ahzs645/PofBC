# Vancouver artwork in the February 2018 Visual Standards Guide

The [City of Vancouver’s published guide](https://policy.vancouver.ca/CP00103P2.pdf)
is a native-vector source for the same blue-and-green emblem design held in
PofBC as the City’s 137 × 54 masthead PNG. The guide is not a standalone artwork
kit or a grant of permission. The [City’s symbols page](https://vancouver.ca/news-calendar/city-symbols.aspx)
directs users to request authorization and approved artwork. This update adds
source links, not hosted copies. The gallery PNG remains the held artwork;
the catalogue does not count an external source link as a held SVG.

| Official source | Mark or example | Verification and scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Page 5](https://policy.vancouver.ca/CP00103P2.pdf#page=5) | City emblem in colour, solid black and reversed white | 17, 21 and 21 native filled shapes, no bitmap or live text in the mark regions. The one-colour artwork has its own geometry at the blossom crossings. |
| [Page 8](https://policy.vancouver.ca/CP00103P2.pdf#page=8) | Engineering Services and Public Art Program, each in colour, black and reversed white | Six native lockups. The former stacked Engineering Services example lower down is marked incorrect/retired. |
| [Page 15](https://policy.vancouver.ca/CP00103P2.pdf#page=15) | City emblem + Greenest City, in colour, black and reversed white | Three native co-brand lockups; the guide pairs the two marks. Do not infer an approved standalone Greenest City mark. |
| [Page 4](https://policy.vancouver.ca/CP00103P2.pdf#page=4) | Ceremonial coat of arms above the city name | 321 native filled shapes; formal and ceremonial use, distinct from the ordinary City emblem. |
| [Page 7](https://policy.vancouver.ca/CP00103P2.pdf#page=7) | Public Library, Police, Economic Development Commission, Parks Board, Fire and Rescue | Dated alignment specimens, not civic-emblem variants or claims about current partner artwork. |
| [Page 14](https://policy.vancouver.ca/CP00103P2.pdf#page=14) | Talk Green to Us, Greenest City 2020, Access to Nature | Retired examples identified by the guide; not current options. |
