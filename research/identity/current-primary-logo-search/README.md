# Primary-logo recovery | 27 November 2026

The first pass screened the 250 existing primary specimens with raster-only artwork or no qualifying display vector. Eight additional historical periods with no accepted specimen remain a separate queue. Do not count anniversary, campaign, event, and context graphics against current primary coverage. A current institution can still have old marks in this list: the current year, a website observation and the logo's date of adoption are separate facts. The batch screened 113 cited PDF references: 74 opened at a candidate page, 39 did not open within the size, access or format limits used. A page-level probe is a triage result, not evidence of paths in the logo.

## Continue the search

Work from the live primary gap IDs. Prefer official branding/press kits, board packages, print templates, financial and annual reports and their cover/footer treatments. Inspect the content inside each mark's bounds, not the container extension. Use a PDF crop or object/shape boundaries and preserve any logo variant whose geometry, typesetting or colours differ. Words-only candidates worth checking next include Tahsis, Gold River and the framed Fishing BC wordmark; determine current use and seek official outlines first. Telkwa has a typographic signature plus a simple flourish; document the former separately. Do not redraft civic arms, Indigenous-designed emblems or pictorial seals as if they were text-only marks.

The construction script requires the specific issuer Q1 2022 PDF (SHA-256 `64094e45ea227a3e8e7c5c8a590b6f44a62803d95e525b0aa2fa88304ddbd2e6`) and PyMuPDF. Pass the PDF and output directory to `build-wordmarks.py`. Rebuild site previews with `scripts/build_collected_display.py` and compare both light and dark images. Never replace a published source with a derived display copy.
