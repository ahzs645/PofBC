# British Columbia Development Corporation — earlier identities (2026-11-14)

Two supplied vector packages, added to the gallery entry `collected-bc-development-corporation`
beside the 1985 annual-report crops already held.

| Gallery asset | Design | Built after | Dated evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bcdc-prism-1975` | Four outlined prisms beside the four-line serif name | Aldergrove Star, 17 Sep 1975, p. 6 (UBC Open Collections) and two Jan 1976 advertisements | In use by 17 Sep 1975; on the Chetwynd G.G. Moore Industrial Park sign in *Action for Industry* (1979), PDF p. 22 |
| `bcdc-four-square-serif-1980` | “The Development Corporation”, serif, over four thin squares | CIPO 0460346 | Filed 22 Oct 1980; reg. 25 Jun 1982 (TMA270427); use claimed since 31 Mar 1980; cancelled 3 Nov 1989 |
| `bcdc-four-square-symbol-1980` | The four squares alone | CIPO 0494945 | Filed 16 Nov 1982; reg. 22 Jul 1983 (TMA281645); use claimed since 31 Mar 1980; cancelled 3 Nov 1989 |
| `bcdc-four-square-sans-1981` | Bold sans name over four rounded squares | CIPO 0901351 (official mark) | Filed 28 Jan 1981; advertised 17 Jun 1981; withdrawn 13 Dec 1989 |
| `bcdc-four-square-horizontal-1981` | Rounded squares beside the name | CIPO 0901352 (official mark) | Filed 16 Mar 1981; advertised 2 Dec 1981; withdrawn 13 Dec 1989 |

The 1985 crops show the Province’s BC-and-flag mark, not a BCDC emblem; their gallery labels now say so.

All five are reconstructions, not recovered masters: constructed geometry and hand-authored letter
outlines, no autotrace. The prism lockup’s name is outlined Nimbus Roman fitted to the scans; the
four-square lettering is drawn glyph by glyph. No typeface, designer or official colour is known.
Each package’s own README is in `source/prism/` and `source/four-square/`; comparison sheets are in
`review/`. `source/build_register.py` writes `source-register.json` from the files in
`public/identity-collected/bc-development-corporation/` (it checks each SVG for images, text and
external references). See `source-register.json` for open questions and archival leads.
