# BC Timber Sales in print, 2004

The Legislative Library catalogues *BC Timber Sales* as a 2004 publication of BC Timber Sales and the Ministry of Forests in Victoria. Its catalogue record number is 367449. PDF page numbers below include the library cover sheet. [Permanent publication record](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/308267)

## Artwork

The booklet’s front and back covers, PDF pages 2 and 15, show the oval Forest Service tree emblem beside green BCTS initials and a dark serif subtitle. A separate provincial coat-of-arms wordmark appears elsewhere on each cover. The Invermere data package dated 8 March 2004 shows a different arrangement, with the oval beside a three-line name. The two publications establish use during the same year. Neither identifies a designer or adoption date. [Brochure](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/308267) · [Invermere data package, cover](https://data.nrs.gov.bc.ca/TSR_Historical/TSA/Invermere_09/TSR_2005/Data_Package/InvermereTSR3_Data_Package_v3.0.pdf)

The back-cover lockup survives as original vector artwork. Its SVG export contains 75 painted paths, including the outlined lettering. The embedded colours include green `#008450`, charcoal `#231F20`, sky blue `#6DC9EF`, blue `#0091C4`, brown `#4B3216` and white. These are file colours. A local transparent render at 1064 × 448 was composited on white and compared with the same PDF rectangle (172, 524 to 305, 580 points, measured from the top left). At thresholds of 128, 200 and 245 on the darkest RGB channel, silhouette intersection over union was 99.59%, 99.42% and 99.58% respectively. Antialiasing and the threshold affect the comparison.

The collection links to the library’s copy. Its item metadata does not give a redistribution licence, and the library’s collection terms reserve rights to the copyright holders.
