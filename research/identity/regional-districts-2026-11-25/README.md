# Regional district artwork acquisition

25 November 2026

The [Regional districts gallery](http://127.0.0.1:5179/dev/data?view=gallery#category-regional-districts) covers all 27 current regional districts and the former Comox-Strathcona Regional District. The [register](http://127.0.0.1:5179/dev/data?view=gallery&coverage=1&identity-family=regional-districts) links each current district to its municipal members. The provincial municipality roster supplies those links. Central Coast has zero incorporated municipal members. The Sunshine Coast record also links the shíshálh Nation Government District and identifies its board representation. [Provincial membership layer](https://delivery.maps.gov.bc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/mpcm/bcgwpub/MapServer/3) · [Sunshine Coast board](https://www.scrd.ca/news/new-and-returning-sunshine-coast-regional-district-board-take-oath-of-office/)

## Sources and artwork

- [District roster](districts.json) records incorporation, former names, and the sources for known reorganizations. The [coverage audit](../regional-district-audit-2026-11-25/README.md) records the baseline and the municipality-to-district comparison.
- [Artwork register](artwork.json) records the publisher, file URL, page or file locator, format, date evidence and SHA-256 for each added file. Each PDF extraction records its page rectangle, document checksum and method. Website artwork retains the source file bytes. Raster publication crops retain the source artwork and its field where relevant. The historical display includes sightings and later reproductions, with captions that identify which date the source supports.
- The Kootenay Boundary logo kit publishes dedicated light, dark and monochrome PDF masters. The gallery converts the masters directly to browser SVGs with their geometry and gradients and offers the original PDFs alongside them. The current capital, Columbia Shuswap, Comox Valley, Nanaimo and Okanagan-Similkameen marks use matched vectors while retaining their website images as selectable versions. [Kootenay Boundary logo kit](https://rdkb.com/Regional-Government/Organization/Branding)
- Theme display files are generated from the collected assets with [the gallery’s display builder](../../../scripts/build_collected_display.py). Where the publisher provides a matching reverse, the gallery can show it on a dark surface. Other dark previews use the existing display pipeline or a plate for raster artwork. Source downloads are independent of these preview treatments.

## Historical coverage

Earlier artwork includes Metro Vancouver’s GVRD signatures, the former Skeena-Queen Charlotte layout, the Powell River Regional District letterhead, and the redesigned 2006 Comox-Strathcona signature. Fraser-Fort George and Bulkley-Nechako retain their travel-guide specimens alongside current artwork. Additional dated layouts and emblems appear where the publisher supplied evidence. [Metro Vancouver brand book, p. 22](https://metrovancouver.org/media-room/Documents/metro-vancouver-brand-book.pdf#page=22) · [Skeena-Queen Charlotte board agenda, p. 1](https://www.ncrdbc.com/sites/default/files/uploads/meetings/agendas/02-20-2015_ag_pkg.pdf#page=1) · [Powell River Regional District 2017 report, p. 1](https://www.qathet.ca/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/2016_CARIP_Survey.pdf#page=1) · [Comox-Strathcona annual report](https://www.comoxvalleyrd.ca/sites/default/files/2022-08/2006_annual_report.pdf#page=1)

Separate archive work remains for the Ocean Falls-era identity, pre-2006 Comox-Strathcona, the three Fraser Valley predecessors, and the Peace River-Liard and Fort Nelson-Liard periods. Verified current vectors remain open for Bulkley-Nechako, Central Kootenay, North Okanagan, Squamish-Lillooet, Strathcona and Thompson-Nicola. Their covers use attributable publisher artwork.
