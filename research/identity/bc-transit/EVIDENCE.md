# BC Transit identity research

Research date: 27 September 2026. Repository target: ahzs645/PofBC.

## Findings

BC Transit’s own history records the UTA’s formation in 1979, the transfer of Victoria and Vancouver operations in 1980, the BC Transit name in 1982, and the transfer of Metro Vancouver operations to TransLink in 1999. These are organizational events, not logo-launch evidence. [S1]

The supplied historical JPEG has upright white lettering and a waving flag on red. Its folder was labelled “pre 2000”; neither first use nor retirement has been independently established. Keep this as an undated archival image. It is not silently promoted to a vector or assigned the corporation’s founding date. [U1]

The official 2007 and 2008 service-plan covers show another application: an angled provincial flag above the name. This is visually different from the supplied horizontal reference. It is recorded as an observed variant, not a proven intervening redesign. Original vector artwork remains to be recovered. [S2, S3]

The 2009 service-plan cover has the loop, italic lettering and tagline “Linking Communities, Businesses & Lifestyles”. The report says it was tabled on 17 February 2009. Thus the identity is documented by that date, but its launch could be earlier. Older artwork can remain on publications during a rollout; the 2008/2009 covers do not prove a strict adoption window. [S4]

BC Transit dates its previous notable bus-exterior change to 2007. Its September 2024 announcement introduced another livery, with rollout on new buses that fall. The accompanying explanation explicitly rules out a logo change. Do not make 2007 or 2024 new logo eras on that basis alone. [S5, S6]

## Artwork audit

`bc-transit-loop-navy.svg`: supplied paths, cropped. No live text, bitmap, or font program. Navy #004370 is a source-file value, not a researched Pantone specification.

`bc-transit-loop-colour-source-hybrid.svg`: source lettering outlined at the source’s own positions. Its original 54×26-pixel clipped shading image remains. This is deliberately labelled HYBRID, not a pure vector master.

`bc-transit-loop-colour-flat.svg`: all-vector derivative of that file. The shaded region becomes flat #004b8d along the exact source clipping boundary, green remains #4cb748, and white highlights become cut-outs. This is not described as official artwork or a historical redesign. No font files or embedded font programs are included.

`bc-transit-flag-horizontal-reference.jpg`: supplied raster unchanged. The flag-above-name artwork is not held. Unknown typography is not filled in with an unlabelled font substitute.

Both modern supplied applications belong to one loop family. Neither file has independently established publication dates. The family’s 2009 observation does not date every current digital variation. [U1, S4]
