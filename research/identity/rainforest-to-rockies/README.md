# Rainforest to Rockies

Destination BC names Super, Natural British Columbia as the parent brand on page 5 of the [March 2024 industry guidelines](https://www.destinationbc.ca/content/uploads/2024/03/Rainforest-to-Rockies-Industry-Brand-Guidelines.pdf). The supplied 38-page guide and the publisher’s PDF have the same SHA-256, `38fb70c62864e6a7dd4650a3b919a088dc82bd9c5aaa081a25dbdad689fa6c60`. March 5, 2024 is the PDF creation date.

## Artwork

Each PNG in `public/initiatives/rainforest-to-rockies/` retains the original image dimensions. PyMuPDF extracted the RGB image and its grayscale soft mask. Pillow combined the decoded channels into an RGBA PNG without resampling.

The [March 2024 companion toolkit](https://www.destinationbc.ca/content/uploads/2024/03/Rainforest-to-Rockies-Industry-Brand-Toolkit.pdf) has SHA-256 `18b075478bd4779e6508865f57eae74dd636f0efc4c1fa45a421f29a0709e83b`. Side-by-side comparison with the supplied guideline specimens on their intended grounds verified the lettering, diamond and branches, relative placement, partner caption, and colourways. After aligning the opaque bounds, the thresholded alpha masks had intersection-over-union scores of 0.995 for the primary, 0.980 for the green partner mark, and 0.983 for the white partner mark at 800 × 300 pixels. The larger toolkit specimens serve as the defaults and the guide specimens remain selectable.

## Vector search

On November 25, 2026, searches covered local artwork holdings, Destination BC’s public media library (queries `Rainforest`, `R2R`, and `Iconics`), 2023–24 regional partner presentations, the designer’s portfolio, the archived consumer site, the Canadian Trademarks Database, and the current brand kit. The following evidence distinguishes the 2024 artwork from the later design.

The March 2024 toolkit’s **Request the Third-Party Logo** button on page 5 directs partners to Destination BC’s brand team at `brand@destinationbc.ca`. This is the most specific lead for the original green and white partner files. The [current BC Content Hub collection](https://www.bccontenthub.com/web/aa6743ca6ed25a39/rainforest-to-rockies-collection---third-party/) is another possible lead. A matching vector master for any of the three March 2024 specimens remains unverified.
