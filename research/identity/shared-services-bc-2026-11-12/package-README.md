# Solutions BC and Shared Services BC — original logo specimens

Research and extraction date: November 12, 2026.

This package preserves original artwork found in government documents. Specimen dates establish dated appearances, not verified design/adoption dates. Designer attribution has not been established. The supplied files contain original embedded images, crops of source scans, or a cropped original PDF; no logo has been redrawn or generated.

## 1. Solutions BC — 2003 colour logo

- File: Solutions_BC_2003_original.jpeg
- Original embedded JPEG: 115 × 149 pixels, extracted without re-encoding.
- Teal rectangular panel with four grey/silver stylized figures and the wording Solutions BC / SHARED SERVICES.
- Primary source: Revenue Management Project Joint Solution Procurement, cover letter dated June 27, 2003. Logo on PDF page 2.
- The same embedded JPEG occurs in the Payroll Operations and Information Management JSRFP, dated October 9, 2003, page 2. Both source PDFs are included.
- Solutions_BC_2003_preview.png is an enlarged rendered detail. It does not contain additional source detail.
- Recommended catalog date: 2003; documented in a government procurement document dated June 27, 2003. April 1, 2003 adoption has not been established.

## 2. Solutions BC — August 17, 2006 letterhead

- File: Solutions_BC_2006_letterhead.png
- Provincial sunrise/mountain emblem, divider, SolutionsBC and Shared Services.
- Source: government letter, RFP SATP-191 Amendment 4, August 17, 2006; preserved on BC Nature's website, PDF page 1.
- A crop of a black-and-white scan. Paper texture, scan artifacts and original geometry are preserved.

## 3. Shared Services BC — 2006/07 colour signature

- File: Shared_Services_BC_2006_2007_original.png
- 386 × 109 pixels. Extracted from original PDF colour image xref 75 with its original transparency mask, xref 74.
- Provincial sunrise/mountain emblem with BRITISH COLUMBIA / The Best Place on Earth, divider, and Shared / ServicesBC in blue and gold.
- Source: Payroll Operations and Information Management and Payroll Services, Alliance Management Office Report — November 2006, PDF page 1.
- The PDF document title says for posting July 2007, and its creation metadata is July 20, 2007. Label this specimen 2006/07 rather than assigning an unproven first-use date in 2005 or 2006.

## 4. Shared Services BC — August 9, 2010 letterhead

- File: Shared_Services_BC_2010_letterhead.png
- Black-and-white reproduction of the provincial signature with The Best Place on Earth.
- Source: letter dated August 9, 2010, reproduced in Appendix C of Vancouver Heritage Register — Annual Update, PDF page 12.
- The containing report is dated April 15, 2011, for the May 3, 2011 council meeting. The filename Shared_Services_BC_2011_source.pdf refers to that containing report; the letter itself is from 2010.

## 5. Shared Services BC — 2015 brochure colour signature

- Files: Shared_Services_BC_2015_colour.png and Shared_Services_BC_2015_original_artwork.pdf
- White Shared Services lettering with gold BC on a dark blue background, alongside the provincial emblem and a gold divider. No provincial tagline in this specimen.
- Source: official Shared Services BC Real Property brochure, PDF page 16. Related branding also appears on the front cover.
- PDF creation metadata: January 13, 2015. The brochure also refers to a planned 2015 rollout. This identifies the document period, not the mark's first use.
- The cropped PDF retains the source's original vector paths and shading. The PNG is a raster preview of that artwork.

## Provenance and source links

The accompanying provenance.json records the source URLs, source pages (1-based), crop coordinates where applicable, extraction methods, and SHA-256 hashes for the main source documents and logo assets. Source PDFs are included unchanged under sources/.

## Historical interpretation

The specimens document multiple uses and variants. They do not, by themselves, prove a single official sequence of logo replacement. In particular, a 2006 Solutions BC letterhead and a Shared Services BC report for 2006 should not be used to infer an exact renaming date. Organizational names, service brands, and older letterhead can overlap.
