# Shared Services BC: why the collection has no graph node

**Superseded on 13 November 2026.** The sources listed under “What would settle it” were read, and the body
now exists: `shared-services-bc` in `research/history/bodies-shared-services-2026-11-13.json` (launched as
Solutions BC on 1 April 2003; Shared Services BC by February 2006; name last seen in 2015, end untraced), with
its events in `research/atlas/events-shared-services-2026-11-13.json`. The collection is bound to it. The
account below is kept as it was written.

Checked 13 November 2026.

`src/gallery/collections.js` places the collection `shared-services-bc` with `graphNode: null`. This is deliberate. The government graph has no node for this body, and this package cannot support adding one.

## What the graph has

- **No node** for Solutions BC, Shared Services BC, Common IT Services, the Information Technology Services Division or the Office of the Chief Information Officer, in `src/government/historyData.js`, `currentData.js`, `atlasData.js` or `eventsData.js`.
- `bc-systems-corporation` (historical body, 1977–2000) is a predecessor of the IT function, not the same body. Its operations passed to the Information Technology Services Division on 31 March 1996. It already has its own collection, so binding it here would also fold that collection away.
- `atlas:bcsc-it-succession-2003` in `eventsData.js` (subject `bc-systems-corporation`) traces the function: ITSD, then Common IT Services, then part of Solutions BC (1 April 2003), then Shared Services BC, then OCIO reporting from December 2011. Its basis is `supplied_research_summary`. It cites the Ministry of Management Services 2002/03 annual report, which was "not re-read in this pass". The collection's “formed on 1 April 2003” rests on this record.
- `sub-connected-services-bc` (established 1 October 2025) combines OCIO, ministry IT teams and Service BC. It is a later body, not the same one.
- Ministry nodes (`ministry-of-management-services-2001`, the Labour and Citizens' Services and Citizens' Services ministries) would be the body's parents. None of them is the body.
- `bc-buildings-corporation` notes that the 2006 Labour and Citizens' Services service plan says BCBC's former services moved to Shared Services BC. That is evidence of the name in use, nothing more.

## Why no node was added

`historyData.js` is generated by `scripts/build-government-history.mjs` from research inputs and must not be edited by hand. A sub-agency record there needs a parent ministry, an established or first-observed claim with a source in `HISTORY_SOURCES`, and its names over time. This package's `source-register.json` cannot supply those. It holds logo specimens: two procurement documents (2003), a letter (2006), a report (2006/07), a letter (2010) and a brochure (2015). They show the names in use. They do not show formation, parentage, renaming or end.

| Needed for a node | State |
| --- | --- |
| Formation as Solutions BC, 1 April 2003 | Only in a supplied research summary that was not re-read |
| Parent ministry, and when it changed | Not sourced here |
| When Solutions BC became Shared Services BC | Not established. Both names appear in 2006 documents, which is evidence of name use, not a transition date |
| When the Shared Services BC name ended, and what took over its parts | Not established |

## What would settle it

1. The Ministry of Management Services 2002/03 annual report, re-read at <https://www.bcbudget.gov.bc.ca/annual_reports/2002_2003/mser/mser_context.htm>. It would give Solutions BC's formation and its parent ministry.
2. A dated source for the renaming. An order in council, ministry announcement or service plan would do. The 2005/06 Labour and Citizens' Services annual report (<https://www.bcbudget.gov.bc.ca/annual_reports/2005_2006/lcs/Service_Delivery_CS.htm>) gives at least a first observation of the Shared Services BC name and its ministry.
3. A source for the end of the name and where its divisions went. One candidate is FOI package CTZ-2013-00102 (<https://docs.openinfo.gov.bc.ca/d64731113a_response_package_ctz-2013-00102.pdf>) for OCIO reporting from December 2011.

Once those are read, add the body through the history research inputs and the generator, not by hand. Then bind its id in `PLACED['shared-services-bc'].graphNode`.
