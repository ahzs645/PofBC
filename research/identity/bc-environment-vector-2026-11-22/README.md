# BC Environment, flag years, 22 November 2026

A supplied vector reconstruction of the Ministry of Environment's flag-years mark: "BC" beside the
flag symbol, "Environment" below. Read by `scripts/build-collected-marks.mjs` (PROGRAM_PACKAGES) from
`source-register.json`; shown in the gallery collection `bc-environment`, tied to the government
map's Ministry of Environment, 1988–1991 (`ministry-of-environment-1988`).

- Source: a scan of an undated printed cover, supplied to the project (`reference/supplied-cover-scan.png`,
  594 × 446 px), cream lettering on a diagonal green band over a photograph. Its title and date are
  not in the picture, so the placement under the 1988–1991 ministry rests on the name alone.
- Method: the flag is the gallery's own twelve-path flag (`src/assets/flagMark.js`) placed as one
  unit; the 13 letters are outlines of the site's Helvetica Bold, fitted letter by letter. Overlap
  with the scan's cream ink is 82.6 percent (lettering 85, flag 69; `BC_Environment_Validation.json`).
- Files in `public/identity-collected/bc-environment/`: the upright mark in one dark ink, in the
  cover's cream, the diagonal band as printed (its cut-outs transparent), and a two-page vector PDF.
- Colours (#F7F3D6 cream, #5EBD7E green) are sampled from a textured scan, not specifications.

The supplier's own notes are in `package-README.md`; `BC_Environment_Geometry.json` holds the path
data, and `review/` the comparison with the scan.
