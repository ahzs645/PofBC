# Font reconstruction notes — revision 2

## Caption: COMMUNITY HEALTH COUNCIL

Albertus was the strongest visual basis among the tested candidates. Native outline geometry from a labelled Albertus specimen was used, then controlled shared edits corrected C terminals, O counter, U foot, N upright and L foot. Weight, width and spacing were adjusted to the supplied screenshot. These are designed outline edits, not extracted raster contours. The reference is Epson's Adobe PostScript 3 manual, page 195: https://download.epson-europe.com/pub/download/271/epson27082eu.pdf

The source proves that the compared specimen is Albertus. The proposed relation to the council's logo is a visual inference. It does not authenticate the council's original typeface, original digital cut or weight. The delivered outlines are customized and will not be reproduced exactly by typing the words in unmodified Albertus.

## Main name: GREATER / TRAIL

The main name uses reusable custom serif capitals after testing Garamond variants, Goudy, Crimson, Hoefler Text, New York, Palatino and other candidates. No tested family matched the combination of the long R tail, counters and proportions closely enough. The main name remains labelled custom reconstructed lettering, historical font unconfirmed.

## What the package contains

Only the outlines needed for this logo wording are included. The SVG/PDF exports are font-independent. Full commercial font files, complete extracted alphabet masters and the source manuals are not included.
