# Thompson Health Region: historical colour specimen found

Research date: 22 November 2026.

## Result

The cross-and-path symbol in the PofBC Thompson Health Region entry has been found in colour in a government-hosted historical publication. The arms and lower upright are teal / blue-green; the upper upright is ochre / golden orange; the winding path and pointed internal details are white.

This is an extracted historical specimen. The colour artwork was not generated, recoloured, traced or redrawn. It is the symbol alone: the serif organization name and italic “A Path to Better Health” tagline do not accompany it in this source.

An original vector has not been found. The symbol in the recovered PDF is a small **59 × 56-pixel bitmap**, stored as two adjacent horizontal image strips. The supplied PNG preserves those pixels and the source sRGB colour profile.

## Exact primary source

**Publication:** *Proceedings of the Risk Assessment Workshop*, compiled by Chris Garrett, Environment Canada, Pacific and Yukon Region, Vancouver. Catalogue publication year: [2003]. Catalogue number: **En40-240-1/03-00E-PDF**.

- [Official Government of Canada catalogue record](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.882150/publication.html)
- [Original 154-page PDF](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2019/eccc/en40-240-1/En40-240-1-03-00-eng.pdf)
- **Logo location: PDF page 71, printed page 63, slides 38 and 39.**
- Slide headings: “ORDER TO CITY” and “ORDER TO CITY (CON’T).”
- Presentation: Barry Boettger, *Risk Assessment in Drinking Water Quality Programs: The Provincial perspective*, Health Protection, Ministry of Health Planning. Its title slide begins on PDF page 52, printed page 44.

If the government website first displays an archived-information notice, choose **Continue to publication**. The notice can otherwise be mistaken for the PDF by automated downloaders.

The source-page extract included here contains original PDF pages **1, 3, 52 and 71**, in that order. The logo is on **page 4 of the extract**. These pages were copied without re-rendering; all four were checked to render identically to the original pages.

## Why the identification is strong

The colour specimen and the 9 September 1999 letterhead share the same distinctive cross outline, winding white path opening at the base, and pointed white shapes within the upper upright. The surrounding slide text explicitly names Thompson Health Region, Kamloops, its medical health officer Dr. James Lu, and public health inspector Ken Christian.

This ties the symbol to the B.C. organization. It is not Alberta’s separate David Thompson Health Region.

For comparison, the earlier monochrome specimen is on PDF page 118 of the [Merritt council agenda dated 12 October 1999](https://old.merritt.ca/wp-content/uploads/archives/meetings/1999/1999-10-12%20RC%20agenda.pdf), in a letter dated 9 September 1999.

## Limits of the evidence

- **The complete colour lockup is unresolved.** This specimen does not establish the colours of the organization name or tagline.
- **The original vector is unresolved.** The visible symbol is embedded raster artwork, even though the containing file is a PDF. No larger matching embedded copy was found in a targeted image search within the report. A short search for the original Boettger presentation did not locate a separate PPT or higher-resolution slide file.
- **The date of introduction is unresolved.** This is a 2003 publication reproducing earlier regional material. It does not establish when the colour treatment began or prove that Thompson Health Region continued operating under this identity in 2003.
- **The reproduced order is not a signed original.** Its day/month and signature fields are blank, with the year 2000 supplied. It is safest to describe it as a reproduced order/template bearing that year.
- **The proceedings give inconsistent event days.** The cover/catalogue title says 16 November 2003; the abstract and executive summary say 18 November 2003. Use “2003 proceedings” or “November 2003 presentation” without resolving that discrepancy.

## Remaining routes to original production artwork

The most specific newly verified physical holding is **Royal Inland Hospital Alumnae Association fonds, Scrapbook #7 (1992–2008), accession 2010.002/007, Box 11**, at Kamloops Museum and Archives. Its finding aid lists correspondence, newsletters, photographs and other hospital material. The scrapbook pages have not been inspected, and the finding aid does not identify this logo.

- [Finding aid, PDF page 18](https://www.kamloopsmuseum.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/rihalumnae_OCR.pdf)
- [Official archivist contact](https://www.kamloopsmuseum.ca/about/contact-us/): Mitchell Fridman, **mfridman@kamloops.ca**.
- [Archival reproduction service](https://www.kamloopsmuseum.ca/the-archives/services-and-fees/)

A focused enquiry could ask whether the scrapbook contains Thompson Health Region printed material from approximately 1998–2001, and whether any original electronic artwork or design records survive. No enquiry has been sent.

Two *Canadian Nurse* recruitment advertisements remain prior text-index leads: **October 2000, volume 96 no. 9, printed page 44**, and **February 2001, volume 97 no. 2, printed page 42**. The publisher records were recovered, but their page images remain inaccessible in this search. They have not been used to infer colours.

- [October 2000 publisher record](https://www.canadian-nurse.com/viewdocument/october-2000?CommunityKey=d320003f-c6d8-4209-a9e7-4fbff8be6289&tab=librarydocuments)
- [February 2001 publisher record](https://www.canadian-nurse.com/viewdocument/february-2001?CommunityKey=d320003f-c6d8-4209-a9e7-4fbff8be6289&tab=librarydocuments)

## Suggested catalogue treatment

Record this as a **historical colour raster specimen of the symbol**, with the source location above. Retain the monochrome 1999 letterhead as evidence for the complete name/symbol/tagline arrangement. Keep official colour specifications and original-vector availability unresolved.

No PofBC repository files were changed during this research.
