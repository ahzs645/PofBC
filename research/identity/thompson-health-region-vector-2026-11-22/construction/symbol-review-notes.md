# Independent review: symbol v1

## Verdict

PASS. The reconstructed symbol is defensible within the surviving raster evidence. No significant geometry issue requires revision before packaging.

Reviewed symbol-v1-colour.png, symbol-v1-overlay.png, the underlying paths in symbol-geometry.json, and the combined-v1.png lockup. Compared these with the independently extracted 1999, 2000 and 2001 monochrome source components and the historical colour image.

## Crown and upper cutouts

The candidate has the correct five white cutouts, with the central point tallest and the side points leaning outward. The gold fingers and connecting white separator remain coherent at normal viewing size. The teal crown has the expected central raised point and four shallow downward white valleys. Smoothing the raster steps is appropriate; there is no need to preserve the scan defects or slight apparent asymmetry.

## River

The path reads as a widening ribbon, with the correct small upper-right turn, middle-left turn and broad lower-right bend. Joins are visually smooth. The nominal right-bank maximum at y=744 is not a material error: its long, almost vertical plateau remains at approximately x=633 at y=780 and x=632 at y=800, matching the source silhouette. Moving that control-point landmark solely to y=780 would risk degrading an already faithful contour.

The right endpoint at x=493 is slightly left of the approximately x=510–518 endpoint suggested by the 1999/2000 material. The 2001 scan reaches approximately x=491 at its last row. Scan skew, the final baseline pixels and reproduction differences account for roughly one to two effective source pixels of uncertainty here. This is not a blocker. A bounded numerical experiment with the final segment and endpoint x=505–510 changed fit by less than one tenth of an effective source pixel; that does not justify another iteration by itself.

## Combined draft

The combined lockup is clean, the symbol remains legible beside the wordmark, and there is no visible clipping or loss of the white separator/path. No changes to root files were made during this review.
