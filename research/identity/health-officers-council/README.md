The Council dates its founding to September 1947 and incorporation under the Societies Act to June 1998. Its 2017 submission describes a professional society whose members include medical health officers from the provincial health authorities. Its constitution provides a forum for the Provincial Health Officer, ministries and health bodies to discuss their plans. [Council history and purpose](https://www.healthofficerscouncil.net/about/overview-of-hoc/)

The Society’s badge catalogue establishes a separate Public Health Service identity. Green directed the provincial department’s Public Health Nursing Division. In 1961 the Department of Health Services and Hospital Insurance had a Health Branch under the Deputy Minister of Health. Dorothy Slaughter’s fonds also lists Public Health Service crests and a badge. The collection descriptions give no common designer or lineage for the two emblems. [Green’s biography](https://bcnursinghistory.ca/project/green-monica-1917-2004/) · [Department Act, RSBC 1960, c. 102, ss. 5–8](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/hstats/hstats/681611917) · [Slaughter fonds](https://bcnursinghistory.ca/project/dorothy-slaughter/)

The Department of Health’s provincial annual report for 1975, printed in 1976, names the Division of Venereal Disease Control under Special Health Services. Its title page carries the provincial arms. A printing of the Public Health Service emblem in a separate 1975 division report remains unverified.
