# Tourism and destination management

Reviewed November 25, 2026. The gallery's tourism category and the related-bodies directory cover the six regional operations, the historical Vancouver, Coast and Mountains society, Vancouver Island's former and current identities, provincial partnerships, Indigenous Tourism BC, and four community destination organizations. GreenStep Solutions appears in the VCM toolkit credits as a project partner.

## Vancouver, Coast & Mountains

- **August 12, 1996.** A fax signed by executive director Doug Banner documents the association's presence on the Lower Mainland Protected Areas Public Advisory Committee. The fax masthead identifies the sender. A distinct society logo remains unverified. [BC Nature archive, PDF p. 20](https://bcnature.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/44-PAS-Framework-for-Effective-Discussion-and-Negotiation-044.pdf#page=20)
- **March 24, 2003.** The Province identifies the association as a nonprofit society with its own board. Its four destination areas were Greater Vancouver, Sea-to-Sky Country, Mighty Fraser Country, and the Sunshine Coast. [Provincial backgrounder](https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/archive/2001-2005/2003CSE0028-000289-Attachment1.htm)
- **2012–2015.** The agreement identifies the incorporated society by certificate No. 9836. Modification Agreement No. 3 records the September 30, 2014 term and transition funding. Modification Agreement No. 4 extends the contractual term through July 31, 2015, with activities and payments due by March 31, 2015. The exact handover and society dissolution dates remain open. [Destination BC contract, PDF pp. 1–3 and 33–35](https://www.destinationbc.ca/content/uploads/2018/06/VCM-FY1415-Contracts_Combined.pdf#page=33)
- **By 2020.** Destination BC's network announcement documents its own VCM team delivering the region's tourism resiliency program. Its regional directory describes direct management. [Network announcement](https://www.destinationbc.ca/news/bc-tourism-resiliency-network/) · [Regional directory](https://www.destinationbc.ca/who-we-are/regional-community-industry-partner/vancouver-coast-mountain/)
- **2023 and 2024.** The coloured and white stacked signatures are present as sets of 44 native filled paths. The public Toolkit PDF has 70 pages. The cover is PDF page 1 and the attribution to Destination BC's VCM region and GreenStep Solutions is on the final page. The April 2024 newsletter dates the toolkit's public release. [Playbook, cover](https://www.destinationbc.ca/content/uploads/2023/07/The-Vancouver-Coast-Mountains-Sustainability-Playbook_Final_July-25-V5.pdf#page=1) · [Toolkit, cover](https://www.destinationbc.ca/content/uploads/2024/02/VCM_Sustainability_Toolkit_Final_Version_V2.pdf#page=1) · [Release newsletter](https://www.destinationbc.ca/newsletters/2024/04/vcm-april-18-2024.html)

## Artwork decisions

The [February 2024 micro-grant guidelines cover](https://www.destinationbc.ca/content/uploads/2024/02/BC-Tourism-Climate-Resiliency-Initiative-Grant-Program-Guidelines-February-2024-FINAL.pdf#page=1) consists of nine separately embedded logo images and live title text. The Cariboo, Kootenay, Northern BC, TOTA, VCM, period 4VI, and Indigenous Tourism BC images were extracted directly. The B.C. provincial signature and Destination BC corporate identity are catalogued in their existing collections. The cover attributes the initiative through the title and partner artwork. A standalone Climate Resiliency Initiative mark remains unverified.

The catalogue records source, locator, observed date, extraction method where applicable, and checksum for each held file. The graphics extracted from the VCM covers, Kootenay governance manual, BCRTS case study, Indigenous Tourism BC report, TOTA strategy, Tourism Whistler fact sheet, and Tourism Chilliwack strategy use their original paths. The gallery's generated theme copies preserve the light source palette where the publisher supplies it. The source SVG remains the default download when it matches the design. Reverse artwork remains selectable.

Different layouts are independently catalogued, including the stacked and horizontal Kootenay, Northern BC and TOTA signatures. VCM's former association, the historical 4VI trading identity, the 4VI Group, 4TVI, 4EVER and 4GOOD retain separate ownership and histories. The dated 4VI from the 2024 cover is distinct from the subsequent 4VI Group and 4TVI lockups.

The Cariboo Chilcotin Coast Tourism Association and the Cariboo Chilcotin Coast Tourism Marketing Association report separate operations and accounts. Their joint cover supplies one documented mountain-and-river signature. [2022–23 Annual Service Report](https://industry.landwithoutlimits.com/resources/uploads/2023/11/CCCT-CCCTMA-2023-Annual-Report-Low-Res_email_ver.pdf)
