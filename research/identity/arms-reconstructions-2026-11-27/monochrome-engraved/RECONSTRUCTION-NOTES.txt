Reconstruction decisions
  Curves were fitted to the supplied drawing and refined conservatively.
  The antler branches, animal faces, horn ridges, hooves, crowns, hatching,
  sun rays, waves, ribbon folds and historical letter shapes follow the source.
  The exterior is transparent. White areas inside the artwork are opaque fills.
  Gray cutout remnants in the lion's open tail/leg spaces and the distant
  horn opening were removed. Those openings are now transparent.
  The asymmetric scroll and original SPLENDOR / SINE / OCCASU arrangement
  are preserved. There is no substituted typeface.

Editing
  Open the SVG in a vector editor. Use the named layers to select the crest,
  large crown, antlers, supporters, shield, scrolls or outlined motto words.
  Each anatomical section contains separate black and white compound paths.
  Curves can be edited at node level. The three motto layers contain one
  compound path per letter, including counters.
  Adjacent sections overlap slightly to avoid antialiasing hairlines.
  Concealed anatomy is not invented; this is a faithful reconstruction of
  the visible supplied image. Moving sections may expose their original cuts.

Quality checks
  Compared against the supplied raster at identical original coordinates.
  Reviewed white and colored backgrounds, narrow antler channels, faces,
  hoof divisions, crown jewels, lettering and ribbon curls at enlarged scale.
  Rendered and inspected the SVG and final PDF.
  Verified that the SVG and PDF contain vector paths, with no embedded images.

Reference
  reference/Original-reference.png is the uploaded source, unchanged.
