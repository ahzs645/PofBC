# Evidence and the current identical-tree assumption

Eleven visually selected corresponding source features strongly support the reuse of a common underlying tree drawing. A fixed five-sixths uniform scale plus translation (53.2772727,23.0666667) fits these landmarks with 0.397 px RMS error, within their approximately 0.5–1 px reading uncertainty. Full details are in uniform-transform-review.json and shared-tree-landmarks.json.

The previous source-fitted reconstruction reused the left pattern and separately completed the fuller pale right side. The user explicitly requested the stronger assumption that the entire tree is the same artwork. The current SVG implements that assumption literally, with one visible master and one live pale use reference, no independent pale shapes or omitted openings. The pale right side consequently differs from the raster source. This is a chosen reconstruction rule, not new proof about historical production.
