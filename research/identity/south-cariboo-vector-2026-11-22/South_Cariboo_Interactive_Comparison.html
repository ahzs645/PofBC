Where agreement changes

This revision follows the requested exact shared-tree construction. It removes the previous independent pale right completion, so agreement with the supplied image may decrease beside the lettering. The overlays show those differences directly; the new construction is not claimed to be closer in every region.

How to read the comparison and measurements

Opacity overlay: 0% shows the original; 100% shows the selected reconstruction. The vector is composited against white so its transparent openings are compared correctly. Vertical wipe: original on the left, selected reconstruction on the right. Red / cyan: red indicates greater original ink density, cyan greater vector density, and neutral black or gray indicates similar density.

Each vector is rendered at 1890 × 846 and reduced to the original 315 × 141 grid with Lanczos for measurement. The density overlay is continuous, with no threshold. Foreground statistics use projected green-ink density ≥ 0.25 for all artwork and the pale window, and ≥ 0.75 for the dark tree and clear-background lettering. Boundary distances are symmetric; region-cut edges are excluded. No additional local warp was applied for this comparison.

The master consists of deliberately authored branch shapes and negative openings. The linked pale instance uses the entire master, with no independent completion and no omitted openings. No automatic tracing or raster contour extraction was used. The measurements describe agreement with the supplied raster, not historical authenticity.

All artwork and controls are embedded. This comparison can be opened offline and requires no external fonts or services.
