# Focused source search: the historical brush-tree artwork

**Checked on 22 November 2026.** This follow-up sought a sharper specimen of
the same South Cariboo Community Health Council brush-tree drawing. It examined
promising council-site documents and earlier/later image routes. It did not
find a stronger specimen. The supplied 315 x 141 colour image remains the
strongest verified source of this exact drawing in the material recovered.

## Later council website and Health Plan

The [council homepage captured on 10 July 2001](https://web.archive.org/web/20010710011547id_/http://www.100mile.com/health/)
uses document folders such as `minutes/`, `agenda/`, `members/`, and
`healthplan99/`. These routes differ from the earlier host's HTML index names.

The [later Health Plan PDF, captured on 28 June 2004](https://web.archive.org/web/20040628093546id_/http://www.100mile.com/health/healthplan99/healthplan99.pdf)
was downloaded and structurally inspected. It is byte-identical to the retained
earlier copy: 78,537 bytes, SHA-256
`52dfc8eb3cd2ba99773fb67efc8ab1606ab95e5da3863ca13b489218e655e19a`.
It has 19 pages, no vector drawing paths, and one 315 x 141 RGB logo image.
The later URL therefore supplies no additional branch detail.

## Earlier annual report: a real reference, an unavailable file

The [13 November 1999 council notice page](https://web.archive.org/web/19991113061927id_/http://www2.bcinternet.net/~100mile/health/notice1.html)
links a "1997 / 1998 Annual Report" as `annual98.pdf`. The
[15 December 2000 notice-page capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20001215012800id_/http://www2.bcinternet.net/~100mile/health/notice1.html)
repeats that link. The original PDF URL is:

`http://www2.bcinternet.net/~100mile/health/annual98.pdf`

Two attempted archival PDF retrievals returned HTTP 404. Wayback's availability
API returned an empty `archived_snapshots` object for that exact URL. Focused
title/name searches did not locate another copy. The annual report could not
be inspected, so its logo content and image quality remain unknown. An
unavailable snapshot does not establish that no physical or alternate copy
exists.

not supply a recovered higher-resolution version of that image.

## Search limits and another unrecovered lead

Exact council-name, historical "100 Mile House & District Community Health
Council," annual-report, letterhead, newsletter and PDF searches were checked
using two search engines. Most results were text references in government
records. Modern foundation branding and the solid black tree variant were not
treated as substitutes for the historical brush drawing.

The [2001 Select Standing Committee on Health report](https://lims.leg.bc.ca/pcms/file/14438/Rpt-HEALTH-37-2-PatientsFirst-2001-DEC-10.pdf)
lists Gail Morrison / South Cariboo Community Health Council as written
submission 246. The original submission was not recovered. No logo or
letterhead claim can be made from the index entry alone.

Individual Wayback snapshots were accessible during this search, while a
prefix CDX index request timed out. The search therefore followed actual
links in recovered council pages and inspected selected promising documents.
No verified vector original, sharper copy of the same brush trees, or
substantiated stock-art/designer attribution was found.
