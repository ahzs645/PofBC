# All photographed handbook branding-panel variants

This folder contains 24 editable vector panel templates, corresponding one-to-one with the photographed source inventory (23 distinct handbook numbers; two separately photographed no.20 copies). The production SVGs have no text elements, embedded images, fonts or placeholder labels. Each also has a PNG preview.

These preserve the visible title line breaks, handbook numbers, subject-emblem presence and museum footer treatment. They use normalized panel geometry and approximate photographic colours. The open central field is intentional: natural-history illustrations and authors are outside this branding-panel reconstruction. They are not exact complete-cover facsimiles.

Nos.36 and37 have no BCPM monogram, following their photographs. No category symbol is added to covers where none is visible. Faint symbols are preserved as approximate pale ink. No.23 only shows “Some Common / Marine Fishes” in its actual photographed header. Bird-volume topic lines are included.

Fixed lettering is reconstructed from the closest inspected public modern Neue Helvetica Pro43ExtendedLight specimen, with straight and cubic paths. The exact historical handbook typeface is unconfirmed. These are limited fixed wordmarks, not a font distribution.

`manifest.json` records the exact source URLs, source date status, heading text, observed mark status, panel positions and approximate palette for each template. Seller dates are not treated as verified publication dates. Source comparison sheets include full source photographs with the unillustrated templates beside them.
