## Holds and next sources

- **Grand Forks:** [the official original](https://www.grandforks.ca/wp-content/uploads/Grand-Forks-Logo-Home.png) and the [2013 launch release](https://www.grandforks.ca/wp-content/uploads/media-releases/Branding-Civic-Info-Media-Release.pdf) are confirmed. The City [sets conditions on logo use](https://www.grandforks.ca/wp-content/uploads/Logo-Use-Guidelines-and-User-Agreement.pdf). Keep source-linked, not copied into the gallery or counted as held.
- **shíshálh Nation Government District:** the [Business Licence Application](https://shishalh.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/sNGD-Business-License-Form.pdf#page=1) confirms use of a crest on District letterhead, not a separate District identity. Keep the original as a source link, not a downloaded gallery asset or an assumed clearance.
- **Okanagan Falls:** the [official placeholder and November 6, 9 and 23 agenda covers](https://okanaganfalls.ca/) did not establish a separate mark. This does not prove one cannot exist.
- **Thompson Health Region:** *Canadian Nurse*, October 2000, vol. 96 no. 9, printed p. 44, general RN recruitment ad. OCR records the agency name and “A Path to Better Health,” not the artwork. The [publisher archive](https://www.canadian-nurse.com/archive/2000-2009) returns 403 and the indexed signed PDF link expired. No scan was visually verified. Further leads: Merritt council packet November 10, 1998, item 10.B.1, printed p. 54 (dated September 24 letter), and March 9, 1999 packet p. 73 (dated February 19 letter). Both direct archive packets returned 404.
- **Sea to Sky Community Health Council:** *The Squamish Chief*, May 8, 2001, printed B12 social-worker recruitment, plus April 22, 1997 and February 8, 2000 notices. The [old indexed scan](https://squamishlibrary.digitalcollections.ca/uploads/r/squamish-public-library/2/2/22781/20010508_The_Chief.pdf) no longer yields the issue and [the replacement index](https://library.biblioboard.com/curation/squamish-history-archives) soft-404s. No design was accepted from headings in OCR.
- **Metro Transit Operating Company:** *The Buzzer*, September 18, 1981, has a publication masthead and an ordinary publisher credit, not an identifiable corporation logo.
- **BC Electric, Crown period:** *The Buzzer*, December 29, 1961, confirms name and date, not a corporate mark. The [transit pass](https://www.powerpioneers.com/media/2016/04/BCEPass1962F.jpg) visibly states validity through December 31, 1962; that is not its issue date. Its [source caption](https://www.powerpioneers.com/our-community/bc-hydro-history/1962-1972/) describes it as the last pass before the transition. The [Museum of Vancouver token record](https://openmovportal.ca/argus/final/Portal/Main.aspx?component=AAFG&record=233138fa-2875-46c8-a9af-9a8ef8b6727f) supports carryover use under BC Hydro, not an original dated Crown-period design.
