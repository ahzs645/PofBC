    <h1 class="visually-hidden">BC Archives</h1>

    
<div class="wp-block-columns grid two-thirds-one-third is-layout-flex wp-container-core-columns-is-layout-7387b849 wp-block-columns-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-column is-vertically-aligned-center col-span-12 is-layout-flow wp-block-column-is-layout-flow">
<p class="has-x-large-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Connecting you with the recorded heritage of British Columbia</p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">Welcome to the BC Archives. We collect and preserve millions of archival treasures that reveal BC’s past, so that they can be accessible to you.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">We are located on the traditional territories of the&nbsp;lək̓ʷəŋən (Songhees and Xwsepsum Nations). We extend our appreciation for the opportunity to live and learn on this territory.</p>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">In late 2026, the BC Archives will be moving to the new <a href="https://rbcm.ca/parc/">PARC Campus</a> in Colwood, BC. <a href="https://bcarchives.ca/about-bc-archives/parc-faq/">Learn more</a> about new location, service levels, and temporary closures.</p>

<h2 id="search-our-collections" class="wp-block-heading">Search Our Collections</h2>



<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph">We have three searchable databases in our collection, with different types of records.&nbsp;</p>



<div id="d41d8" class="wp-block-ds-tabs tabs"><div class="tabs-list" data-mobile-open="false" data-number-tabs="4" role="tablist"><button class="tab-jump-to">Jump to…</button><button class="tab" role="tab" id="tab-0-d41d8" tabindex="-1"><span class="tab-toggle">BC Archives Collection</span></button><button class="tab" role="tab" id="tab-1-d41d8" tabindex="-1"><span class="tab-toggle">Archives Library Collection</span></button><button class="tab" role="tab" id="tab-2-d41d8" tabindex="-1"><span class="tab-toggle">Genealogy Database</span></button><button class="tab" role="tab" id="tab-3-d41d8" tabindex="-1"><span class="tab-toggle">Search the BC Archives Website</span></button></div>

<div class="tab-content" hidden id="bc-archives-collection" role="tabpanel" tabindex="-1"><h3>BC Archives Collection</h3>

<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph"><strong>What&#8217;s Inside the Collection</strong></p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The BC Archives’ records include over 5 million textual records, photographs, documentary art, sound recordings, moving images and cartographic records such as maps.&nbsp;</p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The records include include government, private, genealogical and Indigenous material. View our research guides for more info.&nbsp;</p>


<p class="has-medium-font-size wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Using the BC Archives online database</strong></p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">With this database you can search for descriptions of archival records held at the BC Archives and access digital versions, where available.</p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Please read the Notes Area in all higher levels of description for access and copying restrictions, to see availability of the records in other formats and to locate the finding aid.&nbsp;</p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For instructions and tips for searching, please see the main page of the BC Archives Collections Search.</p>


<div class="wp-block-button"><a class="wp-block-button__link wp-element-button" href="https://search-bcarchives.royalbcmuseum.bc.ca/">Search BC Archives Collection</a></div>

</div>


<div class="tab-content" hidden id="archives-library-collection" role="tabpanel" tabindex="-1"><h3>Archives Library Collection</h3>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You can search for descriptions of publications held in our library using this database. Publications include books, directories, pamphlets, government reports, and more.</p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">For help with your search, please see the Help tab at the bottom of the Search screen.</p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">There are also indexes to and inventories of some of our library materials available on our&nbsp;<a href="https://bcarchives.ca/guides-indexes-and-inventories/">Reference Guides and Inventories</a>&nbsp;page.</p>


<div class="wp-block-button"><a class="wp-block-button__link wp-element-button" href="https://rbcm.catalogue.libraries.coop/eg/opac/home?physical_loc=488&amp;locg=">Search Archives Library Collection</a></div>

</div>


<div class="tab-content" hidden id="genealogy-database" role="tabpanel" tabindex="-1"><h3>Genealogy Database</h3>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You can search for open birth, marriage and death registrations using this database. Many registrations have been digitized and are attached to the listing under Vital Stat Image(s).</p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Watch our instructional video:&nbsp;<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FirL5UOx47Y&amp;index=3&amp;list=PLN9Hdj6yjKtWyfnPyT-UVxasVYcVyd_6g" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">How to: BC Archives Genealogy Search</a></p>


<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you cannot find an image of the vital event registration record online, please refer to&nbsp;<a href="https://bcarchives.ca/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/05/Vitaleventregistrations_Information_BCA2018.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Accessing BC vital event registrations guide</a></p>


<div class="wp-block-button"><a class="wp-block-button__link wp-element-button" href="https://search-collections.royalbcmuseum.bc.ca/Genealogy">Search the Genealogy Database</a></div>

</div>


<div class="tab-content" hidden id="search-the-bc-archives-website" role="tabpanel" tabindex="-1"><h3>Search the BC Archives Website</h3>

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">You can also search within the content of the pages on this website. Results will not contain any records from the BC Archives collection.</p>
