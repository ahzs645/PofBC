# Undated newspaper clipping: “Identity crisis for Socreds?”

## Primary-source summary

An uploaded clipping headed **“Identity crisis for Socreds?”** reports that the British Columbia Social Credit Party / League had several competing identity designs: the national party's wheel-and-cross, a new stylized provincial profile, and clasped hands. It compares the provincial-profile symbol to the **B.C. Forest Service emblem without the tree**.

The article attributes to League president Grace McCarthy an explanation that the provincial-profile design signified a B.C.-based political party without national affiliation. It does **not** claim the Forest Service transferred artwork, designed the party mark, or approved its use. Nor does it establish when the Forest Service mark was introduced or retired.
