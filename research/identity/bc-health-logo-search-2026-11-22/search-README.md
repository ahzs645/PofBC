# Historical BC health logos: original-vector search

Research date: 2026-11-22

## Result

No original vector was verified for any of the three requested identities. This search recovered a new colour website variant for Kootenay Boundary and two larger original colour raster variants for Campbell River/Nootka. The existing Sunshine Coast website banner remains the best verified reference examined.

All recovered GIF and JPEG originals in this package retain the downloaded bytes. The Sunshine Coast PNG is the previously published gallery assembly of three original JPEG strips, which are included separately. No SVG, tracing, upscaling, or reconstruction is represented as an original vector.

## 1. Kootenay Boundary

The recovered artwork has purple figures and lettering, teal landscape/background areas, white mountain tops and a circular white boundary. The embedded web palette includes #330066 and #669999. These are values in the surviving GIF, not established print specifications.

- Original GIF: [archive capture, August 12, 2002](https://web.archive.org/web/20020812142027id_/http://kbchss.hnet.bc.ca:80/images/nav_top/kbh_logo.gif)
- Referring homepage: [July 19, 2002 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20020719103729id_/http://www.kbchss.hnet.bc.ca:80/)
- About page: [August 3, 2002 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20020803165237id_/http://www.kbchss.hnet.bc.ca:80/about_us.html)
- Local original: kootenay/archive-logo.gif

The homepage identifies Kootenay Boundary Health, connects its formation to the community health services societies, and embeds the original image. Its last-revised statement is January 21, 2002. This is strong attribution to the requested historical organization.

### A separate historical variant

The current gallery's 1998 letterhead specimen places "together" at the top inside the circle, "for" below the figures, and "health" at the bottom. The recovered website variant instead places "Kootenay Boundary Health" around the outer upper arc and "Together for health" along the lower inside arc.

The new colour file should therefore be retained as a separate specimen. It does not prove the exact colours of the 1998 letterhead arrangement, and its archive date does not establish an adoption date. The two baseline gallery crops are supplied for comparison, with their source record.

### Vector recovery checks

The 1998 letterhead pages in the [official FOI disclosure](https://docs.openinfo.gov.bc.ca/Response_Package_TRA-2017-70554.pdf) are full-page scans. PDF pages 28 and 32 contain no original drawing paths to extract. The archived source-domain inventory returned HTML and raster artwork. The linked publications page survives only as a captured 404. Queries for the alternate kootenay-boundary-health.ca domain were partly empty and partly unsuccessful; those failures do not establish comprehensive absence.

The [credits page](https://web.archive.org/web/20020803171643id_/http://www.kbchss.hnet.bc.ca:80/credits.html) names Designucleus Corporate Communications in its author metadata. An [archived Designucleus homepage](https://web.archive.org/web/20020119132952id_/http://www.designucleus.com/) confirms a design firm offering print, identity and web work. This is a possible production-file lead, but the evidence identifies website authorship only; it does not establish who drew the logo.

The prior project lead to Canadian Nurse, September 2001, vol. 97 no. 8, printed page 56 remains uninspected because attempted public download routes returned 403. No artwork claim is made from that lead.

## 2. Campbell River/Nootka

Two larger original images were recovered by following a historical server address embedded in the council website.

- [Original GIF, April 22, 2002 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20020422014408id_/http://209.52.239.53:80/crnchc/images/gif_logo_june_00%5b1%5d.gif)
- [Original JPEG, June 8, 2002 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20020608184156id_/http://209.52.239.53:80/crnchc/images/jpg_logo_june_00%5b1%5d.jpg)
- [Referring council homepage](https://web.archive.org/web/20020527212227id_/http://209.52.239.53:80/)
- [Referring council employment application](https://web.archive.org/web/20020213094302id_/http://209.52.239.53:80/crnchc/htmdocs/careers/emply_app1.htm)

Both original files are 527 x 507 pixels. The existing gallery GIF is 345 x 328. The recovered images provide clearer boundaries and internal details. The JPEG looks smoother; the GIF contains visible palette dithering. Both have been retained unchanged.

The original homepage explicitly names Campbell River/Nootka Community Health Council and references the GIF, while the employment application names the council and references the JPEG. The files were served at larger native dimensions than the small display size specified in the HTML.

Text edging and the highlighted map treatment differ from the earlier gallery GIF. Keep these as additional source variants. The filename includes "june_00", but this is not an independently verified design or adoption date; the verified archive sightings are in 2002.

### Vector recovery checks

Fresh all-date inventories returned 223 unique domain URLs (133 successful) and 137 unique historical-IP URLs (124 successful). No native vector or document target appeared in these returned indexes.

The [archived What's New page](https://web.archive.org/web/20020214114950id_/http://www.crncare.org:80/crnchc/htmdocs/whatsnew.htm) supplies concrete missing-file leads:

- annualreport.pdf
- Vol1-Iss1-Apr01.pdf
- Vol1-Iss2-Sep01.pdf

An employment page also links chc-application.zip. Archive retrieval returned 404 for the attempted files. Their contents and artwork format have not been inspected.

A separate search downloaded and text-searched 81 available Discovery Islander issues spanning the 1997-2001 range, totaling 1,752 pages. The two text-hit pages were rendered and visually inspected; they contain council notices without the logo. The other 1,750 pages were text-searched only. The query-specific result is not proof that no image-only advertisement appears elsewhere. Exact issue URLs and the scan log are preserved under campbell/.

## 3. Sunshine Coast

The best verified reference examined remains the 625 x 150 pixel website banner. It includes the sun, three people, blue ground, the council name and a photographic background.

- [Original council homepage, April 2, 2001 capture](https://web.archive.org/web/20010402060735id_/http://www.sunshinecoastcommunityhealthcouncil.com:80/)
- [Original top JPEG strip, 625 x 54](https://web.archive.org/web/20010602235437id_/http://www.sunshinecoastcommunityhealthcouncil.com:80/images/banner_top_1.jpg)
- [Original middle JPEG strip, 625 x 73](https://web.archive.org/web/20010602234716id_/http://www.sunshinecoastcommunityhealthcouncil.com:80/images/banner_mid_2.jpg)
- [Original bottom JPEG strip, 625 x 23](https://web.archive.org/web/20010602233316id_/http://www.sunshinecoastcommunityhealthcouncil.com:80/images/banner_bottom_3.jpg)
- [Existing gallery assembly](https://projects.ahmadjalil.com/PofBC/identity-collected/sunshine-coast-community-health-council/sunshine-coast-community-health-council-2001-website-banner.png)

The three strips were downloaded again in this pass and match the hashes in the project's prior source record. The PNG joins their decoded pixels at their native width. June 2, 2001 is the original strip capture date, not an established logo design date.

The expanded archive query returned 895 capture records covering 75 distinct normalized URL keys, of which 577 records were successful. No successful PDF or vector file appeared in that returned inventory. The three concrete PDF filenames are Health_Plan.pdf, Health_Plan_print.pdf and Health_Plan_screen.pdf, each preserved only as a 404 HTML response.

A surviving original copy of one of those health plans is a specific remaining recovery target. It is not yet known whether the files contain the logo or whether their artwork is vector. The site's inspected newsletter, plans and other information pages reuse the same raster banner. Four additional GIF candidates were viewed and contained unrelated illustrations or quotations.

## How to use these findings in the gallery

1. Add the Kootenay colour GIF as an additional original raster specimen, dated by its archive capture.
2. Add both Campbell River/Nootka files as larger original raster variants, retaining the earlier specimen.
3. Keep Sunshine Coast classified as an original raster banner with vector status unresolved.
4. If vector reconstructions are created later, identify them explicitly as reconstructions and preserve the source variants and dates independently.

No recovered file in this package is a verified native vector. A negative search result is limited to the sources inspected; it does not establish that an original vector never existed.

## Package and verification

The asset-manifest.json file records original source URLs, native dimensions, byte lengths and SHA-256 hashes for the principal images. Large third-party source PDFs and the full newspaper collection are not bundled; their exact URLs and inspection logs are retained.
