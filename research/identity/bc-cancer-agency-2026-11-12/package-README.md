# Fraser Valley Health Region and BC Cancer Agency: logo sources

Research and extraction date: November 12, 2026.

## Findings

Both marks in the supplied screenshot are identifiable in the cover of the **Fraser Valley Health Centre / Eastern Fraser Valley Cancer Centre: Project Definition Report**, dated **September 27, 2000** on its second PDF page. A public copy of the cover and contents is hosted by the Czech Ministry of Finance:

https://mf.gov.cz/assets/attachments/PPP-Canada-Project-definition-report-Contents.pdf

The cover contains two embedded raster images: the Fraser Valley Health Region colour flower at **78 × 78 pixels**, and the BC Cancer Agency monochrome circular symbol at **162 × 162 pixels**. The extracted PNG files preserve those native dimensions. A larger screenshot of the cover does not contain additional original image detail.

## BC Cancer Agency: usable original vector artwork found

The official **BC Cancer Agency 2015 Research Report**, PDF page **32**, contains the blue circular emblem and complete horizontal logo as vector paths. The SVG files in this package copy those original exported paths; they were not traced or reconstructed. The full wordmark and supporting lettering are outlined. The PDF files are cropped excerpts that preserve the original PDF artwork and colour resources. SVG colours are the RGB rendering of that publication.

Source (full report, approximately 33 MB; linked rather than bundled):

https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/about-site/Documents/ResearchReport2015_Final_Web.pdf

### Independent colour and artwork evidence

The official **Radiation Therapy: What to Expect** Chinese/English booklet also has the historical logo as vector artwork on PDF page **28**:

https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/patient-and-public-info-site/Documents/Radiation_Therapy_What_to_Expect_Chinese_English_2.pdf

The booklet's PDF metadata is dated July 2010. Its embedded artwork metadata identifies **BCCA_Template_Horz**, created in **Adobe Illustrator CS2**, with a file date of **June 21, 2007**. Named PDF colour resources include **PANTONE 273 U** and **PANTONE Warm Gray 11 U**; the page's blue and warm-gray logo fills are consistent with their embedded alternate RGB values. These are evidence of artwork and colours used in this publication, not a claim that the logo was first designed in 2007 or that a complete historical brand standard has been recovered.

The unchanged booklet is included as `radiation-therapy-2010.pdf`.

A separate photograph of the actual facility signage, photographed April 22, 2014, is available at **2,562 × 800 pixels**:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:BC_Cancer_Agency_logo.jpg

Photographer: Canuckle. The photograph is licensed CC BY-SA 3.0; see its source page for details. The photograph is linked, not included in this package.

The organization announced its simplified **BC Cancer** name and new identity on **November 15, 2017**:

https://bccancerfoundation.com/news-and-media/blog/bc-cancer-and-bc-cancer-foundation-strengthen-partnership-break-down-cancer/

Official history: https://www.bccancer.bc.ca/about/history

The old circular emblem is documented in use in the 2000 report and the later official publications. Its original designer and introduction date were not established.

## Fraser Valley Health Region: confirmed original colour reference

`fraser-valley-health-region-2000-original-78px.png` contains the embedded blue/green flower from the 2000 report. **No verified standalone vector or higher-resolution colour master was located in this search.** The unchanged cover/contents PDF is included as `fraser-valley-2000-project-report.pdf`.

A useful further lead is **Canadian Nurse, May 2000, volume 96, no. 5**, whose indexed recruitment advertisements identify Fraser Valley Health Region and the slogan **Integrating Health Care**. The indexed issue points to printed pages 46 and 49. The archive and its expired signed download could not be retrieved for visual verification during this research, so these are leads rather than supplied logo assets:

https://www.canadian-nurse.com/archive/2000-2009

June 1998, volume 94, no. 6 is another indexed recruitment-ad lead:

https://www.cna-aiic.ca/dev-cn-en/archive/1990-1999

The historical flower belongs to the former **Fraser Valley Health Region**. The December 2001 regulation lists it, Simon Fraser Health Region, and South Fraser Health Region as the authorities amalgamated and renamed **Fraser Health Authority**:

https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/bcgaz2/bcgaz2/v44n25_293-2001

For a historical logo catalogue, keep the former region distinct from the successor Fraser Health identity. The 2000 date is a documented appearance, not an established first-use date.

## Verification and provenance

`source-manifest.json` records source URLs, SHA-256 checksums, source-page numbers, extraction bounds, dimensions, and limitations.
