Registration stays fixed

One source pixel maps to 4 SVG units. Both vectors retain their 784 × 872 artboard. There is no independent fitting, rotation, skew or warping.

Visible boundaries only

Outlines are derived from high-resolution renders of the finished artwork. Covered paths and invisible white-on-white construction edges are excluded.

Scan blur is not a radius

The original is only 196 × 218 pixels and is already blurred. Soft pixels do not establish that every original corner was rounded. Enlarged source pixels are deliberately visible. Numbered anchors locate areas for inspection.
