# South Cariboo Community Health Council vector search - 22 November 2026

## Result
No original vector emblem found in this pass. Downloaded all 27 successful archived PDF URL entries from the two historical website /health/ prefixes. They represent 26 unique PDF files and 149 unique pages (27 downloads / 168 pages including a duplicate health plan). Parsed graphics for every page and visually checked all 27 first pages plus pages with complex drawing paths.

The 1999 Health Plan is the only one of these PDFs containing the two-tree council emblem. Page 1 holds image XObject 8, JPEG/DCTDecode, 315 x 141 pixels. The full PDF has no drawing paths. The mirrored healthplan99.pdf is byte-identical. Other files contain meeting text, tables, heading letters and a DRAFT watermark, not a vector council tree emblem.
