# Sources and evidence boundaries

Research date: 2026-09-27. The evidence register distinguishes text, inspected images, and uninspected research leads.

## Established chronology

The official retrospective places the board’s foundation on 1 January 1917 [S1]. It identifies graphic changes in 1968 and 1983, while treating the 1974 wording amendment separately [S2]. The 2005 entry dates adoption of the WorkSafeBC operating name [S3]. None of these entries establishes an exact retirement date for a logo.

## What the vector artwork represents

The province-and-worker reconstruction follows undated secondary visual specimens [S5–S6]; connecting this specimen to the 1983 family is a reasoned identification, not a recovered dated master. The current reconstruction follows the supplied JPEG, cross-checked against a live official asset [S4]. The 1968 design is described in the timeline but is not fabricated.

## Outstanding evidence

The original 1968 design, pre-1968 artwork, a dated primary 1983 lockup, modern graphic first-use date, retirement dates, original typography and designer attribution remain unresolved. Official 2004 and 2005 report landing pages were found but their PDF files could not be retrieved in this session. Those covers were not inspected. The video landing page is a lead only.
