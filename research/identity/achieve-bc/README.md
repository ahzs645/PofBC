# Achieve BC artwork

The province launched the initiative on September 19, 2003. CIPO records the filing on September 11 and published notice on November 19 for the ACHIEVE BC name and five designs. The printed notices identify the province, represented by the Premier, as the party adopting and using the marks. The publication date supplies the date for each CIPO specimen in the index. [Launch release](https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/archive/2001-2005/2003OTP0081-000837.htm) · [Trade-marks Journal, pp. 311–312](https://epe.lac-bac.gc.ca/100/202/301/trade_marks_jrn-ef/2003/031119.pdf?nodisclaimer=1#page=314)
