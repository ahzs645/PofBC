# FORREX

FORREX was an independent charitable society in Kamloops. B.C. funded its work delivering the Provincial Forest Extension Program. Forest managers and other practitioners received research through workshops, publications, field tours and online services. The gallery gives SIFERP (1998–2002) its own collection and links it to FORREX at the 2002 registered name change. Both FORREX operating names appear on the FORREX timeline. The related-bodies directory records their shared legal history under society S-0038583. [The State of British Columbia’s Forests 2010, p. 236](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/environment/research-monitoring-and-reporting/reporting/envreportbc/archived-reports/sof_2010.pdf)
