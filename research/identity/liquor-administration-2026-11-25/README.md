# Liquor and cannabis administration

The institutional timeline appears on the Liquor Control Board and Liquor Distribution Branch gallery pages. Both link to the identity register. The family retains the stable URL parameter `identity-family=liquor-distribution`.

## Institutions and functions

| Date | Evidence and interpretation |
| --- | --- |
| October 1, 1917 and October 20, 1920 | Prohibition took effect in 1917. Voters chose government liquor sales in the 1920 plebiscite. [Legislative Assembly](https://www.leg.bc.ca/learn/discover-your-legislature/1917-prohibition-begins-by-referendum) |
| April 2 and June 15, 1921 | The Government Liquor Act received assent on April 2. The first nine government stores opened June 15. The Board’s first annual report begins with that operational date. [Original Act](https://free.bcpublications.ca/civix/document/id/hstats/hstats/929176308) · [First annual report](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/242418) · [LDB centenary](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2021FIN0038-001103) |
| April 18, 1973 | Amendments reassigned Board functions to a general manager and established the British Columbia Liquor Board for appeals. The Atlas records interim store administration under the Liquor Administration Branch. [Amendment Act, ss. 11–12 and 18](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/hstats/hstats/787618959) |
| June 26, 1975 | The distribution and licensing Acts received assent on the same day. Section 2 of each provided for a separate branch. Sections 32–34 of the licensing Act provided for the three-member British Columbia Liquor Board and specified appeals and directed hearings. [Distribution Act](https://free.bcpublications.ca/civix/document/id/hstats/hstats/706478044) · [Licensing Act](https://free.bcpublications.ca/civix/document/id/hstats/hstats/1305767717) |
| December 31, 1976 and April 1, 1977 | The old Act was repealed. Section 2 of the distribution Act came into force, with government stores and their employees continuing under the new framework. The budgets for distribution and licensing separated the following April. [Repeal order](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/oic/arc_oic/3493_1976) · [Commencement order](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/oic/arc_oic/3494_1976) · [Hansard, September 1, 1977, question 97](https://lims.leg.bc.ca/hdms/file/Debates/31st2nd/31p_02s_770901a.htm) |
| April 1, 2015 | All liquor retailers began purchasing under a common wholesale pricing model. The LDB separated its own retail and wholesale operations. [Government announcement](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2014JAG0319-001743) · [LDB 2017/18 annual report](https://www.bcldb.com/sites/default/files/LDB_17-18_ASPR_FINAL.PDF) |
| 2018 | The government had announced the LCLB’s new name, Liquor and Cannabis Regulation Branch, by July 5. The LDB’s cannabis division began wholesale and retail operations October 17, opening a public store in Kamloops and an online store. [Rename announcement](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2018AG0053-001343) · [LDB 2018/19 report](https://www.bcbudget.gov.bc.ca/Annual_Reports/2018_2019/pdf/agency/ldb.pdf) |
