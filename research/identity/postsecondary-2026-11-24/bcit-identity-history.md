# BCIT: dated logo sources and extraction records

The existing held website logo and the `bcit` subject ID are unchanged. This research documents dated use, not an exhaustive brand chronology. None of the historical artwork, including the separate review reconstructions, is hosted or embedded by this gallery.

## 2007 native-vector extraction receipts

[Machine-readable receipt](./bcit-2007-extraction-receipt.json). The SHA-256 of the original BCIT PDF is `fee6b026fcf3bef84b8c70475ce47956fb158c442c701a66950bd02808a18f1a`. Both examples come from the same archived page. The outgoing example is above the incoming example. Crop boxes use top-left PDF points.

| Lockup | PDF crop and original contents | QA at 8× |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outgoing Eras | `(218.5, 519.9, 392.4, 551.3)`, 97 fills and 2 strokes; no images or live text. | Filtered PDF matches the original crop pixel-for-pixel; final SVG thresholded ink overlap 1.0000. |
| Incoming Trade Gothic | `(216.25, 683.95, 392.75, 716.55)`, 38 fills and 2 strokes, plus 36 embedded font glyphs (two lines of name). No raster or external installed font used. | Filtered PDF matches the original crop pixel-for-pixel; final SVG thresholded ink overlap 0.9706. |

On verified source bytes, isolate each crop using the PDF graphics and clipping rather than tracing a render. The Trade Gothic outlines come from embedded PDF glyph paths: export text as paths through MuPDF, inline only their local glyph references, and retain paint order, transforms, stroke and clipping. Compare the filtered PDF to the original crop at the same scale, then compare the final SVG. The overlap figures are not a claim of pixel-identical SVG/PDF rendering or a chain of title for the artwork.

The private 1964 globe/spear and 1978 outline studies are geometric reconstructions from period rasters, not original vectors. Their review overlaps (0.856 and approximately 0.784, respectively) are measures against the chosen reference images, not proof of authenticity. They are not included in this repository.

## Reuse boundary

[BCIT procedure 7506-PR1](https://www.bcit.ca/files/pdf/policies/7506_pr1.pdf) documents the external permission process for BCIT-owned materials. The [1978–79 archive record](https://atom.lib.bcit.ca/index.php/full-time-calendar-1978-79) names BCIT as copyright holder and BCIT Photography marks its 1964 image all rights reserved. The [Canadian Heraldic Authority reproduction FAQ](https://www.gg.ca/en/heraldry/faq) requires permissions from both the grantee and the Authority to reproduce heraldry. Links to originals are not clearance to reproduce or distribute.
