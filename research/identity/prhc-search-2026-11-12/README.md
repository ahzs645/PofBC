# Provincial Rental Housing Corporation: logo search, 12 November 2026

**Result: no separate PRHC logo or readable seal impression has been found.** The corporation's
own reports use plain name headings, and its correspondence and property signage carry BC
Housing's identity. That fits its structure, but it does not show PRHC never had a mark.

The corporation holds land and has no employees. The British Columbia Housing Management
Commission (BC Housing), established separately in 1967, acts as its agent. Dated findings are
in `research/atlas/events-prhc-2026-11-12.json`.

## Names (corporation BC0052129, OrgBook BC)

| From | Name |
|---|---|
| 13 December 1961 | Pemco Holdings Ltd. |
| 7 May 1976 | British Columbia Housing Corporation |
| 21 December 1976 | Provincial Rental Housing Corporation |

The Province acquired the company in 1973 (Auditor General, 1999). BC Housing gained control of it
on 26 January 2024 and consolidates it from 31 March 2024.
