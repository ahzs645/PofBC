# Provincial Rental Housing Corporation: logo search, 12 November 2026

**Result: no separate PRHC logo or readable seal impression has been found.** The corporation's
own reports use plain name headings, and its correspondence and property signage carry BC
Housing's identity. That fits its structure, but it does not show PRHC never had a mark.

The corporation holds land and has no employees. The British Columbia Housing Management
Commission (BC Housing), established separately in 1967, acts as its agent. Dated findings are
in `research/atlas/events-prhc-2026-11-12.json`.

## Names (corporation BC0052129, OrgBook BC)

| From | Name |
|---|---|
| 13 December 1961 | Pemco Holdings Ltd. |
| 7 May 1976 | British Columbia Housing Corporation |
| 21 December 1976 | Provincial Rental Housing Corporation |

The Province acquired the company in 1973 (Auditor General, 1999). BC Housing gained control of it
on 26 January 2024 and consolidates it from 31 March 2024.

## What was checked

- **Name treatments, not logos:**
  - the 1976 Municipal Affairs and Housing report page (stacked serif capitals);
  - the 1978 Crown grant map (slanted capitals, OIC 1121/1978);
  - BC Housing's 2000/01 report section heading (orange spaced capitals in a ruled box);
  - the 2003/04 financial statements cover;
  - the 2022/23 Statement of Financial Information cover.
- **Orders in council:** 3283/1976 and 279/1979 carry the provincial arms only. All 125 "provincial
  rental housing" matches in the historical orders were reviewed.
- **Trademark database:** searched for PRHC, Provincial Rental Housing Corporation, Pemco Holdings
  and British Columbia Housing Corporation, with no matches. BC Housing's own illustrated-houses mark
  is trademark 0919577.
- **Seals:** the Vancouver Bylaw 8155 agreement shows only a "C/S" placeholder, and the Surrey Bylaw
  17264 agreement has no readable impression.
- **BC Housing branding on PRHC's behalf:**
  - a 6 November 2025 letter to 100 Mile House, written on PRHC's behalf on BC Housing letterhead;
  - the Riverview Lands sign protocol (12 May 2022), which specifies BC Housing and Kwikwetlem
    identities for the site.
