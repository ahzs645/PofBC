# Tourism, Recreation and Culture, 22 November 2026

A supplied vector reconstruction of the Ministry of Tourism, Recreation and Culture's mark, with
"HON. BILL REID, MINISTER". Read by `scripts/build-collected-marks.mjs` (PROGRAM_PACKAGES) from
`source-register.json`; shown in the gallery collection `tourism-recreation-culture`, tied to the
government map's ministry of that name (`ministry-of-tourism-recreation-and-culture-1986`).

- Source: a screenshot of the logo, 730 × 442 px, supplied to the project; the document it came
  from is not recorded. Its wording dates it: Bill Reid was the ministry's only minister, from
  6 November 1986 to 6 July 1988.
- Method: eight paths of the gallery's own flag (`src/assets/flagMark.js`, as on the Super, Natural
  sign) each placed to the screenshot; three wave stripes redrawn as closed curves running out to the
  left; the gold sun left out, as the one-ink print shows it. Lettering is outlined C059 Italic and
  Liberation Serif Italic, spaced to the screenshot; the original typeface is not identified.
- Overlap with the screenshot (intersection over union at its resolution): flag and stripes 95.0
  percent, flag alone 88.8, lettering 58–66 (`Overlap_Measurements.json`).
- Files in `public/identity-collected/tourism-recreation-culture/`: the mark in its blue-violet
  (#3C40A0, estimated from the screenshot), in black, and a vector PDF.

The supplier's own notes are in `package-README.md`; `reconstruction/` holds the placements and
rebuilt curves, `review/` the overlap analysis and the source-aligned SVG.
