# IUS font notes and reconstruction schedule

**Revision: v1.2.** This kit reconstructs the lettering visible in the supplied photographs. No original typeface has been conclusively identified. Font names below identify the digital outlines used to build this kit; descriptions such as *Helvetica-like*, *Bookman-like* and *collegiate* describe observed styles or comparison families, not authenticated historical specifications.

The original exhibition photographs take priority over the four separately supplied contemporary raster reconstructions. The evidence is affected by perspective, reflections, print spread, metal edging, fabric folds and limited resolution. A different apparent weight on a pin or small label does not establish a different original font cut.

## What changed in v1.2

- **Northern capital N:** replaced the Bookman-derived N with a deliberately drawn vector letter. Its thinner tapered serifs, lighter diagonal and mainly left-projecting top-right serif follow the supplied original button photographs more closely.
- **Retained lettering:** the fitted Bookman-derived **“orthern”** and the separately constructed large **U** retain the v1.1 treatment. This is a focused capital-N correction, not a newly identified historical font or a redraw of the complete word.
- **Evidence and construction:** [the Northern N detail proof](reference/font-audit/northern-n/Northern_N_Detail.png) compares the source and revised letter. The N is constructed from editable vector geometry without automatic bitmap tracing. The exact historical typeface remains unconfirmed.

The existing campaign comparison panels document **v1.0 to v1.1**. They remain part of the review history; the separate N proof records the **v1.1 to v1.2** correction.

## What changed in v1.1

- **Pin weight:** added a controlled black-edge expansion of **0.020 × 108 = 2.16 units per edge**. The construction uses a 4.32-unit centred black outline while retaining the separate metal backing geometry. The adjustment is documented independently of the base font name.
- **Toll-free label:** replaced the initial Roman serif words and telephone numerals with fitted **URW Bookman Demi**. The sans organization wording is retained.
- **YES button:** changed the SAY YES TO ink box from **642 × 104** to **615 × 113**, retaining its lower ink anchor at **307**. NORTHERN now has rounded cubic corners. These are optical fitting changes, not evidence for a new historical font identification.
- **Northern U button:** refined the large U as custom geometry; the word Northern retains **URW Bookman Demi**.
- **Business appeal:** restored italic display lettering using actual **Nimbus Roman Bold Italic** outlines. Upright serif questions and body remain upright.
- **Brochure wording:** raised CAMPAIGN from **120** to **132** against SUPPORT at **160**, preserving word widths, right alignment, cap-top anchoring and the component canvas.
- **Invitation transcription:** preserved the photographed **DAHLLOFF / Dahlloff**, with two final Fs. This is a source-text correction, separate from font identification.
- **Authoring dependency:** corrected the regular italic filename to **NimbusRoman-Italic.otf** and added the separate bold italic mapping. No synthetic slant is needed for the revised appeal.

See [the application audit](reference/font-audit/applications-audit.md) and [the direct before-and-after panel](reference/font-audit/applications-before-after.png). Its vector panels are rendered from the actual saved v1.0 and revised v1.1 SVG paths.

## Why these digital font families are used

The [CTAN URW Base35 catalogue](https://ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/urw/base35?lang=en) documents the legacy Nimbus Sans, Nimbus Roman and Nimbus Mono families as substitutes for Helvetica, Times and Courier respectively. This supports the selection of practical digital surrogates; it does not identify the fonts in an IUS photograph.

[Artifex's Nimbus font configuration](https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/urw-base35-fonts/blob/master/fontconfig/urw-nimbus-sans.conf) explicitly maps Nimbus Sans to Helvetica and Nimbus Sans Narrow to Helvetica Narrow. These are substitution mappings, not proof that either original brand name was used by IUS.

The [Linotype Eurostile Next family description on MyFonts](https://www.myfonts.com/pages/linotype-eurostile-next) describes squared forms with subtle curves and a later redrawing of the older Eurostile design. It provides candidate-family context for the YES button's NORTHERN lettering. No Eurostile Next font is used in the delivered artwork, and no historical attribution to that later digital family is made.

## Editing and font availability

**Font binaries are not bundled.** The delivered logo and application SVG/PDF/EPS masters use outlined lettering and need no installed font to open, scale or recolour. Individual letters remain editable as vector geometry, rather than live text.

Rebuilding wording through the supplied Python authoring scripts requires the declared URW Base35 fonts; rebuilding the explanatory guide also requires Open Sans. Custom lettering, bars, pin shapes and collegiate forms come from the supplied vector construction code. A clearer original scan, printer's artwork or contemporary specification could justify future glyph corrections; this kit makes the current evidence and substitutions explicit.
