# IUS reference audit

## Main conclusion

Use the original photographed print as the primary geometry reference. The earlier generated raster versions are substantially heavier, more condensed, and have inconsistent bar lengths. They can be supplied as separate adaptations, but should not silently replace the historical print construction.

## Print construction

The brochure and letterhead show three uppercase sans rows aligned at the right. Orange bars sit below each row. Each bar is beveled at its left edge: its upper edge begins farther left than its lower edge. The bars extend approximately 0.5-0.7 cap heights beyond the lettering on the right. Suggested normalized drawing: width 1000, cap height 107, text right 934; word widths approximately 690/880/640; bar starts 0/174/338; row pitch 202-210; bar thickness 43-46; gap below letters 20-25. These are reconstruction estimates, not historical specifications.

The I/N stem appears about 0.18-0.21 cap heights including image blur. Nimbus Sans Bold is defensible; Nimbus Sans Regular appears too light. The R has a gently curved lower kick. Exact historical face is unconfirmed. The normal-width font is preferable to the condensed extra-bold raster interpretations.

## Pin

The outer body is a rectangle with its lower-left corner removed: normalized points (0,0), (1,0), (1,1), (.39,1), (0,.33), with slightly softened corners. The rectangle aspect is visually around 1.35:1 but uncalibrated. Black letters have their own silver metal surrounds. Burgundy bars have silver metal edges. Orange is the infill and silver the outer rim. The earlier generated board lost the metal around the lettering and displaced the left chamfer.

## Date and provenance

The invitation in IMG_7950.jpeg visibly says JUNE 8, 1988. This provides direct evidence for use of the mark in 1988; it does not identify the design date. The exhibition label refers to Interior University Society fonds and accession 1992.2. That accession date must not be conflated with creation of the identity.

## Remaining uncertainty

No scale, ink specification, type specification or original mechanical artwork appears in the references. Exact physical dimensions, CMYK/Pantone colors, original fonts, small form copy, covered text and sweatshirt lettering cannot be authenticated. Recommended clear space, minimum sizes, monochrome variants and reverse variants are new practical guidelines unless separately documented.

## Analysis images

The crops and projective rectifications alongside this audit are inspection aids only. They must not be embedded in production SVG masters or presented as vector reconstructions. Rectification uses estimated page geometry and is not a dimensional measurement.
