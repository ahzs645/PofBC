# Campaign lettering: font audit and corrections

## What was checked

The audit compares the original supplied photographs, rather than the previous clean-up images, with locally available font outlines at equal ink heights. It reviews the Northern U button, the YES button, the yellow bumper sticker and the toll-free labels. The six numbered PNG boards record the comparisons and the final before/after result.

The boards retain each candidate's natural proportions before a separate width-fit test where useful. Matching a word's width is not evidence that the original font has been identified. The source photographs include oblique perspective, curved button faces, print spread, blur, ageing and reflections from display-case glass. No exact historical font is authenticated by this audit.

## Changes implemented

### Northern U button

The word Northern remains based on URW Bookman Demi. C059 Bold, Nimbus Roman Bold, Liberation Serif Bold, P052 Bold, DejaVu Serif Bold and Noto Serif Bold were also compared. Bookman remains a plausible local substitute, but its t, r and e are not established exact matches. The source is too small and blurred to resolve those terminal details confidently.

The large U was the clearer mismatch. The automatic Bookman capital U had a more conventional rounded bowl and different top serif extensions. It has been replaced with one deliberately constructed cubic path: nearly equal stem weights, leftward top serifs, a broader and flatter lower bowl, and a right stem ending directly in that bowl without a lowercase exit foot. This is a source-led letterform reconstruction; it is not claimed to identify the original font or prove whether the original designer adapted a capital or lowercase form.

The new large-U box is x=340, y=507, width=320, height=280 in the 1000-square master.

### YES button

The top SAY YES TO remains Nimbus Roman Bold as a substitute. Its ink box changed from 642 x 104 to 615 x 113, with the same lower baseline at y=307. The width-to-height ratio is about 11.8% smaller, producing a more condensed appearance while retaining the overall span visible within the circular face. Condensation is a reconstruction adjustment, not a newly identified font.

The custom NORTHERN had conspicuous chamfered O and R corners. The source shows rounded squared forms. Both outside and counter corners now use cubic curves. This is compatible with the broad appearance of extended square sans lettering; the word alone does not distinguish a specific Microgramma/Eurostile cut or another related face.

The custom collegiate INTERIOR and the separately placed Nimbus Roman Bold UNIVERSITY arc remain. Times-like bold serif shapes are a reasonable working substitute for the latter; the source does not support declaring a precise original digital font.

### Toll-free label

CALL / TOLL / FREE and 1-800-663-3219 were changed from Nimbus Roman Bold to URW Bookman Demi. The source has rounder, heavier, more slab-like letters and numerals than the initial Times-like interpretation. Bookman is the closer local substitution in the comparison, especially for the wide label letters and rounded numerals. The fitted boxes, historical wording, colours and frame remain.

The society name remains Nimbus Sans Regular. The historical telephone number is archival source text and is not verified as a current contact.

### Bumper sticker

The condensed main line retains Nimbus Sans Narrow Bold. The source R has a curved, nearly vertical leg that is closer to the Nimbus Sans family than the straight diagonal R of Liberation Sans Bold. The supplied artwork still uses an explicitly fitted condensed span; this is not proof that the original typeface was Nimbus Sans Narrow.

The italic introduction retains Nimbus Roman Bold Italic after comparison with Nimbus Roman Italic, Liberation Serif Italic/Bold Italic, Bookman Demi Italic, C059 Bold Italic and P052 Italic. The alternatives did not establish a sufficiently clear improvement across all visible details to justify a speculative replacement. Its exact original face and weight remain unresolved.

## Candidate families outside the installed comparison

Souvenir, Cooper, Windsor and Clarendon remain possible families to investigate if a sharper scan or original typesetting record becomes available. They are not identifications. Monotype's ITC Souvenir field guide itself groups Bookman, Cooper and Windsor among related alternatives, reinforcing the need to check diagnostic glyphs rather than assign a name from a general impression: https://www.myfonts.com/a/font/content/font-field-guide/souvenir-font-itc/
