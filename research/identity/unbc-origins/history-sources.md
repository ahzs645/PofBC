# UNBC origins: history evidence

Reviewed 19 November 2026. Published data: [`unbcHistoryData.js`](../../../src/gallery/unbcHistoryData.js).

## Interpretation

“The university that never was?” is an editorial question about the campaign wording visible in the supplied material. It is not a historical quotation or a claim that an institution formally named Interior University existed. IUS was the advocacy society; the statutory university is UNBC. Do not encode this connection as a corporate rename or failed university.

The reconstructed artwork supplies visual interpretation, not independent proof of incorporation, government decisions, original typeface names, design dates or an officially adopted proposed university name. Follow the separate asset manifest for each reconstruction’s status and limitations. Historical originals and contemporary personal/donor material are not reproduced by this evidence note.

## Deliberate limits

- No claim of a legally adopted “Interior University” name or formal IUS-to-UNBC rename.
- No exact date assigned to an undated campaign object from its appearance alone.
- No claim that the campaign stopped on the society’s registry dissolution date.
- Foundation support is an intended role described in parliamentary debate, not an inferred transfer of the society’s legal identity or assets.
- Source-derived prose is concise and distributed among the contemporary notices, legislative record, institutional accounts and Coates’s firsthand retrospective. There are no extended quotations or reproduced supporter lists.
