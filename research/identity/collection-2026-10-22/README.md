# PofBC identity artwork collection

**Acquisition date: 22 October 2026**

This pass collected **110 artwork files** for **68 previously uncollected organizations**. The inventory accounts for the 183 organizations in the supplied collecting list. Five already had gallery collections; those existing artworks were not duplicated in this package.

## Coverage

| Group | Listed | Newly covered | Already collected | Still wanted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Current Crown corporations | 33 | 28 | 4 | 1 |
| Current health organizations | 7 | 7 | 0 | 0 |
| Former Crown corporations | 29 | 22 | 1 | 6 |
| Former health organizations | 114 | 11 | 0 | 103 |

The counts include one organization, Urban Transit Authority, represented only by an attributed third-party reconstruction. Contemporary original artwork for it remains wanted. Every other newly covered organization has supplied artwork or a published/archival specimen. Four selected files in total are explicitly classified as third-party reconstructions or unverified renditions: the UTA image, two Expo 86 SVGs, and a Forest Renewal BC SVG.

The package contains 35 SVG files, 6 EPS files, 57 PNG files, and 12 JPG/JPEG files. Of those, 37 are pure-vector files recovered from sources or supplied with the brief; three further pure-vector files are third-party renditions. One SVG, the complete North Okanagan mark, contains its original low-resolution bitmap wordmark. Separate generated PNG previews are additional to the 110 selected artwork files.

## How to use the collection

Open `index.html` after extracting the ZIP. Search by organization, filter the four groups, and switch to “Still wanted” to review gaps. Each artwork card offers the actual file and its public source. The `identity-collected/` folders contain the same selected SVG, EPS and image files used in the repository. `inventory.json` preserves source URLs, specimen dates, methods, and SHA-256 checksums.

## Evidence and format decisions

- A report or letter date establishes a dated sighting. It does not establish a logo’s adoption date or entire period of use.
- Several formats can be copies of one design. File counts are not counts of distinct logos or historical generations.
- SVG files containing embedded bitmap artwork are not classified as pure vectors. BC Housing’s website SVG was such a wrapper; its embedded PNG was recovered losslessly.
- Northern Health’s four supplied SVGs contain outlined lettering and vector paths. The supplied EPS files were UNBC logos, and the supplied PDF was an ID-card photograph. Only the four Northern Health logo SVGs are included; ID layouts, personal photographs and fonts are excluded.
- Native PDF paths were retained where possible. Scanned reports and photographs remain images. Existing third-party redraws, if included, are identified as reconstructions.
- PRHC’s use of BC Housing branding was not treated as proof of a separate PRHC logo. Other parent and successor brands were likewise not silently substituted.
- An unresolved record means the pass did not recover attributable artwork. It does not establish that the organization never had a mark.
