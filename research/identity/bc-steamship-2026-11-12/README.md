# British Columbia Steamship Company (1975) Ltd.: identity leads

From a research summary supplied on 12 November 2026. Dated events from it are in
`research/atlas/events-power-steamship-2026-11-12.json`; the company's corrected years (operating
assets sold November 1988, renamed Victoria Line Ltd. in 1994, legal end untraced rather than 1992)
are in `research/history/bodies.json`.

## What is held

- **Circular company emblem** (Union Jack over stacked waves) on a 1987 casino token
  ([Numista 130778](https://en.numista.com/130778)); the gallery's only specimen. It dates the token,
  not the emblem's introduction, colours or whether the surrounding lettering belongs to the logo.

## Identities seen but not collected

- **Princess Marguerite service mark**: ship and Victoria skyline with "The Last of the Great
  Romantics", and the cover's three-band title lettering, on a brochure dated 1983 whose small print
  names the company. Seen only in a sale listing's photographs
  ([listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/147644435138)). A service identity, to be catalogued apart
  from the company emblem.
- **1975 funnel livery**: a modified Union Jack in red, blue and white
  ([The Trainmaster, June 1975, printed p. 8](https://pnwc-nrhs.org/Trainmaster1975/TM-1975-06.pdf);
  [Canadian Rail, June 1976](https://exporail.org/canrail/canadian_rail_1962_1989/canadian-rail-293-1976.pdf)).
  Colour placement needs a clear photograph or drawing.
