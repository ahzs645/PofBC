## Legislative Library catalogue checks

Searches used the Library’s [DSpace discovery API](https://digitalcollectionsrepository.llbc.leg.bc.ca/server/api/discover/search/objects). Full-text searches were followed by title and issue-year filters, item metadata and inspection of the ORIGINAL bundle PDFs. Library repository copies usually prepend a bibliographic sheet, so a cover can be PDF page 2. Handles below identify records independently of storage filenames.

- A title filter for “Geological Survey Branch” retrieved the [1990–91 project inventory](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/70474), [1991–92 plan](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/67266), [1992–93 plan](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/67432), [1992–93 project inventory](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/67377) and [1992 style guide](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/59350). Each cover carries the circular seal. The [1987–88 inventory](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/71157) uses a provincial signature on its title page. These observations leave the adoption date open.
- For the linocut, the Library’s 1985 annual report and March 2011 publication provided dated pictures. Searching for the B.C.L.A. Bulletin, the association's name and the 1955 issue yielded Library history and secondary mentions. The original issue was not recovered in this search. The 1985 report gives the month, volume and number.
- Searches for hunting regulations by title and for Fish and Wildlife with the 1973 issue-year filter yielded modern regulation records, government accounts and other reports. The [Province’s historic regulations series](https://a100.gov.bc.ca/pub/acat/public/viewReport.do?reportId=22560) provided the actual 1972–73 cover. For Lands the government FOI release supplied the dated fax. Library results for PEP in 1997 include [Flood Precautions for Homeowners](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/71022). That title alone does not identify its artwork.
- [Draft #86](https://github.com/ahzs645/PofBC/pull/86) handles Travel Industry, the Departmental Logos rename, BC Youth and the GCPE footer. The other open drafts covered rockers (#84), Police College (#85), BC Timber Sales (#87), the Oil and Gas Commission (#88), the Justice Institute (#89) and liquor administration (#91). The Geological Survey and Library artwork was absent from those drafts.
