# Evidence carried over from PR #45

PR [#45](https://github.com/ahzs645/PofBC/pull/45) (3 November 2026, branch
`codex/bc-place-historical-vector-20261103`) redrew the same British Columbia Place Ltd. signature
from the same grand-opening programme as this package, and was closed as superseded by it. Its
artwork is not carried over; two dated observations are, because nothing here recorded them.

## Taken into the gallery

**22 September 1982, Gulf Islands Driftwood, printed page 6.** A British Columbia Place
corporate advertisement headed “Already Paying Its Way” carries the same outlined b/c/p monogram
and staggered three-line wordmark in its lower-right signature, and calls the company a provincial
Crown corporation. PR #45 inspected the downloaded issue
(<https://saltspringarchives.com/driftwood/1982/v23n38p1Sept22-1982.pdf>, 18,806,100 bytes,
SHA-256 `0820b303f9a139c8a9fc7cce70594551cc07dafbb7899b0da0a635f3fdaede5c`, PDF page 6, embedded
image xref 22, crop 1440,2490–2080,2810 of 2264 × 3119).

It is recorded on `bc-place-1983-rebuilt` as an `alsoSeen` sighting and in its `dateNote`:
documented use **by** that date, not the first use. The builder
(`scripts/build-collected-marks.mjs`) now keeps a register's own `alsoSeen` sightings ahead of
those it derives from superseded files.

## Recorded here only

**1984, a circular, reversed variant.** PR #45 found a 1984 newsletter header (a museum image,
<https://openmovportal.ca/argus/final/ViewImage.aspx?template=Image&field=DerivedIma&hash=7a0e5571fef0c4a25f1e0ea422510259&width=max&height=max&r=-1735779844&lang=en-CA>,
145,180 bytes, SHA-256 `5f872d682c8d554cf2ef4ea785a4d9fd57799b5404eb9163ed15e8ba7d4125ea`, crop
130,135–825,340 of 948 × 1200) showing a circular, reversed arrangement. That is a different
design from this package's signature, so it is not a sighting of it. It is left as a lead for a
separate gallery entry, should its artwork be acquired.

The evidence crops and the comparison board stay on the PR's branch and are not copied here.
