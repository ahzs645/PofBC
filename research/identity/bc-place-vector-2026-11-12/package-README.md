# British Columbia Place - 1983 vector reconstruction

## What this is

A clean reconstruction of the blue interlocking symbol and the three-line
"british columbia place" wordmark visible on page 4 of the supplied
2023-F0003-24.pdf. The programme cover identifies the Grand Opening Gala as
June 19, 1983. The supplied PDF contains scanned page images, not vector artwork.

## Files

- BC_Place_1983_Reconstructed.svg: primary editable full-colour logo.
- BC_Place_1983_Vector.pdf: the same artwork as genuine vector paths.
- BC_Place_1983_Symbol.svg: blue symbol alone.

## Construction

The symbol was constructed geometrically using equal circular bowls on an
equilateral centre grid, two straight stems, a diagonal opening, and a fine
offset outline. The fine outline is expanded to filled shapes. Curves are
smooth cubic Beziers. This is not an automatic pixel-contour trace.

Every wordmark letter is an individually editable outline. The staggered lines,
open b/p/a forms, diagonal e and the continuous h/l shared stem are retained.
The paths remove speckling, print damage and uneven scan edges.

The full logo contains 26 named closed paths in two editable groups.
There are no embedded images, live text, font dependencies, filters, masks,
clipping paths or external assets. SVG viewBox: 0 0 700 330.

## Colour and typography limits

Blue: #3093D2, selected from the median colour of interior blue pixels in the
supplied scan. Black: #111111. These are reconstruction colours, not verified
Pantone or original CMYK specifications. Black/white adaptations are included
for convenience and are not claimed to be documented historical variants.

The wordmark is strongly consistent with the ITC Bauhaus family. The exact
original weight and any original modifications are unconfirmed. The artwork
uses custom reconstructed paths and does not embed or redistribute a font.
Family reference: https://www.myfonts.com/collections/bauhaus-font-itc

The geometry was checked visually against the registered source. Paper colour,
scan softness, photographic distortion and small printing irregularities are
not reproduced. Treat this as a reconstructed historical logo, not an official
production master or a claim of exact original specifications.
