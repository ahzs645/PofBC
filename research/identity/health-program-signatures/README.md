# Provincial health signatures

The Ministry of Health Services used the provincial arms beside blue and red sans-serif program names. **BCHealthCare** appears on the January 23, 2003 cover of the request for expressions of interest for the Abbotsford Hospital and Cancer Centre. The cover separately credits the ministry and its partners. [Legislative Library copy, PDF p. 2](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/307972)

**BCPharmaCare** appears on the front panel of the ministry’s 2003 *Your new Fair PharmaCare program* brochure. An April 7, 2005 ministry newsletter also uses it in the masthead. The supplied button places “Register for Fair PharmaCare” below the signature and frames the whole application. The standalone brochure signature establishes where the logo ends. [2003 brochure, PDF p. 2](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/301527) · [2005 newsletter, PDF p. 2](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/268865)

PharmaCare began in 1974. The Fair PharmaCare plan introduced income-based coverage in May 2003. The dates of the publications establish uses of the artwork. Its adoption and retirement dates remain open. [Ministry overview, February 2003](https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/archive/2001-2005/2003HSER0013-000192-Attachment2.htm) · [Ministry update, December 2004](https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/archive/2001-2005/2004HSER0087-001049.htm)

## Artwork

The gallery’s **Programs, services and subsidiaries** section holds the two signatures together. Each entry retains its own program owner. The collection links to the 2003 ministry in the atlas and to PofBC’s crest generator, where the provincial arms can be explored independently.

Both SVGs retain the outlines of the provincial arms and every letter from their respective documents. The source PDFs produce the same RGB fills, blue `#0060A9` and red `#EF3D42`. The visible signature is isolated from the cover artwork on a transparent canvas. The source register pins the full archival PDFs and SVG downloads by SHA-256.

At ten pixels per source point, ink-mask intersection over union against each corresponding original page crop measured 99.994% for BCHealthCare and 99.984% for BCPharmaCare (white ground, any RGB channel below 160). Both wordmarks are outlines in the source. The publications do not identify their typeface.
