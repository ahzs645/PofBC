# Rural Health Services Research Network of BC

## Network history

- On July 18, 2005, UNBC announced the BC Rural and Remote Health Research Network. UNBC hosted the network, and the Michael Smith Foundation for Health Research funded it through 2010. [UNBC launch announcement](https://www.unbc.ca/releases/2005/07-18rural), [Michael Smith Health Research BC award record](https://www.healthresearchbc.ca/award/bc-rural-and-remote-health-research-network/)
- The predecessor used a landscape with a sun, blue-green mountain skyline, a house and the BCRRHRN initials. The 2008 conference program embeds the 266 × 140 pixel badge as an image on PDF page 1. The archived website referenced a GIF at the same dimensions. [2008 conference program](https://www.unbc.ca/sites/default/files/stories/bcrrhrn_conference_program.pdf#page=1), [archived predecessor website](https://web.archive.org/web/20080505023636/http://www.bcrrhrn.ca/)
- In May 2010, Dr. Stefan Grzybowski re-established the network as the RHSRNbc after it secured new funding. UBC’s Department of Family Practice houses the network, with RCCbc support through the Joint Standing Committee on Rural Issues. [UBC network history](https://rhsrnbc.med.ubc.ca/about-rhsrnbc/), [RCCbc initiative history](https://rccbc.ca/initiatives/rhsrnbc/)

The river forms the S in the RHSRNbc initials. The blue serif and white sans-serif treatments share the mountain silhouette and river but have different lettering. Both spell out the network name in their full-name layouts. The dates record appearances in publications. The designer and introduction dates remain open.

Earlier newsletter layouts pair the white sans-serif emblem with heavier full-name lettering. The May 2013 sidebar also adds the web address. The September 2019 header places the emblem and bold name on a navy square. [May 2013 newsletter, printed p. 2](https://med-fom-rhsrnbc.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2018/11/May20132.pdf), [September 2019 newsletter, p. 1](https://med-fom-rhsrnbc.sites.olt.ubc.ca/files/2023/09/september-2019.pdf#page=1)

The blue full-name badge held in the gallery comes from PDF page 15 of the November 2013 UNBC conference program, where it is embedded as a 362 × 362 pixel image. The smaller RCCbc web version is selectable on the same design. The UBC media library also carries a [blue emblem PNG](https://rhsrnbc.med.ubc.ca/files/2018/10/Logo-no-text.png) and a [white emblem on grey JPEG](https://rhsrnbc.med.ubc.ca/files/2015/02/rhsrnbc-logo.jpeg). [2013 conference program, printed p. 11](https://www.unbc.ca/sites/default/files/sections/unassigned/2013fullconferenceprogramoct11finalforprint.pdf#page=15), [RCCbc web badge](https://rccbc.ca/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/logo-rhsrnbc@1x.png)

No native vector of the RHSRNbc badge was verified in local holdings, public UBC or RCCbc media, the newsletter archive, or the checked conference programs, proceedings and application forms. The identified badges in those PDFs are embedded images. The archived 2012 site used a Flash slideshow in its header. The recovered SWF is the generic Flashmo player and references an external photo list that could not be recovered.
