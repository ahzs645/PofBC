# ResWest

ResWest was the privately operated, provincewide reservations service promoted by the Province and Expo 86. The gallery collection (`?view=gallery&logos=reswest`) sits under Tourism and destination management and links to Expo 86 Corporation through the identity register. The advertisement shows how the three identities appeared together in promotional material.

## Service history

- **1984, as reported in 1985.** Tourism Minister Claude Richmond told the Legislative Assembly in June 1985 that the Province had franchised the ResWest reservation system the previous year. He described how it listed bed-and-breakfast accommodation through associations. [B.C. Hansard, June 18, 1985, pp. 6709–6710](https://lims.leg.bc.ca/hdms/file/Debates/33rd3rd/33p_03s_850618p.htm#06709)
- **1985.** Expo 86 Corporation’s U.S. brochure identifies ResWest as the fair’s official accommodation agency and advertises a single $5 charge for B.C. reservations. Its back panel places the ResWest badge beside the Expo and provincial artwork. [Expo 86 Corporation, *The 1986 World Exposition*, PDF pp. 3–4](https://www.worldsfairphotos.com/expo86/booklets/1985-brochure-us.pdf#page=3)
- **April 1986.** Richmond told the Assembly that ResWest had been in place for about a year and was handling roughly 1,000 to 1,200 room nights per day. The service also took bookings for the Host Home program. A weekly coordination committee included the hotel and motel industry, the Ministry of Tourism, Expo and ResWest. A member raised the B.C. Motels, Resorts and Trailer Parks Association’s request for a free referral alternative. [B.C. Hansard, April 29, 1986, pp. 8015–8016](https://lims.leg.bc.ca/hdms/file/Debates/33rd4th/33p_04s_860429p.htm#08015)
- **June 1986.** *The Washington Post* identified Jon DeWest as the head of ResWest. DeWest told the paper the service represented hotels, motels, campgrounds, bed-and-breakfasts and four cruise ships docked in Vancouver for the fair. [The Washington Post, June 1, 1986](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/travel/1986/06/01/fearless-traveler/c3030fa1-5e64-4718-a2ef-26235fcce20f/)
- **June 1986.** Richmond described ResWest as the successful bidder for a provincewide system run by the private sector, with the Province agreeing to promote it in tourism literature. He said the intention was for the service to continue after the fair. The debate gives a proposed promotional period. The service’s last operating date remains unknown. [B.C. Hansard, June 17, 1986, p. 8780](https://lims.leg.bc.ca/hdms/file/Debates/33rd4th/33p_04s_860617p.htm#08780)

The advertisement offered to book hotels, motels, bed-and-breakfast homes, resorts and car rentals through one computerized service. Its mail-in coupon accompanied the telephone offer. The U.S. brochure and colour ad show the same Vancouver number and $5 charge, giving the undated ad a connection to the dated material beyond the logo itself.

## Gallery artwork

The [colour reconstruction](../../../public/identity-collected/reswest/reswest-badge-colour.svg) uses hand-drawn Bézier outlines for the lettering, rule and badge field. Its proportions follow the colour screenshot. The green `#5ca33f` and blue `#4f649f` approximate median RGB samples from the two printed fields. The warm white approximates the scan’s light lettering. The [one-colour reconstruction](../../../public/identity-collected/reswest/reswest-badge-one-colour.svg) uses the same outlines and follows the reversed lettering and rule in the 1985 brochure. The title and description describe the provenance within each SVG. Both files consist of vector geometry, with no bitmap, external resource or font dependency.

The gallery groups both reconstructions with two selectable raster crops under `reswest-badge`. The colour crop uses pixel bounds `(352, 382, 513, 471)` in the supplied screenshot. The one-colour crop uses `(360, 905, 412, 935)` in the native JPEG of the Expo brochure’s back panel. These are zero-based bounds measured from the top left, with exclusive right and bottom edges. The full advertisement retains the co-branding and booking form. The [gallery records](../../../src/gallery/resWest.js) hold the source locators and checksums. The generated dark previews adjust the display palette for the page, while the downloadable source-based SVGs use their documented reconstruction palettes.
