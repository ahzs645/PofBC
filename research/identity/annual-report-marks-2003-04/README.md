# Report-cover signatures

The Forest Practices Board published its green trees, brown ground and blue stream with a three-line name on both covers of its 2003 annual report. The back cover of PHSA’s January 2004 progress report repeats the map signature from its front cover. The SVG detail omits the two-line vision statement beneath it. These dates document use. [Board annual report, front cover](https://www.bcfpb.ca/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/FPB-2003-Annual-Report_0.pdf) · [PHSA progress report](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/317802)

The SVGs retain the original PDF paths and their transforms. The comparison renders each SVG at four pixels per PDF point and compares its alpha mask above 50% with the original page at the same coordinates. The source mask uses Euclidean RGB distance greater than 85 from the upper-left background pixel. The PHSA covers have a pale gradient. Antialiasing, that gradient and the choice of threshold affect the figures.
