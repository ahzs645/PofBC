# Additional B.C. initiatives

## Programs documented alongside BC Youth

On May 12, 1989, Minister Responsible for Youth Stan Hagen described the Youth Advisory Council’s grants, its guide *The Next Step*, and three separately administered programs. The initiatives register now includes those three programs. Each links to the ministry or ministries responsible during the period. [Legislative debates, pp. 6749–50](https://lims.leg.bc.ca/hdms/file/Debates/34th3rd/34p_03s_890512a.htm#06749)

| Initiative | What the contemporary record establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Challenge ’89 | The province advertised wage sharing for employers offering new summer jobs. The notice names the Ministry of Advanced Education and Job Training and directs employers to Job Training Area Offices. In May Hagen reported both federal and provincial contributions. His approximately 6,000 jobs figure was an estimate. [March 29 advertisement, p. A22](https://www.saltspringarchives.com/driftwood/1989/v30n13March29-1989A.pdf#page=22) |
| Passport to Apprenticeship | The ministries of Education and Advanced Education and Job Training jointly announced the program in February 1989. Education later counted 90 participating secondary students in 1990/91. [Ministry of Education annual report, printed p. 34](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED369660.pdf#page=33) |
| Environment Youth Corps | Environment led the provincial training and employment program. The Ombudsman identified Social Services and Housing as a co-funder and reported over 2,000 participants and about 350 projects by the 1990 report. [1990 Ombudsman annual report, pp. 47–48](https://bcombudsperson.ca/assets/media/1990-Annual-Report-of-the-Ombudsman-web.pdf#page=48) |

The March 29 advertisement is reproduced as a crop from the original scan. The PDF page was rendered at a longest edge of 2,600 pixels, then cropped at x=1,179, y=1,191 to 691 × 1,331 pixels. The second printing in *Northwest Prospector* also shows the program emblem and ministry imprint. [May/June 1989, printed p. 05, PDF p. 76](https://propertyfile.gov.bc.ca/reports/PF016366.pdf#page=76)

## Programs already represented elsewhere in the gallery

The gallery’s campaign and service-brand subjects were compared with the initiatives index. These five have dated evidence identifying the program and the public body responsible, and can be researched independently of the youth programs.

| Gallery subject | Dated program evidence and organizational connection |
| --- | --- |
| Power Smart | BC Hydro launched its electricity conservation program in 1989. The operator and artwork are already catalogued under BC Hydro and Power Smart. [BC Hydro’s corporate chronology](https://www.bchydro.com/news/press_centre/news_releases/2012/affordable_hydro_power.html) |
| AirCare | The Lower Mainland vehicle emissions program began in 1992. The Ministry of Environment announced the end of light-duty passenger vehicle testing on December 31, 2014. The gallery identifies TransLink and its subsidiary Pacific Vehicle Testing Technologies in the service’s operation. [Provincial bulletin, December 2014](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2014ENV0112-001944) |
| WorkBC | The Ministry of Social Development launched the Employment Program of BC through WorkBC centres on April 2, 2012. That date establishes the program and centre opening. The WorkBC brand also appears on other services. [Provincial launch announcement](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2012SD0004-000410) |
| PreparedBC | Emergency Management BC developed the public education campaign within the Ministry of Justice. The ministry announced the website and household guides in May 2015. [Ministry briefing note](https://docs.openinfo.gov.bc.ca/Response_Package_JAG-2015-52545.pdf) · [May 2015 launch notice](https://archive.news.gov.bc.ca/releases/news_releases_2013-2017/2015jag0109-000607.htm) |
| StrongerBC | Premier John Horgan and Finance Minister Carole James released the COVID-19 economic recovery plan on September 17, 2020. The gallery holds standalone and Province-endorsed campaign marks. [Provincial announcement](https://news.gov.bc.ca/releases/2020PREM0052-001780) |
