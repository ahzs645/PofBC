# Regional district and municipal logo coverage

25 November 2026 · [Municipal register](http://127.0.0.1:5179/dev/data?view=gallery&coverage=1&identity-family=municipalities)

This report records the baseline before the [regional district artwork acquisition](../regional-districts-2026-11-25/README.md).

## Regional district artwork

The gallery has district-owned marks for **3 of B.C.’s 27 regional districts**. One has source vectors, two have dated raster specimens, and 24 have no catalogued corporate artwork. I checked the gallery’s owner-resolved records, including the tourism shelves, and searched the local holdings by district names and former names. [Provincial regional district dataset](https://open.canada.ca/data/dataset/d1aff64e-dbfe-45a6-af97-582b7f6418b9)

- **Metro Vancouver Regional District** holds the corporate wordmark and the full-colour signature with tagline. Both are native-path extractions from page 1 of Metro Vancouver’s logo guidelines. The separately registered Regional Parks and Transit Police marks are service identities. [Gallery record](http://127.0.0.1:5179/dev/data?view=gallery&subject=metro-vancouver) · [Official logo guidelines](https://metrovancouver.org/media-room/Documents/metro-logo-guidelines.pdf#page=1)
- **Regional District of Fraser-Fort George** holds colour and white versions of its old triangular signature, from sponsor strips in the 2012 and 2015 Northern B.C. travel guides. The district’s 2025–2026 Corporate Workplan shows a different mountain signature. Its current signature and the original artwork for the older versions are open. [Gallery record](http://127.0.0.1:5179/dev/data?view=gallery&subject=regional-district-fraser-fort-george) · [2012 guide, p. 19](https://digitalcollectionsrepository.llbc.leg.bc.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/a30e5117-4e75-43f6-b52e-017cdba837e7/content#page=19) · [2015 guide, p. 18](https://digitalcollectionsrepository.llbc.leg.bc.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/2704bdf5-89c5-4778-a978-ba04e1ab02dd/content#page=18) · [Corporate Workplan](https://www.rdffg.ca/media/file/corporate-workplan-2025-2026)
- **Regional District of Bulkley-Nechako** holds a raster crop of the circular seal from a 2015 travel guide. The district still uses the same seal design in its 2025 public hearing package. A clean source master is open. [Gallery record](http://127.0.0.1:5179/dev/data?view=gallery&subject=regional-district-bulkley-nechako) · [2015 guide, p. 52](https://digitalcollectionsrepository.llbc.leg.bc.ca/server/api/core/bitstreams/2704bdf5-89c5-4778-a978-ba04e1ab02dd/content#page=52) · [District hearing package](https://www.rdbn.bc.ca/application/files/8717/5158/2142/RZ_F-02-25_BL2077_Public_Information_Package_2.pdf#page=1)

## Municipal artwork and membership

The register has accepted artwork for **160 of 161 municipalities**. Across all 162 register entries, 73 have source vectors, 87 have rasters only, and two lack a gallery mark. The provincial municipality IDs and regional district assignments were checked against the live provincial boundary layer. The shíshálh Nation Government District is an additional register entry and has a representative on the Sunshine Coast Regional District board. Northern Rockies is a regional municipality with no separate regional district. Central Coast has zero incorporated municipal members. The roster below covers municipalities and the shíshálh register entry. Regional boards also include electoral areas and, in some districts, Treaty First Nations. [Provincial municipality layer](https://delivery.maps.gov.bc.ca/arcgis/rest/services/mpcm/bcgwpub/MapServer/3) · [Sunshine Coast board](https://www.scrd.ca/news/new-and-returning-sunshine-coast-regional-district-board-take-oath-of-office/) · [Central Coast overview](https://www.ccrd.ca/our-region/about-us/) · [Metro Vancouver governance](https://metrovancouver.org/boards/governance-structure)

- **Okanagan Falls** is the only incorporated municipality with no gallery mark. It incorporated on 6 November 2026. Its interim site and early council agenda covers have yet to show an adopted logo. [Gallery record](http://127.0.0.1:5179/dev/data?view=gallery&subject=municipality-okanagan-falls) · [B.C. Gazette](https://www.bclaws.gov.bc.ca/civix/document/id/bcgaz1/bcgaz1/470736345) · [District website](https://okanaganfalls.ca/)
- **shíshálh Nation Government District** has no standalone gallery entry for its artwork. The local research files document the district’s use of the same crest as shíshálh Nation on a district business licence application. An exclusive district design remains unconfirmed. [Local evidence review](../evidence-2026-11-21/notes/municipal-island-details.md#1-shíshálh-nation-government-district-actual-district-use-of-shared-artwork-is-visually-documented) · [District form, p. 1](https://shishalh.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/sNGD-Business-License-Form.pdf#page=1)

## Documented identity history and source leads

The gallery explicitly tags 37 municipal specimens across 21 municipalities as historical. That count describes the tagged holdings, and many municipal design histories still have open dates or versions. A publication dates a specimen’s appearance. The institutional incorporation and renaming dates below come from governance records.

| Metro Vancouver | Incorporated as the Regional District of Fraser-Burrard in 1967 and renamed Greater Vancouver Regional District in 1968. Metro Vancouver was already in use as the brand when the legal name changed in 2017. The 2025 brand book illustrates marks dated pre-2004, 2005, 2009, 2011, 2017 and 2021. The two earlier GVRD marks and the 2009 and 2011 lockups are still open as standalone artwork.
