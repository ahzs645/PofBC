## What was checked

The inventory follows the running project’s gallery collection data and includes the source versions attached to each collected entry. The online pass attempted 1,655 distinct URLs, including publisher sites, archived sources, and targeted brand and publication searches. Automated screening covered 3,454 selected pages across 684 retrieved PDFs. I compared accepted results visually to the gallery specimens and checked the relevant PDF objects for paths, text, shadings and bitmap images or masks.

Coverage varies by subject. For PDFs, screening usually focused on the cover, opening and closing pages, and any page cited by the gallery. Some archive and publisher requests failed. “No source vector verified” records the outcome of this pass.

A vector grouped with a raster can represent another printing or colourway. The inventory records the existing file and the source separately from the new online findings. Gallery links for archived images retained alongside a reconstruction may open the reconstruction first. The inventory names the exact raster file.
