# BC — The Education Province

The October 13, 1998 issue of *The Similkameen News Leader* shows a circular campaign badge on page 9. The Ministry of Advanced Education, Training and Technology used a horizontal companion wordmark in its fiscal 1999/2000 annual report. The March 14, 2001 throne speech and March 20 debate document the slogan in speech. The design and campaign dates are sightings. A designer, launch date and retirement date remain unconfirmed.
