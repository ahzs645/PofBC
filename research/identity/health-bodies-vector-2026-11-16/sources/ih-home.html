Our Vision, Mission and Values

Our vision is health and well-being for all, by working together to improve quality of life for individuals and communities, inspired by innovation and partnership. Through living out our values – Quality, Integrity, Compassion, Safety – we are committed to providing the highest quality care and services to those in the southern B.C. Interior.

Patients & Visitors

We want to make it easy for you to plan your hospital visit. Patients can access our resources for preparation, admission and medication needs and our care teams can also provide information and answer your questions. We have visitor guidelines in place to ensure family and friends can visit patients while respecting hospital rules. You can send well wishes to a patient if you can’t visit them in person.
