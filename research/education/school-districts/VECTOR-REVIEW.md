# School district vector review

Reviewed 25 November 2026. The review covers the 60 current districts in the provincial roster, including every earlier logo already held in their collections. The historical predecessor register has 31 separate district records with no artwork holdings.

Compared the retained artwork with district website assets, local holdings, and candidate brand guides, strategic plans, learning reports and board documents. The search retrieved 844 distinct candidate PDFs across the district checks, after deduplication within each district. Verified imports have native paths and were compared with the retained specimens for artwork, lettering, colour treatment and layout. The catalogue records the source, page, extraction bounds and checksum for each imported PDF specimen.

**29 of 60 current logos now use verified vector geometry as their default.** Source vectors also document alternative colourways and earlier designs. The district records preserve the published image and give each alternate a source and its own artwork file.

The second pass revisited all 37 districts whose default was still raster. It checked interior report and agenda pages, district media archives, letterheads, and provincial climate and compensation filings. Seven SVGs were added across six districts. Langley and Maple Ridge–Pitt Meadows use vectors from district publications. Rocky Mountain, Delta, Richmond and Greater Victoria use verified publication outlines with their own published website colours. The SVG records link both the geometry and colour sources.
