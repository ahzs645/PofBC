# BC Parks crest and Ranger flash

Becoming’s BC Parks case study documents how two pieces in the patch catalogue were worn together. The closest records are the scenic crest (S30-O04) and detached Ranger tab (S27-O02). S27-O04 records a different specimen with RANGER inside a continuous outer edge. The November catalogue audit already flagged the two constructions. Becoming adds a placement rule, a flat drawing and a photograph of the two-piece assembly on a sleeve. ## Patch comparison

| Feature | Becoming’s evidence | Comparison with the collection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Placement | The guide specifies a gap of one outer white frame thickness. The drawing aligns the left and right edges of the two pieces. Read down across the join and the three equal intervals are white border, exposed shirt, white border. | Link S30-O04 and S27-O02 as the components for comparison. Preserve S27-O04 as the integrated version. The photograph documents the pairing, while the drawing supplies the spacing. |
| Lettering | Gold serif capitals follow the arch for BRITISH COLUMBIA. PARKS and RANGER are centred on straight baselines. Both words occupy about 77% of the full patch width. The six letters of RANGER are shorter than the five letters of PARKS and reach almost exactly the same endpoints. | At equal outer widths, PARKS on the S30-O04 preview occupies about 80% and RANGER on S27-O02 about 81%. The guide drawing gives each word a little more room at the sides. The exact patch typeface and letterspacing remain unidentified. |
| Landscape | A white line outlines the scene. Two snowcapped peaks rise behind overlapping, flat green firs. A few broad navy strokes cross the blue water. | The reconstruction has finer branch and ripple detail, a darker mountain mass and a paler sky. The sleeve shows how stitching breaks up these shapes. Each is a reference for a different rendering of the scene. |
| Colour | The crest has its own seven-swatch strip. Approximate digital samples, top to bottom, are gold `#FAB511`, navy `#053573`, blue `#008BC7`, dark green `#205C38`, green `#039147`, light green `#76B041` and white `#FFFFFF`. | The S27-O04 reconstruction uses `#E1BC59` for the lettering, `#172923` for the ground, `#232D3B` for the mountains and `#5BA3ED` for the sky. Its colours describe the specimen reconstruction. The guide has a separate page for the crest palette and a different page for the general brand palette. |

Measurements use the publisher’s 1200 × 1200 flat drawing. The crest is approximately 668 px wide, PARKS spans about 510 px and RANGER about 513 px. Their ink heights are approximately 82 and 73 px. The gap is about 24 px in this export. Local comparisons use the visible patch width of each library preview, excluding transparent padding. Colours above come from the interiors of the seven AVIF swatches. The published guide shows print and digital specifications in small type. A legible original would establish those values more reliably and a thread specification would be needed for an embroidery match.
