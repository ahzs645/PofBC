MINISTERIAL ORDER 253

Ministry Responsible: FORESTS, LANDS, NATURAL RESOURCE OPERATIONS AND RURAL DEVELOPMENT

Statutory Authority: Forest, s. 170

With respect to the Trees Designated Area No. 1 (B.C. Reg. 170/2019), it is ordered that

1. any person who, under the Forest Act, has a discretion to issue a permit or licence is directed to not issue the permit or licence to the extent that it relates to all or part of an area designated in a schedule, and

2. those parts of the rights of a holder of a permit or licence that relate to an area designated in a schedule, are suspended.
