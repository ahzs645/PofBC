MINISTERIAL ORDER 20

Ministry Responsible: HEALTH

Statutory Authority: Health Professions, s. 12 (4)

The notice period for amendments to the Health Professions General Regulation, removing the qualifier limiting the use of naloxone to opioid overdose emergencies "outside hospital settings", is shortened to 24 hours.

MINISTERIAL ORDER 21

Ministry Responsible: HEALTH

Statutory Authority: Health Authorities, s. 7.1

Regional health boards are directed to adjust their policies and practices to ensure that naloxone is available and may be administered in outpatient settings and publicly accessible areas within and adjacent to a hospital, in case of opioid overdose.
