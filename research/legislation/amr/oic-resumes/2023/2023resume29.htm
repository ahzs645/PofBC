Ministerial Orders

MINISTERIAL ORDER 212

Ministry Responsible: FORESTS

Statutory Authority: Land, ss. 97 (1.2) and 103

The registration of described land [H26295 and H30084] are removed from the operation of the Land Title Act.

MINISTERIAL ORDER 213

Ministry Responsible: FINANCE

Statutory Authority: Pay Transparency, s. 10 (1)

Asha Bhat, Assistant Deputy Minister of the Gender Equity Office, is designated as the director of pay transparency.

MINISTERIAL ORDER 214

Ministry Responsible: MUNICIPAL AFFAIRS

Statutory Authority: Provincial Immigration Programs, s. 16 (2) (c)

Effective August 1, 2023, section 3 (a) (iii) of the Provincial Immigration Programs Regulation, B.C. Reg. 20/2017, is amended by striking out "$1 150" and substituting "$1 475".

MINISTERIAL ORDER 215

Ministry Responsible: EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AND CLIMATE READINESS

Statutory Authority: Emergency Program, s. 10

The Little Blue River Fire Evacuation Order, made by MO 205/2023, is repealed.

MINISTERIAL ORDER 216

Ministry Responsible: HEALTH

Statutory Authority: Health Professions, s. 19 (3.2) (b) and (6.2) (a) (ii); Ministry of Health, s. 4 (2)

In respect of the amendments to the bylaws of the College of Chiropractors of British Columbia, as set out in the schedule to the resolution of the board of the College dated June 25, 2023:

• the notice period is shortened to 24 days;

• the amendment comes into force on July 26, 2023.

MINISTERIAL ORDER 217

Ministry Responsible: HEALTH

Statutory Authority: Health Professions, s. 25.05 (4)

Allen Paul Seckel, K.C., is appointed to exercise the powers and perform the duties of the board of each of the listed colleges as it relates to the amalgamation of that college pursuant to section 11 or 12 (as applicable) of the Health Professions Designation and Amalgamation Regulation, B.C. Reg. 270/2008.
