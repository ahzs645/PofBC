ORDER IN COUNCIL 444

Statutory Authority: Constitution, ss. 10, 13 and 14

1. OIC 233/2013 is amended in Appendix A by adding section 27.3, regarding the LeanBC program transferred from the Minister of Finance to the Minister of Small Business and Red Tape Reduction and Minister Responsible for the Liquor Distribution Branch.

2. OIC 233/2013 is amended in Appendix B under the heading "Public Acts" regarding entries for: Fish Protection; Fire Safety; Food and Agricultural Products Classification; Great Bear Rainforest (Forest Management); Local Government; Municipal Replotting; Profits of Criminal Notoriety; Riparian Areas Protection; Sexual Violence and Misconduct Policy; Water; Water User's Communities.

Approved and Ordered June 27, 2016
