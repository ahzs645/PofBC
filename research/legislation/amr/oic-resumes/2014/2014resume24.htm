ORDER IN COUNCIL 368

Statutory Authority: Constitution, ss. 10, 13 and 14

OIC 233/2013 is amended

1. in Appendix A by adding section 16.1 regarding the transfer of British Columbia's participation in the Softwood Lumber Agreement with Canada from the Minister of International Trade and Minister responsible for Asia Pacific Strategy and Multiculturalism to the Minister of Forest, Lands and Natural Resources; and

2. in Appendix B, under the heading "Public Acts"

a) by striking out the entry for Provincial Symbols and Honours Act and substituting as set in the Schedule,

b) by striking out the entry for Survivorship and Presumption of Death Act and substituting as set in the Schedule,

c) and by adding entries in the following Schedule:

Animal Health Act; Agriculture

Laboratory Services Act; Health

Local Elections Campaign Financing Act; Community, Sport and Cultural Development

Missing Persons Act; Justice

Off-Road Vehicle Act; Forest, Lands and Natural Resource Operations

Pooled Registered Pension Plans Act; Finance

Presumption of Death Act; Justice

Provincial Capital Commission Dissolution Act; Technology, Innovation and Citizens' Services

Provincial Symbols and Honours Act (except Part 3); Premier

Provincial Symbols and Honours Act (Part 3); Justice

South Coast British Columbia Transportation Authority Funding Referenda Act; Transportation and Infrastructure

Water Sustainability Act; Forest, Lands and Natural Resource Operations.
