# B.C. sub-agencies through time — research brief

_Generated by `npm run build:research-brief` from the PofBC government atlas (`src/government/historyData.js`, `eventsData.js`). A fill-in template for every record is alongside: `TEMPLATE.json`. How to hand research back: `research/README.md`._

## 1. The problem

The government graph draws **239 sub-agencies** — divisions, offices, programs, boards, tribunals and Crown subsidiaries — as dots beside their parent ministry or Crown corporation. All 239 were collected from **2026** sources (the 2026/27 Estimates, service plans, ministry and Crown websites). **Only 41 have a start date.**

In any year other than today, a sub-agency is drawn only if it is known to have begun by then, so the rest disappear the moment the timeline moves:

| Year | Drawn (dated only) | Drawn with "Undated bodies" on |
|---|---|---|
| 2026 | 238 | 238 |
| 2025 | 40 | 238 |
| 2020 | 36 | 219 |
| 2010 | 23 | 196 |
| 2000 | 8 | 152 |
| 1990 | 7 | 84 |
| 1980 | 4 | 71 |

That is deliberate — an undated body can't be placed in 1990 without claiming it existed in 1990 (the Liquor and Cannabis Regulation Branch did not) — but it means the history of the public service below ministry level is almost empty. This brief lists what is needed to fill it.

## 2. What we have

**Per record today:** `id`, `name`, `shortName` (some), `parent` (today's ministry or Crown), `kind`, `established` (41 of 239), one-line `description`, `url` (official page), `source` (one URL, usually a 2026 document).

**Not held for the unreviewed records (and only in part for the reviewed ones):** end date, earlier names, the ministry it sat under in earlier years, predecessor/successor bodies, the statute or order that created it, date precision, evidence status.

**By kind:** program 82 · office 48 · division 44 · board 22 · subsidiary 17 · agency 16 · tribunal 10

**Sub-agencies that no longer exist:** 12 (Workers’ Compensation Review Board; Appeal Division of the Workers’ Compensation Board; Small Business Forest Enterprise Program; Electrical Safety Advisory Committee; Electrical Safety Appeal Board; Electrical Safety Board of Review; Elevating Devices Advisory Committee; Elevating Devices Appeal Board; Gas Safety Advisory Committee; Gas Safety Appeal Board; Power Engineers and Boiler and Pressure Vessel Safety Advisory Committee; Power Engineers and Boiler and Pressure Vessel Safety Appeal Board), plus GPEB, end-dated 13 April 2026. Everything else is today's, so a year like 1995 still cannot show most branches that have since closed or merged.

**Reviewed (first tranche, 2026-09-26):** 62 records carry reviewed claims; 2 need reconciliation (Environmental Appeal Board, Powertech Labs Inc.). The rest are unreviewed.

## 3. What is needed, per sub-agency

In priority order:

1. **Start** — the date it began *as this body* (under this or an earlier name), with its precision (`day`, `month`, `year`, `decade`, `circa`) and the kind of evidence (`statute`, `order_in_council`, `news_release`, `annual_report`, `service_plan`, `archival_description`, `secondary`). If only a first sighting is known, record it as `first_observed`, not `established`.
2. **Names over time** — each earlier name with its dates, e.g. Liquor Control and Licensing Branch → Liquor and Cannabis Regulation Branch (2018). A rename keeps the same `id`.
3. **Parent over time** — which ministry (or Crown/agency) it sat under, and from when to when, **as the source names that ministry**. Give the ministry's name as printed; the build matches names to the ministry history. Moves at cabinet shuffles matter most (2001, 2005, 2017, 2020, 2022, 2024).
4. **Predecessors and successors** — bodies it was formed from, split out of, merged into or replaced (`formed_from`, `split_from`, `merged_into`, `replaced_by`, `absorbed`), each dated.
5. **End** — only for bodies that no longer exist (see §4).
6. **Legal basis** — the Act or OIC that created or continues it, with a citation (e.g. *Civil Resolution Tribunal Act*, SBC 2012, c. 25).

One source per claim, with a locator (page, section, archived URL). Prefer archived copies (Wayback) for pages that change.

## 4. Sub-agencies that are gone

A separate list is needed of divisions, boards, tribunals and Crown subsidiaries that existed **after 1871 and no longer do**, with the same fields plus an end date and what replaced them. Suggested scope, most useful first:

- Anything with a successor in today's list (so the dot can carry back under its old name) — e.g. Gaming Policy and Enforcement Branch, Emergency Management BC, Industry Training Appeal Board, BC Ambulance Service, Liquor Control Board, Provincial Game Commissioner, Forest Service divisions.
- Tribunals and boards abolished in the 2002–2003 core review and later consolidations.
- Crown subsidiaries of BC Hydro, BC Rail, BC Ferries, BC Transit, ICBC and BCLC that were wound up.
- Pre-1905 branches of the Provincial Secretary and Lands and Works (the atlas already notes this as R09).

## 5. Rules (from the atlas package — the build and tests enforce these)

- **Keep precision.** "1957" stays 1957, never 1957-01-01.
- **Unknown is not absent.** Leave a date `null` rather than guess; say *why* it is unknown in `note`.
- **Stated, not inferred.** Record a parent only where a source says the body reported to or was part of that ministry. A body funded by or appointed through a ministry is a different relation (`funded_by`, `appointed_by`) and is not drawn as a parent.
- **Function ≠ body.** "Fire protection began in 1874" is a `function_origin` event, not the Wildfire Service's establishment.
- **One claim, one source.** Every date, name and parent gets a URL and locator.

## 5a. Suggested sources

- **BC Laws** (bclaws.gov.bc.ca) — Acts, point-in-time versions, and the Orders in Council search (OIC PDFs from 1970s on).
- **BC Archives authority records** (search-bcarchives.royalbcmuseum.bc.ca) — administrative histories for most pre-2000 branches, with dates and predecessor/successor links.
- **Legislative Library** (llbc.leg.bc.ca) — ministry annual reports, *Estimates* and *Public Accounts* back to 1871 (the organisation charts in annual reports are the best evidence of parent over time).
- **Service plans and annual service plan reports** (bcbudget.gov.bc.ca, back to 2002), and the older **Ministry Annual Reports**.
- **BC Gov News** (news.gov.bc.ca, back to 2001) — creation, rename and transfer announcements.
- **Wayback Machine** snapshots of gov.bc.ca ministry "organisation" pages and the old BC Government Directory (dir.gov.bc.ca, no longer public — archived copies still work).
- **Tribunal annual reports** and the Attorney General's tribunal-sector page for the 20+ administrative tribunals.
- **Crown corporation annual reports / consolidated financial statements** for subsidiaries (the note to the statements lists them each year).
- Wikipedia only as a lead, never as the sole source.
