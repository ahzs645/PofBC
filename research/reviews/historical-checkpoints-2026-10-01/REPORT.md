# PofBC historical research: six-checkpoint release

**Finalized research handoff · Release 1.0 · 1 October 2026**

## Executive findings

This pass searched beyond today's surviving names and current ministries. The ministry rosters for six official Estimates presentations were reviewed, selected name-bearing passages were recorded under their historical ministry presentation, and targeted changes between checkpoints were investigated using legislation, commencement orders, institutional histories and annual reports.

There are **120 ministry scopes, 590 observation rows and 314 distinct printed labels**. Neither row count nor label count is a count of verified institutional identities. The dataset deliberately retains programs, funds, accounts, systems, functional headings and contextual references rather than incorrectly calling every item a government agency.

The 26 event rows distinguish establishment, operational commencement, regulatory transition, rename, abolition, dissolution, transfer, a saving for pending work and planned changes. The source registry has 61 entries for this pass. The inherited first batch, Finance checks and CAWS rosters remain separate; their overlapping claims must not be added together as a count of unique findings.

## 2. What helps fill the gaps

### Earlier evidence can be outside the current ministry

The 2002 Management Services corporate-support description names the **Public Sector Employers' Council Secretariat**. It is an earlier observation than the previous Finance-only pass found. The support relationship does not make Management Services the Secretariat's structural parent. That contrast is a direct demonstration of why a scoped non-match is not evidence of nonexistence. [OBS-0064; [EST-2002-management-services](https://www.bcbudget.gov.bc.ca/2002/Estimates/html/MINISTRY_OF_MANAGEMENT_SERVICES.htm), Vote 34(b)]

The Office of the Comptroller General and Treasury Board Staff have collected named observations at **all six checkpoints**. These support checkpoint history, not independent proof of every intervening year. Treasury / Provincial Treasury Operations identity remains unresolved, and the short budget label is not silently converted to the present-day record. [See individual source-linked observation rows.]

### Contemporary presentation is not a permanent parent

Tribunals named in Attorney General budget-support passages need an independence-aware relationship type. The 2025 Passenger Transportation Board support line and Transportation's Passenger Transportation Branch line must not be merged. A reference to another body's jurisdiction or a cost-recovery counterparty is not structural membership. [[EST-2025](https://www.bcbudget.gov.bc.ca/2025/pdf/2025_Estimates.pdf), printed pp. 40 and 188]

### A prior-year comparison can be a false positive

The 2010 Tourism BC line is a **prior-year comparative**, not accepted as a current-fiscal-year operating observation. Its legal dissolution was resolved separately. A simple text search would miss the difference between the columns. [[EST-2010](https://www.bcbudget.gov.bc.ca/2010/estimates/2010_estimates.pdf), printed p. 172]

Likewise, the 2015 Pacific Carbon Trust reference concerns wind-up/residual liabilities. It is preserved as such; it does not establish ordinary continued operation or an exact corporate-dissolution date. [[EST-2015](https://www.bcbudget.gov.bc.ca/2015/estimates/2015_Estimates.pdf), printed p. 78]

### A special account is not the corresponding organization

The older creation date of the timber-sales special account does not establish when the BC Timber Sales program replaced its predecessor. The BCTS replacement event is separately sourced. Wind-up accounts can remain after their underlying programs cease. The dataset therefore retains account identity and programme identity separately. [[BCTS-FPB-HISTORY](https://www.bcfpb.ca/release-publications/releases/audit-bcts-100-mile-house-forest-district/); [EST-2005-provincial-revenue](https://www.bcbudget.gov.bc.ca/2005/est/30_ProvincialRevenue.pdf), p. 115]

### Different institutions can have similar names and different change dates

Industry Training Authority / SkilledTradesBC and the Industry Training Appeal Board / Skilled Trades BC Appeal Board are separate bodies. BC Energy Regulator and Energy Resource Appeal Tribunal also have separate 2023 rename dates. These source-backed bridges preserve old names without replacing every earlier label with the current one. [[STBC-RENAME-2022](https://skilledtradesbc.ca/introducing-skilledtradesbc); [STBCAB-AR-2024](https://www.stbcab.ca/app/uploads/sites/771/2024/05/STBCAB_AR_2023-24.pdf), PDF pp. 2 and 8; [BCER-RENAME-2023](https://www.bc-er.ca/news/transitioning-to-bc-energy-regulator-iu-2023-04/)]

## 3. The candidate crosswalk

There are **239 candidate mapping rows across 72 of the inherited 238 records**. Normalized same-name matches are not independently verified institutional continuity. Explicit historical-name bridges are flagged separately. Functions and accounts retain their separate categories and must not be accepted automatically as an organizational presence claim.

The matrix contains **1,428 cells = 238 records × six checkpoints**. This is an index lookup across collected observations, not 1,428 independent archival searches. Its unresolved cells never mean absent; no cell approves a production rendering or continuous lifespan. The per-record coverage table also retains inherited Finance positives.

No unmatched label is automatically created as a production node.

## 6. Method, dates and limits

**Source scope.** Individual locators describe the inspected passages. Relevant PDF tables and selected pages were inspected as images in the preceding research pass; this does not mean every page of each source was read. This resumed package reconstructs the retained extraction notes and reruns internal checks, rather than claiming a second complete source review.

**Evidence date.** A checkpoint year identifies a budget presentation. It is not an exact operational observation day, a verified whole-fiscal-year interval or an original founding date. Event dates separately represent legal changes, operations, incorporation, rename or a planned change at the source's precision.

**Identity.** A name match can route a reviewer to an existing record, but it does not resolve changes of legal form, parentage, scope or institutional continuity. Rejected mappings must not delete original observations. Programs, services and accounts may still belong in the atlas if the UI displays their kinds accurately.

**Relationships.** Budget presentation, administrative support, funding, jurisdictional reference and organizational membership require different relationship types. No budget observation in this release claims a structural parent. Missing parent evidence should not erase supported existence evidence.

**Source archive.** Complete remote document bytes are not bundled. URLs, titles, reviewed locators and access information are. The optional source-download tool creates a separate, newly hashed archive; newly downloaded bytes may not equal the pages reviewed on 1 October 2026. The resumed direct re-open of the 2002 Management Services page failed; the earlier source-reviewed passage and locator are retained, not claimed freshly fetched.

**Baseline and deployment.** The 238-record baseline is an inherited review export, not a fresh clone of current main. No GitHub file was changed, no current-branch import was executed, no repository build ran, and no new count of rendered 2025 nodes was measured. Validation covers release integrity and safeguards, not historical truth or production behavior.

**Remaining coverage.** The complete history of all 238 records, all intervening years and all Crown/health-authority subunits is not finished. Those gaps remain explicit rather than being disguised as nonexistence. `data/review_queue.json` contains specific issues, and `data/baseline_record_coverage.json` routes every inherited record.
