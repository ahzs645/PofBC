## Research method for closing a row

1. Record the question as an explicit claim, including the legal person, function or territory concerned.
2. Retrieve the original statute/order or audited note where possible; use an official history as a locator, not automatic proof of an exact legal date.
3. Distinguish signing, assent, deposit, proclamation, announcement, incorporation and operational commencement.
4. Record the page, section or schedule and any conflicting source.
5. Add a date at the precision actually supported. Do not substitute January1 for a year-only source.
6. Check aliases and similarly named bodies before creating a new identity.
7. Add the appropriate relationship or event; do not turn every functional transfer into a corporate merger.
8. Mark the backlog row resolved only for the claim actually established.
