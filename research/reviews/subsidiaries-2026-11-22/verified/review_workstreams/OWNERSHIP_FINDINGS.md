# Ownership verification: questions 6–9

Reviewed 22 November 2026. This review changes research conclusions only; it does not edit the repository.

## Recommended decisions

| Question | Decision | Date handling |
|---|---|---|
| 6. Swan Valley Foods | It qualifies as a historically wholly publicly owned company immediately before its sale. | The start of full ownership is unknown; the sale on 1 December 1977 is exact. Do not claim it was wholly owned for several months. |
| 7. Plateau Mills | Add it. The Province's 97.5% direct interest was supplemented by a 2.5% holding through BC Cellulose. | The latter purchase is dated 23 June 1975. Show the two ownership interests, not 100% direct ownership by the Province. |
| 8. British Columbia Cellulose Company | Add a distinct historical Crown holding company, including its earlier legal name. | Incorporation 15 March 1973; statutory continuation/name change 18 April 1973; 1986 termination, with a one-day legal/accounting discrepancy preserved below. |
| 9. Crown Development Corporation | Move the start to its verified incorporation date. | 26 August 1975, not merely a first-seen year of 1977. Retain winding-up by 31 March 1978 without inventing a dissolution day. |

## 6. Swan Valley Foods

The buyer's own 1977 financial statements identify a transaction on **1 December 1977** involving all outstanding shares and certain debentures purchased from the BC government for **$2.2 million**. This is direct support for full public ownership immediately before the sale. The statement is in Standard Brands Limited's annual report, printed page 30, note 6, PDF page 33. I downloaded the original and read the rendered page. [OWN-S03]

The 1977/78 Public Accounts show a 40% holding in the 31 March 1977 comparison column and additional shares acquired during the next fiscal year. They also account for the eventual sale. Together, these sources constrain attainment of full ownership to after 31 March 1977 and on or before the sale. They do **not** prove how long that stage lasted. [OWN-S02, B5 and B8]

For the graph, a bounded or unknown start is appropriate. If the renderer can only draw a continuous interval with an exact start, preserve the fact and the sale event without fabricating that interval. A label such as “wholly provincially owned immediately before the December 1977 sale; acquisition date unresolved” accurately states the result. The sale of shares is not a dissolution event.

## 7. Plateau Mills

The decisive addition is BC Cellulose's own note 2 in the 1975/76 Public Accounts: an option exercised on **23 June 1975** bought 225 Plateau shares, equal to **2.5%**, while the Province owned the remaining **97.5%**. The same document identifies BC Cellulose as a Crown corporation. Consequently, combined public ownership was 100%; that total is an explicit arithmetic inference from the documented holdings. [OWN-S01, F8–F9, PDF pages 394–395]

The 1977/78 Accounts subsequently reconcile the interests: 8,775 shares were the Province's direct investment; 225 came from BC Cellulose as a dividend; all 9,000 went to BCRIC. OIC 530/1978 approves a sale agreement dated **9 March 1978**, and the Accounts confirm the transfer by fiscal year end. The order establishes approval and agreement date; do not overstate it as independent proof of the precise execution moment. [OWN-S02, B4–B5; OWN-S08, PDF pages 1 and 3–4]

A further chronological lead is verified in official Statistics Canada text: the 1973 publication, printed page 21, reports 97% acquired by BC Cellulose effective 26 June 1973 and transferred directly to the Province on 24 October 1973. Preserve **97% as printed** in that source; do not silently replace it with the later 97.5%. The 1973 page was read through the official web extraction rather than a local page image. [OWN-S09]

Recommended ownership observations:

| Date / period | Direct owner | Interest | Evidentiary meaning |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 26 June 1973 | British Columbia Cellulose Company | 97% as printed | Acquisition reported by Statistics Canada |
| 24 October 1973 | Province of BC | Same transferred holding | Transfer reported by Statistics Canada |
| 23 June 1975 | British Columbia Cellulose Company | 2.5% | Option exercise and purchase |
| 31 December 1975 | Province of BC | 97.5% | Audited direct-interest snapshot |
| 31 December 1975 | British Columbia Cellulose Company | 2.5% | Audited direct-interest snapshot |
| By 31 March 1978 | BCRIC | 9,000 shares | Accounts confirm transfer; OIC/agreement dated 9 March |

The Province and BC Cellulose should be separate edges. This distinction is useful even if the visualization displays a single “publicly owned” badge.

## 8. British Columbia Cellulose Company

The 1975 financial statements identify **British Columbia Cellulose Limited**, incorporated **15 March 1973**, as the company continued under the statutory name **British Columbia Cellulose Company** on **18 April 1973**. The original 1973 Act independently confirms continuity and Crown-agent status. Its scheduled agreement, dated 2 April 1973, already describes the former-name company as wholly Crown-owned. Thus incorporation, observed ownership, and statutory continuation are three separate facts. [OWN-S01, F8; OWN-S04, sections 1–2 and schedule recital B]

**Do not merge it with Canadian Cellulose Company, Limited or Columbia Cellulose Company, Limited.** The statutory agreement names them separately. The holding company's own statements report a 79% Canadian Cellulose stake, along with 100% of Kootenay Forest Products and 2.5% of Plateau. Those subsidiary percentages are not the Province's ownership percentage in the holding company. [OWN-S01, F9; OWN-S04]

### Termination: retain a documented one-day discrepancy

The 1983 repeal statute was assented to on 23 September 1983 but needed a commencement regulation. Section 2 connects dissolution to repeal. The official statutes table dates repeal to **31 March 1986**. The 1985/86 Accounts state that the Act came into force on that day but describe dissolution and transfer of assets/liabilities as effective **1 April 1986**. Both statements were checked; the Accounts page was visually inspected. [OWN-S05–S07]

The original commencement regulation has not been located in this pass. Therefore, the safe graph endpoint is **1986 at year precision**, with both assertions retained in the evidence record. An exact legal dissolution day requires that regulation or a sufficiently authoritative reconciliation. Do not use the 1983 assent as the end date, and do not erase the one-day difference by calling the dates interchangeable.

A statistical roster headed 31 December 1986 reportedly still includes BC Cellulose. That is a source-roster observation, not evidence that the legal entity continued beyond the dissolution reported in the Accounts. Keep the roster exactly as printed while marking the conflict.

## 9. Crown Development Corporation

The best source is **F99**, rather than only the related-company references on F84–F85. Crown Development's own notes give incorporation on **26 August 1975** and explain that its capital was held by the Attorney-General for the Crown. They describe ferry-ship and Crown-land purposes. This supports a precise incorporation event. [OWN-S01, F99, PDF page 485; visually verified]

Those notes also distinguish asset transfers from ownership: an agreement dated 31 March 1976 dealt with the Princess Marguerite interest and debts involving BC Steamship; the Prince George was sold on 23 April 1976. These do not make Crown Development a subsidiary of BC Steamship. The 1977/78 Accounts record winding-up and a $1,000 investment write-off, supporting “wound up by 31 March 1978,” not a precise legal dissolution day. [OWN-S01, F99; OWN-S02, B8]
