# T. S. Holdings subsidiary correction — 22 November 2026

**The 1977/78 Public Accounts resolve Vancouver Island Transit’s parent and overturn the blanket “loan only” classification of Thompson-Okanagan Transit.**

T. S. Holdings’ consolidated financial-statement notes are dated **31 March 1978**. Note 1(a), printed **F220** (PDF 648), lists these wholly owned subsidiaries:

| Company | Reported holder | Holding | Evidence date |
|---|---|---|---|
| V I T Holdings Ltd. | T. S. Holdings group | 100% | 31 March 1978 |
| Thompson-Okanagan Transit Ltd. | T. S. Holdings group | 100% | 31 March 1978 |
| Vancouver Island Transit Ltd. | T. S. Holdings group | 100% | 31 March 1978 |

The same note records a **98.5% interest**, held by V I T Holdings directly or through a subsidiary, in five companies:

- Vancouver Island Transportation Company Limited
- Vancouver Island Coach Lines Limited
- Gray Line & Empress Taxi & Sightseeing Company Limited
- Imperial Charter Service Company Limited
- British Columbia Parlour Car Tours Limited

F221, note 1(d) (PDF 649), further identifies Vancouver Island Transportation as V I T Holdings’ subsidiary and discusses the remaining minority shares. [TR-S01]

## Integration decisions

Add the three wholly owned subsidiary observations. Preserve Thompson-Okanagan’s loan as a separate financing relationship. Keep the 98.5% tier distinct and do not invent immediate-parent edges where the source permits an intermediate subsidiary. These are ownership snapshots, not incorporation dates. The three similar names—V I T Holdings, Vancouver Island Transit and Vancouver Island Transportation—identify separate companies.

The prior Statistics Canada review remains accurate about that source’s limits: its 1981-Q1 roster did not name Vancouver Island Transit’s parent. This direct corporate statement supplies the missing relationship at an earlier date.

## Source and verification

**TR-S01:** Public Accounts of British Columbia, 1977/78, T. S. Holdings Ltd., notes to consolidated financial statements dated 31 March 1978, F220–221, notes 1(a) and 1(d). PDF pages 648–649 are one-based.
