# Identity follow-up: exact renames and remaining spelling bridges

22 November 2026. Research only; no paid searches, sign-in, or repository changes.

## Exact dates newly resolved

The original Gazette page of **8 March 1979, printed 386**, explicitly links two companies to their new names. Both changes took effect **14 February 1979**. The page was visually inspected; the original source is linked below. [F 1]

| Company no. | Earlier legal name | Later legal name | Effective date |
|---|---|---|---|
|63265|Panco Poultry Ltd.|Surrey Farm Products Investments Ltd.|14 Feb 1979|
|64184|Pacific Hatchery Ltd.|Surrey Farm Products Hatchery Ltd.|14 Feb 1979|

The same notices record Panco's **amalgamation on 1 February 1965** and the hatchery's **incorporation on 12 April 1965**. Keep amalgamation and incorporation distinct, and do not substitute either for the later Crown acquisition. The 1979 name changes resolve two uncertainties in the previous package. [F 1]

The exact boundary also corrects historical event labels: the **29 January 1979 asset sale occurred under Panco Poultry Ltd.**, although it is described in later financial statements headed Surrey Farm Products Investments. The **4 May 1979 dividend** belongs after the Surrey rename. [F 1; F 4]

## A new Venture registry lead

The Gazette of **10 May 1979, printed 776**, identifies **Venture Realty Ltd., no.83729**, and records a change to **Venture Realty Consultants Ltd. effective 11 April 1979**. It gives the company's incorporation date as **30 December 1968**. This is a useful lead for the singular-name company after the documented government sale. The notice does not name its shareholder, and does not establish that “Ventures Realty” was an earlier legal name. Avoid turning the earlier spelling difference into a legal rename event. [F 2]

|Western Feeds|Statistics Canada 1975 uses Feeds; Panco's 1976 audited note uses Western Feed Co. Ltd., as do later accounts.|No registry number or explicit Feeds→Feed change located.|

The newly inspected **1975/76 Public Accounts F 150** explicitly places **Pacific Hatchery Ltd. and Western Feed Co. Ltd.** under Panco as wholly owned subsidiaries. The statement is dated **3 April 1976**; the audit report at F 147 is dated **1 June 1976**. This is stronger contemporary name and group-membership evidence than the abbreviated Statistics Canada wording, but it does not itself supply registry-number equivalence for those variants. Preserve as-printed source aliases and mark the unresolved identity bridges. [F 3; F 6]

## Surrey Investments→Food Exhibitions remains undated

The second Panco-chain rename is still not dated exactly. The conservative legal transition bounds remain **after 14 February 1979 and by 17 June 1985**. The latter is the auditor-report date for the financial statements headed British Columbia Food Exhibitions Ltd., formerly Surrey Farm Products Investments Ltd.; the statements themselves are as at 31 March 1985. [F 1; F 5]

**The archive gap is not an event-date window.** No new evidence establishes a March–June 1985 rename. All 52 1984 Gazette issues and the 11 available 1985 issues were searched by OCR without a relevant hit; 1980–1983 were not comprehensively searched. Internet Archive's 1985 run retrieved here ends 14 March, while the official Historical Gazette catalogue lists 1986–2002. Earlier notices, missing 1985 issues, and OCR errors remain possible. [F 7; F 8; `SEARCH_LOG.json`]

## Evidence-correct display recommendations

1. Apply **14 February 1979** as the exact legal name boundary for both Panco→Surrey Investments and Pacific Hatchery→Surrey Farm Products Hatchery.
2. Keep the Food Exhibitions legal effective date empty. For a card presenting the 1985 financial statements, show **British Columbia Food Exhibitions Ltd.** with the qualifier **“Name used in 1985 statements; legal rename date unverified.”** Store the 31 March 1985 statement date and 17 June 1985 audit date separately.
3. A strict legal-name timeline should represent the uncertain transition between Surrey Investments and Food Exhibitions. If the application instead displays the latest documented name, make that display policy explicit; an observation must not silently become a dated legal event.
4. Prefer the full names in the audited/legal sources for canonical display, while retaining Ventures, Hatching and Feeds verbatim in source rosters and as candidate aliases. Do not manufacture extra entities or dated legal changes from these spelling differences.
5. Preserve later inactive Statistics Canada rosters as printed. Their stale Surrey/Pacific names cannot override the verified 1979 Gazette changes or narrow the Food Exhibitions transition.
