# Transit follow-up: company identity, ownership percentages and Pacific Coach chronology

Verified 22 November 2026 against the scanned Public Accounts and Gazette pages identified below. No repository files were changed. All PDF page numbers are one-based and refer to the specified scan; printed page labels are the stable citation.

## Findings that change the graph

1. **Pacific Coach Lines Limited existed and operated in 1978.** Its own accounts give incorporation on **2 August 1978** and the start of operations on **31 August 1978**, when it purchased Vancouver Island Transportation Company Limited's assets and assumed its liabilities. **1 April 1979** is the later acquisition of Pacific Stage Lines assets and operating responsibilities. These are separate events. [FT-S04, F190 and F193]
2. **The five V I T group percentages change across the annual observations:** 98.24% at 31 March 1976, 98.4% at 31 March 1977, and 98.5% at 31 March 1978. Preserve those decimal values. [FT-S01–03]
3. **V I T Holdings sold its 98.5% Vancouver Island Transportation holding to the Province on 24 July 1978, and was liquidated on 22 January 1979.** T.S.'s 31 March 1979 consolidated subsidiary list contains only Thompson-Okanagan Transit and Vancouver Island Transit. [FT-S04, F222–224]
4. **Two former names are explicit:** Thompson-Okanagan Transit was formerly Tweed Line Tours Ltd.; Vancouver Island Transit was formerly McArthur Transportation Limited. Both are documented by 31 March 1976, but this source gives no effective rename date. [FT-S01, F176]

## 1. T.S. Holdings and the five-company V I T group

|---|---|---:|---|

| 31 March 1977 | The same three subsidiaries | 98.4% | FT-S02, F202, note 1(a), PDF 600 |
| 31 March 1978 | The same three subsidiaries | 98.5% | FT-S03, F220, note 1(a), PDF 648 |
| 31 March 1979 | Thompson-Okanagan Transit Ltd.; Vancouver Island Transit Ltd. | No continuing five-company V I T holding is established by this note | FT-S04, F222, note 1(a), PDF 540 |

The five names are:

- Vancouver Island Transportation Company Limited
- Vancouver Island Coach Lines Limited
- Gray Line & Empress Taxi & Sightseeing Company Limited
- Imperial Charter Service Company Limited
- British Columbia Parlour Car Tours Limited

The notes expressly allow ownership through a subsidiary. They therefore do **not** establish that V I T Holdings was the immediate shareholder of all five companies. They also do not make the five companies wholly owned merely because T.S. wholly owned V I T Holdings.

### What establishes an immediate parent

There is additional evidence for **V I T Holdings → Vancouver Island Transportation Company Limited**. The 1976 and 1977 goodwill notes describe acquisition of Transportation by V I T Holdings (FT-S01, F177, note 1(c); FT-S02, F203, note 1(c)). The 1978 income-tax note expressly describes Transportation as V I T Holdings' subsidiary (FT-S03, F221, note 1(d)). Finally, the 1979 disposal note identifies V I T Holdings as seller of the 98.5% shareholding to the Province (FT-S04, F223, note 6).

No comparable immediate-parent statement for the other four was found in the 1976–1979 consolidated notes inspected. Record their V I T-group relationship as a reported direct-or-indirect interest with unresolved intermediate ownership. If the graph can represent only immediate ownership, retain those four relationships as qualified observations or held research rather than drawing an unsupported immediate-parent edge.

The percentages are observation dates, not transaction dates. The reviewed statements do not date the increments from 98.24 to 98.4 to 98.5. A line connecting observations would be a display convention or continuity inference; it must not silently turn each percentage into a constant value throughout 1976–1979.

The 24 July share transfer and 31 August asset transfer are different transactions involving different acquirers. The accounts do not establish Transportation's dissolution or give an end date for the Province's remaining shareholding after its business assets were transferred.

### Supersession of the earlier package

The earlier verified package's **PC-C01** and **A25** treated 1 April 1979 as Pacific Coach's operational start and left incorporation unknown. **That interpretation is superseded.** Retain the Hydro annual report's statement as a correctly transcribed, source-specific description of the 1979 service replacement. Use the company's own accounts for the legal and operational chronology: incorporation **1978-08-02**, initial operations **1978-08-31**, Pacific Stage transfer **1979-04-01**.

The new findings do not reverify the earlier **31 March 1984 operations-cessation** claim. That existing claim can retain its separate source and event meaning. It must not be promoted to a legal dissolution date.

## 3. Three Pacific Coach legal identities in the 1990 Gazette

The **2 August 1990** Gazette records these changes, under the effective date headings shown. The notice begins on p1083, and the 19 July entries continue across the columns of p1084. [FT-S05]

| Company number | Incorporation stated in Gazette | Former name | New name | Effective change date | Printed/PDF page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 178561 | 2 August 1978 | Pacific Coach Lines Limited | 178561 B.C. Ltd. | 19 July 1990 | 1084 / 12 |
| 273925 | 7 February 1984 | Pacific Coach Lines (1984) Ltd. | 273925 B.C. Ltd. | 17 July 1990 | 1083 / 11 |
| 362538 | 29 March 1989 | Royal Victorian Holdings Corporation | Pacific Coach Lines Ltd. | 19 July 1990 | 1084 / 12 |

The incorporation date links number **178561** to the original company described in the 1979 accounts. A 1984 operating cessation did not mean that this legal entity had already disappeared: its name changed in 1990. The other two numbers identify separate companies. The Gazette establishes names, numbers and dates; it does not establish their ownership, the business-sale terms, privatization dates, or dissolution dates. Do not merge all Pacific Coach brands into one legal node or add the later companies as Crown subsidiaries without ownership evidence.

### Remaining research limits

- Exact effective rename dates for Tweed Line Tours and McArthur Transportation remain unknown.
- The four other V I T group companies' immediate parents, acquisition dates and subsequent share dispositions remain unresolved.
- No precise date was found for the increases between the annual reported percentages.
- V I T Holdings' liquidation date is established; its registry dissolution date is not.
- Pacific Coach's incorporation and first operation are established. Province ownership is explicit at 31 March 1979, while the exact initial share-subscription date is not stated in these notes.
- Pacific Coach's 1990 name change preserves legal continuity for number 178561 but does not establish continued Crown ownership until that date.
