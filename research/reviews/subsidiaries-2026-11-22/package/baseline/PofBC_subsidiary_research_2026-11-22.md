## Priority findings

1. **Dunhill:** two distinct BC registration numbers. Original company **73385** became Housing Corporation of BC on **1976-11-18** and dissolved **1990-06-18**. Subsidiary **W.K.P. Construction No. 91003** changed name to Dunhill eight days later and dissolved **1989-06-12**. Existing repo relationship records already enumerate eight subsidiaries in 1974, including W.K.P. Do not collapse legal entities.
2. **Panco Poultry → Surrey Farm Products Investments → British Columbia Food Exhibitions:** official 1975 acquisition, 1978/79 former-name statement, 1984/85 another former-name statement. Exact registry number/rename dates unresolved. **Contradiction:** federal 1986 roster still lists Surrey Farm Products inactive; resolve before normalizing.
3. **Panco 1975 subsidiaries:** B.C. Testing Farms, Pacific Hatching, Western Feeds. Printed names may be abbreviated.
4. **I.O.K. Poultry Ltd.:** 100% Province ownership, 600 common shares, March 1979 Public Accounts B4. Not located in GitHub search.
5. **Peace Power Constructors, HCBC Construction, Techwest Enterprises, Duke Point Terminals:** historical federal subsidiary tables, 1986/1987, with better earlier-origin evidence for Techwest (1970) and Peace Power (1971).
6. **Discovery-related entities:** four separately printed 1987 corporate names (plus different 1986 Discovery Park Industries). No automatic identity merging.
7. **Postsecondary enterprises:** Great Northern Way Campus Trust, UVic Heritage Realty, SFU Community Trust, UBC Properties Investments, Vancouver Island Technology Park Trust. Ownership classification differs from ordinary Crowns.
8. **BC Ferries FY2026:** Pacific Marine Leasing Inc. and Pacific Marine Holdings Inc.; latter incorporated September 2025. B.C. Ferry Authority is the owner of BC Ferry Services common share.
9. **BC Hydro 2026:** inactive and nominee subsidiaries should be research-visible even if excluded from a simplified public diagram; source enumerates fourteen `Other Subsidiaries`.

## Evidence modelling instructions

- `company_id` is a stable surrogate for a **legal entity**; preserve official registration number separately. Name changes are `name_valid_from` / `name_valid_to` on the *same legal entity only when supported*.
- Ownership must be a **typed, dated edge**; use `observed_on` for a financial snapshot, never infer it means `owned_from` or `owned_to`.
- Separate 100%-owned, minority investment, joint venture, administered program, Crown-agent, public trust and independent corporation. Do not infer ownership from consolidated presentation alone when the source says only administered.
- Retain `source_url`, printed page/PDF page, exact printed name, original quote excerpt, confidence, contradictions, and rejected candidate mappings.
- All unresolved legal incorporation dates, share transfers and dissolution dates remain `null`. No attempt was made here to certify these entities against BC Registries / OrgBook or to exhaust every annual Public Account.
