# Continuation handoff

This package is a review of the uploaded 2026-09-30 queue, not a completed history database.

Read FULL_REVIEW.md, audit/access-and-scope.json and research/source-register.json first. Every original record is preserved and indexed in audit/record-review.json. All 23 batches have tailored notes in audit/batch-review.json.

The completed external evidence pass is 72 checks within specified Finance vote sections plus five targeted case checks. Thirty positive named observations support ten proposed Finance records. Founding dates, complete identity/name/parent continuity and pre-2002 history remain unresolved. The other batches have archive triage, not a completed primary-source sweep. Do not label all 238 histories reviewed or verified on the basis of this package.

Obtain the actual commit that generated the queue before working on the importer, generator or renderer. The public main scripts inspected did not match the handoff's command names. No production build or merge was run. The documentation patch was tested only against an isolated copy of the uploaded snapshot. Its generated-batch changes also need implementing in the matching generator.

Recover original source-bearing claim objects for the 175 flattened held events and scalar selected dates. Preserve competing claims and editorial decisions. Add a source revision/hash to the next export; do not silently overwrite existing evidence with null proposal fields.

Use exact historical document scope and date. A selected Finance chapter non-match is not an all-government absence. A budget placement is not automatically part_of. A related label is not an accepted identity match. A retrospective history is not a contemporary observation. A project, programme, fund, facility and corporation need different semantics.

High-risk next cases are Timber Sales, Corrections, Wildfire, CRT, EAB, Powertech, retrospective tribunal/health observations and the C-only/not_researched records. Government Chief Risk Office/2005 Risk Management Branch and the Treasury headings need an identity bridge. Do not merge CABRO and Crown Agencies Secretariat without direct evidence.

For broader coverage, extract contemporary historical rosters, including bodies that do not survive in the 2026 population. The present-day queue alone cannot reconstruct the whole earlier government. Keep discovered candidates separate from accepted identities.

External PDFs/webpages are not archived here. Source URLs, checked sections, page offsets and research dates are recorded. Reopen sources when extending their claims; do not infer unread content from the title or existing review notes.
