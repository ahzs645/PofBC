## Retrieval and evidence boundaries

- **79 source records newly catalogued**, including failed and irrelevant candidates; **2 prior originals reused**. The catalog is not an accepted-source count.
- **50 new originals** support substantive historical findings; **2 prior originals** also support substantive findings; **2 other originals** support only a diagnostic or archival lead.
- **24 retrieved candidates** are not used for accepted claims. These include duplicate consolidation pages, current websites without historical dates, irrelevant adjacent orders and source material that does not resolve entity identity.
- **3 unusable retrievals:** a failed early Buy BC release request, a missing newsletter URL, and a UBC blocked response saved with HTTP200. The block page is not treated as an original report.
- Public BC Laws PDFs were retrieved directly when the web retrieval layer could not expose them. All accepted legal dates needing image confirmation were checked against the scans; filenames, search snippets and OCR were not treated as authoritative when they conflicted with the image.
- No archives were contacted, no registry fees or orders were placed, and no private records were accessed. Missing internal directives and Gazette items remain open rather than being replaced with invented exact dates.
