# Round 4 health module: what to apply, and what to leave

Generated by `make_proposal.py` and `r3_part.py` (this folder). The module holds ten Round 4 claims
(R4HC-001 to 010) and carries the Round 3 health claims (`health/prior/claims.json`, R3HC-001 to 186),
none yet in the repository. All 196 claims are accounted for in proposal.json.

**Sources for the Round 3 claims.** The Round 3 source register (`round3/health/sources.json`) is not
in the handback, so no R3H source has a URL there. I used a Round 3 claim only where I recovered the
document's public URL and re-read the cited passage on 21 October 2026. Each such event records this
in `evidence.url_basis`. For the orders behind the body changes, the URL is the BC Laws address the
converter's own `order_url()` builds.

## Set aside (not_applied, 20 entries)

- **The seven rural boards (A1).** Their starts are proved, but no end is known, and with `ended: null`
  they would be drawn to 2026. The draft records are kept. No 1999 end and no `becameId` to the
  societies. They need either an "ended, day unknown" convention or a document.
- **FNHA and governance bodies (A11).** `first-nations-health-authority` is a diagram node but not in
  ids.json. Drafts from re-read registry records are included: S0054796 2009-2012, FNHDA S0056622 2010.
- **Nisga'a Valley Health Board (A10).** Not an atlas body, and a First Nations institution, so adding
  it is an editorial call. Draft from the registry: S0019586, 1984 and 2004.
- **No URL in the handback** (ready once found): the SSO 2009-2010 steps; FPSC 1999/2003 (OIC
  1424/1998 re-read: a board reconstitution, not a creation); PLS 2003; HIBC 2005; the 2004/05 VCH
  affiliates; Statistics Canada HSDAs; the 2008 Abbotsford share transfer; all 30 C5 sources.
- **C5 colleges** also need a regulators layer; none is an atlas body.
- **Owned elsewhere.** Perinatal's 2009/10 checkpoint belongs to the branches module. R4HC-009 (OIC
  1321/1995) goes to root as the better source for R4RC-112.
- **Not for import:** R4HC-001, R4HC-010. **Already held:** Prince George (R3HC-044), Women's 1994 (R3HC-102).
