## Correction resolved

Root incorporated the correction below. The regenerated `round4/root/report.md` was checked and now uses the land-location wording.

**Section 1, “How these districts connected to electors”: Gulf Islands proxy eligibility is based on the location of the qualified voter’s land, not the voter’s residence.**

The draft says “permits specified proxies for residents off Salt Spring Island”. R4R-120, PDF p.3, Letters Patent clause 5, permits otherwise qualified voters whose lands are not situated on Salt Spring Island to exercise their vote by proxy. The annual-general-meeting clause repeats the land-location condition (PDF pp.4–5, clause 9).

Suggested replacement: **“Gulf Islands permits proxy voting by otherwise qualified voters whose lands are outside Salt Spring Island.”**

This matters for the historical electorate model: residence and landownership/location are different qualifications.
