## Findings affecting interpretation

Seven original names differ from the 1969 schedule. Keep the original names: Creston Valley, Castlegar, Kootenay Valley, Fraser Canyon and Cariboo initially lack their later numbers; Windermere initially is **Windermere District Hospital Improvement District**; Prince George initially includes **and District**. The exact intervening rename/numbering instruments are not established by this review.

These are Water Act improvement districts with elected landowner governance. The original voter rules require Canadian citizenship, age 21+, eligibility under the Provincial Elections Act, and land ownership or authorized representation. The JSON supplies each district’s trustee count, zonal allocation, clause and page. It does not treat this electorate as all residents or infer a ministry-parent edge merely from a minister’s signature.

Three specific rules matter: Gulf Islands permits proxies for qualified voters with land outside Salt Spring Island; Creston extends trustee candidacy to qualified voters’ spouses without thereby enfranchising them; Fort Nelson’s 1958 letters appoint the first trustees and bar hospital employees and practising physicians from later trustee candidacy.

Fort Nelson begins **7 June 1958**, is continued by replacement letters on **7 July 1959**, and ends **13 June 1972**. The 1959 letters move to district-wide elections and include ambulance service. The 1972 order transfers the district’s assets and liabilities to Peace River-Liard RHD; its attached 23 May 1972 resolution provides an onward transfer to the hospital society on dissolution, with specified debt/fundraising obligations retained. The signed conveyances are not attached.

The 1969 North Peace row instead labels its transferee **Peace River Regional Hospital District**. Preserve this source variation unless a formal naming bridge is available.
