# PofBC Round 4 evidence

These three evidence volumes contain the new Round 4 modules and their preserved public-source downloads, selected-page extracts, OCR aids, search ledgers and review records. Extract all volumes into one directory. Files live under `round4/`; the two reused prior originals are included under their existing `round4/prior/evidence/round3/` paths.

The consolidated Markdown and JSON reports retain the prior 59-item research and identify the new findings and remaining records. Earlier full Round 3 source archives are not repackaged; their original filenames and source manifest remain in the consolidated JSON. Original downloads and extracts are distinguished by hashes and page maps. Full long volumes are retained as well as compact cited-page extracts.

A retrieved or screened file is not automatically accepted historical evidence. Read each source's role, the claim's locator, date meaning, identity limitations and import gate. The 442 ministerial orders, 71 FOI-search OICs and other discovery sweeps are bounded searches, not proof that missing records do not exist. OCR text can be wrong; original scans control.
