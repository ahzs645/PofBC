# Northern BC travel guides · follow-up logo audit

I rendered and reviewed all 291 pages of the 2012, 2013 and 2015 editions, enlarged the ads and partner strips, and compared the designs with the gallery’s artwork records. Page numbers count the library’s cover sheet as page 1. For example, PDF page 17 is printed page 16. A guide date establishes that the design appeared in that edition.

The first import covered 46 source files. It includes the 2012 brown moose and green skyline, the 2015 City of Prince George civic logo and the centennial badge. The follow-up artwork is also available in the gallery. **Vector visible** indicates that native artwork survived removal of embedded page images. The artwork register records which complete marks passed the vector check and which use PNGs to preserve details from the source.
