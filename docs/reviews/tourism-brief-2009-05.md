# May 2009 Tourism Brief coverage review

Reviewed November 25, 2026. The Legislative Library’s public copy and the supplied PDF have identical extracted text and the same 12-page order. Page numbers below count the Legislative Library cover as page 1. [Legislative Library catalogue record](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/handle/123456789/55920)

The Tourism initiative now has a **Programs & partners** tab with 21 records. Its **Publications** tab links to the issue. The existing **SuperHost and WorldHost** record now covers the full training lineage and links to go2HR. The register covers the organizations, operating functions, campaigns and named projects discussed in this issue. People, employers, event speakers, outlets and delivery participants appear as historical context where they explain a program or partnership.

| PDF page | Coverage and current status |
| --- | --- |
| 1–2 | **The Tourism Brief** is indexed in Publications. **Tourism BC Industry Programs** links to Destination BC’s current industry services. **Tourism 2010 Resource Centre** has a record and an April 2009 archived site. **HelloBC**, the corporate website and **British Columbia Magazine** already had records. HelloBC’s current destination is **SupernaturalBC.com**. |
| 3 | **Welcome the World** has its own campaign record. It captures **DiscoverOurBC.com**, **BC Field Reporters**, **HelloBC videos**, the television and social campaign, contests and newsletters. The record links the existing **British Columbia Insider’s Guide** publication. The original campaign microsite remains a lead. |
| 4 | The vice-chair interview documents Tourism BC’s board in 2009. Tour operator, hotel association and advisory council references give context about Kevin Walker’s career. Tourism BC and its corporate identity already have records in Government and the Gallery. |
| 5 | **SuperHost / WorldHost** was already represented by artwork and a training website record. The new history covers the 1985 Expo 86 preparation, May 2009 rebrand, 2013 transfer and 2017 handover to **go2HR**, which offers **SuperHost** today. **Team 2010 and the Ambassador Workshop** has its own record and documents the role of VANOC, YVR, CBSA, TransLink, the RCMP, 2010 Legacies Now and visitor centres. VANOC dissolved in 2014. |
| 6 | **Research & Planning** now has a record. **Tourism Indicators** and **International Visitor Arrivals** continue through Destination BC. The edition also indexes historical research including TAMS sea kayaking, international market profiles, the Sunshine Coast visitor study and the Nordic ski facility study. |
| 7 | **Visitor Services and Visitor Centres** covers the 2009 appointment, the gateway centres, community network and call centre. Destination BC transferred airport visitor services to YVR in 2014/15 and continues to support the community-owned network. |
| 8 | **Travel Media Relations** covers the 2009 Japanese press trip and the continuing Destination BC media visit function. The article’s Japanese outlets are examples of coverage. |
| 9 | **go2HR** is an independent industry partner. **Propel and Professional Cook** covers its former Industry Training Organization division, cook, baker and meatcutter, and the 2009 three-level cook program. Provincial Industry Training Organization functions moved in-house to the Industry Training Authority in 2014. The credentials continue through **SkilledTradesBC**. Tourism HR Canada runs a separate, newer **Propel** student placement program. |
| 10 | **LinkBC** was formed in 2007 from the BC Centre for Tourism Leadership and Innovation and the BC Centre for Hospitality Leadership and Innovation. It wound down in the mid-2010s. Its activities have linked records for **Study Tourism in BC**, **Hire BC’s BEST and Student-Industry Rendezvous**, the **Student Case Competition**, the **Tourism Educators Conference** and the **Tourism Online Resource Centre**. Its revised open textbook is still available from BCcampus. The September 2009 Games education portal was announced as a plan. |
| 11 | **BC Sport Tourism Events Marketplace**, the **Hosting BC** website and grant, and **2010 Legacies Now** have separate connected records. The Marketplace and directory have April 2009 archived sites. The grant continues through **viaSport**. **2010 Legacies Now** became **LIFT Philanthropy Partners** in 2011 and **LIFT Impact Partners** in 2021. |
| 12 | **Thompson Okanagan Tourism Association** and **Indigenous Tourism BC** were already in the Gallery. The new records connect them to their newsletter mentions and current sites. **Destination Osoyoos** has a new record as a local destination partner. TOTA’s name history and current subsidiaries are documented in its own profile. |
