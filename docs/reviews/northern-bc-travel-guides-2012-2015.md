# Northern British Columbia Travel Guide logo audit

Reviewed the [2012](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/items/8b200dfd-62d8-46d0-aa0f-d3c7dbf13258), [2013](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/items/d7fb81ff-7be4-478b-b7ea-69d73ed1762f) and [2015](https://digitalcollections.llbc.leg.bc.ca/items/80245552-4c2a-4f59-8166-b346e6568d5c) editions published by the Northern British Columbia Tourism Association and supplied through the Legislative Library of British Columbia’s Digital Collections Repository. Each PDF has 97 pages. Page numbers below count the library sheet as PDF page 1, the front cover as page 2 and the inside front cover as page 3.

I scanned all three editions for publisher, tourism, visitor-information, government and destination marks. I compared the relevant specimens with the gallery records and local artwork. For possible vector exports, I compared the source crop against the artwork with bitmap objects removed. The import holds 26 native SVGs and 20 source images. Each file’s publication, page, crop bounds, extraction method and checksum are recorded in the [artwork register](../../src/gallery/northernTravelGuideArtwork.json). The tables describe the gaps found in the audit and the artwork used to fill them.
