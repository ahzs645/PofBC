# Environment Youth Corps of British Columbia

The Province used this stacked mark in its “Working for a better world” recruitment ad. The ad credits John Reynolds, Minister of Environment, and directs applicants to the Canada Employment Centre. The June 1990 dates below document printings of the mark. The program was established in 1989.

- June 13, 1990, *Gulf Islands Driftwood*, p. A10, right-column advertisement. The local PNG crops the mark from the scan’s embedded 2,275 × 3,313 JPEG at x=1667, y=1303, width 328, height 319. [Salt Spring Archives, PDF p. 10](https://saltspringarchives.com/driftwood/1990/v31n24June13-1990A.pdf#page=10)
- June 20, 1990, *Gulf Islands Driftwood*, p. B11, lower-right advertisement. This printing repeats the same flag, lettering and layout. [Salt Spring Archives, PDF p. 11](https://saltspringarchives.com/driftwood/1990/v31n25June20-1990B.pdf#page=11)

Both archived pages are scans. The local holdings and the published sources checked yielded no original vector for the complete mark. An original recruitment brochure, application package or production mechanical from the Ministry of Environment remains a lead.

## Reconstruction

The attached crop supplied the tracing reference, with the dated ad used to check the drawing. The three lettering areas were isolated, smoothed and fitted to editable Bézier outlines. The flag uses eleven ink paths from the repository’s [Spirit of BC flag](../../../src/assets/flagMark.js), fitted to the printed proportions and slightly thickened to match the narrow wave gaps. The sun is transparent. The single dark ink approximates the newspaper imprint. The artwork uses transparent backgrounds and requires no font files.

The [master SVG](../../../public/initiatives/environment-youth-corps/environment-youth-corps-reconstruction.svg) keeps the flag, each script line and the central lettering in distinct groups. The [source crop](../../../public/initiatives/environment-youth-corps/driftwood-1990-06-13.png) is the default gallery evidence. The initiative uses the reconstruction as its cover and shows both versions in the logo record. The gallery offers the reconstruction as a selectable version.

## Typeface comparisons

- **Frankfurter Medium** by Alan Meeks for Letraset is a close comparison for the rounded ends, circular `o`, and general weight of `youth corps`. The transfer sheet has a closed `p` and separate `u` and `t`. The ad has an open `p` bowl and a joined `ut`. [Original Letraset transfer sheet](https://letraslut.com/wiki/index.php?title=File:Frankfurter_Medium_48.jpg) · [ITC font listing](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/frankfurter-font-itc)
- **ITC Bauhaus Heavy** shares the open `p` construction, with squared terminals and a different `y`. [ITC specimen](https://fast.fonts.net/FontsCom/Live/ulc-scans/Volume%2024-2%20%28Low%20Res%29.pdf)
- **Brush Script MT Italic** has a similarly curved capital `E` and connected lowercase rhythm. Its `B`, `C`, and standard `t` differ from the advertisement’s capitals and extended crossbars. [Monotype font listing](https://www.myfonts.com/collections/brush-script-font-monotype-imaging)

The printings establish the letter shapes. Their typefaces and lettering credits remain unconfirmed.
