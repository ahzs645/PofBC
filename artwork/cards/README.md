# Cards: source packages

The reconstruction packages behind the **Cards** view (`?view=cards`), kept as supplied minus their
reference photographs, comparison images and raster previews.

| Folder | Card | Used by the site as |
|---|---|---|
| `forest-service-card/` | British Columbia Forest Service, “England Expects Every Man to Do His Duty.” Do Yours: Prevent Forest Fires. | `src/cards/forest-service/england-expects.svg`: `artwork/forest-service-card.svg` with its ids prefixed `fsc-`, its title reworded and inherited paint removed (the package README says how; it renders identically) |

The card reuses this repository's Lands Department seal (`src/internal/forest-service-lands-department.svg`),
its parts moved and scaled to the card's layout; the package's copy of that file is left out. These are
reconstructions, not authenticated masters, and imply no affiliation with or rights in the historical designs.
