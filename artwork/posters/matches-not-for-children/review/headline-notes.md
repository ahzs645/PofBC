# Reconstructed headline

- Two original lines: `MATCHES ARE NOT` and `FOR CHILDREN !!`.
- 28 independently editable outline paths, organized by word and occurrence; no bitmap embedding or font dependency.
- The source script draws manually authored smooth Bezier contours from letter construction, then places them within measured glyph extents on a normalized 1000 x 1400 reference plane. Pixel segmentation is used only to measure placement and inspect alignment, never to derive vector contours.
- Reusable glyph masters include intentional occurrence variants for the second A, first T, second and third E, lower C, lower H, first lower R and lower N.
- This is a reconstruction of lettering visible in the photo, not an identification of a historical digital typeface. Fine edge texture, damage and small pen irregularities are intentionally not duplicated.
- The normalized reference plane is estimated from the photographed poster corners; it is not evidence of the original physical poster size.

Files: headline.svg (full canvas); headline.py (reproducible hand-authored source); headline-detail-vector.png (visual review); headline-overlay.png (diagnostic overlay; reference ink teal, vector ink magenta, shared coverage dark).

Final visual refinement: lightened the S lower hook, lowered and narrowed the lower C terminal, and introduced a small rise in the lower E crossbar/foot. The lower H bar retains its checked placement.
