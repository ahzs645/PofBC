CONSTRUCTION
The artwork was manually rebuilt from the supplied reference using seven
editable vector objects, rather than extracting or automatically tracing the
raster outline:
  1 curved badge, constructed with symmetrical cubic Bezier curves
  1 mainland silhouette
  3 independent island silhouettes
  1 triangular tree crown
  1 rectangular tree trunk

The three main SVG groups are named Badge, Mainland and islands, and Evergreen.
The tree crown and trunk overlap slightly to prevent a rendering seam. The
mainland continues behind the tree; its concealed boundary is reconstructed.

The main SVG has a transparent surrounding canvas. The map and islands remain
solid white objects; they are not transparent holes. The white-background
version adds a separate background object.

PALETTE
Badge green:  #278F38
Tree green:   #00240B
Map/islands:  #FFFFFF

These are flat-colour approximations of the supplied image, not verified
official colour specifications. Raster texture and shading were intentionally
removed. The badge and tree were geometrically regularized while retaining
the visual proportions of the supplied design.

The SVG contains no embedded images, fonts, filters, masks or linked assets.
The PDF was checked for embedded raster images (none) and rendered separately
for visual inspection. This is a reconstruction, not an original official
vector master or a claim of ownership of the emblem.
