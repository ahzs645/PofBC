# Reconstructed poster signature

## Deliverable

`signature.svg` is a transparent, self-contained SVG with `viewBox="0 0 460 60"`. It contains 25 individually editable Bézier stroke paths, organized into Robert / Davidson / 1985 groups. Stroke widths are 1.35–1.8 units, with round caps and joins. Ink is slate `#485052`.

There are no font glyphs, bitmap images, filters, or external resources. The artist name and year in the SVG title/description are accessibility metadata, not rendered lettering.

## Reference

The primary reference is the signature in supplied photograph `46925791_2.jpg`, approximately x1070–1530 and y445–505 in its 1800×1350 image. `46925791_3.jpg` shows the Expo logo and printed artist credit rather than the handwritten signature.

The name **Robert Davidson** and year **1985** are clear. The construction preserves the signature's large looped R, tall narrow b ascender, long t crossbar, open D, connected lower-case rhythm, descending n terminal, and the broad gap before the four year numerals. Curves were authored manually from the letter structures; no automatic bitmap tracing or handwriting-font substitution was used.

## Fidelity limits

The image resolves the main strokes, but glass reflections, low contrast, blur, and small photographed pen widths obscure some overlaps. In particular, the small o/leg crossing after R, the fast r/t transition in Robert, and the i/d/s joins in Davidson are reconstructed as smooth connections. The precise pen pressure, small ink breaks, and individual pixel irregularities are not retained. This is a visual signature component for the recreated historical poster, not an original autograph asset.

The source photograph has mild perspective. The normalized asset retains the visible handwriting proportions; placement and any whole-poster perspective normalization belong to the poster layout.

## Placement

The artwork footprint is approximately x4–451 and y3–55 inside the 460×60 viewBox. It can be placed below the right-hand part of the artwork, scaling the whole group uniformly. The year begins near x369, preserving the source's space between the artist name and date.
