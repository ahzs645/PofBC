# Layout and copy audit

## Reference and registration

All measurements use the original 4576 × 13715 pixel JPEG, normalized to 1000 units wide: 4.576 source pixels per poster unit. The full poster viewBox is `0 0 1000 2997.159090909091`. The earlier 1000-pixel preview has a slightly different height and was not used for final measurement. Native crop registrations are copied unchanged from `source-registration.json`; the northwestern toad crop starts at x = 187.937 units to preserve its visible left hind foot.

## Copy

The wording, punctuation, capitalization and line breaks in the existing transcription match the source. All text ink boxes were remeasured against the native JPEG. The historical wording is preserved, including “Over 80 species,” “and /or veterans,” and the absence of a final period after “and add nutrients to the soil.” The six animal captions are unchanged.

All six caption source-ink boxes are accompanied by exclusion boxes with 3 units of surrounding paper. These remove the source lettering before fresh vector lettering is assembled. The same boxes are safe whiteplate rectangles when needed; this does not imply the original poster contained separately printed whiteplates.

## Graphic layout

The title rectangle, column divider, six illustration frames and footer logo placements are measured from the native scan. Clean frame rules use the measured centerlines and individual widths. A 1.35-unit band removes the old raster frame before the clean rule is drawn. The hawk extends beyond the frame, so the animal must be drawn after the rule without clipping its complete silhouette to the frame. The scene is not blanked with a whole rectangular whiteplate.

The column divider follows the slight source tilt from (506.1784, 2252.6224) to (504.2576, 2912.3689), with a 1.34-unit width. The older line extended too low. The title background rectangle is (171.3287, 2012.0192, 646.6346, 92.8759).

## Logos and remaining typography limits

The existing PofBC ministry and forest-service vector assets are retained. Their internal paths are not retraced or redesigned. Whole-asset placement is adjusted to match the source ink bounds, including a slight independent horizontal/vertical scale adjustment. The old placements were roughly 1.7–1.8 units too high.

Body copy, title and captions remain a Nimbus Sans substitution; the exact historical typeface has not been authenticated. Fitting each line to its measured source bounds improves spacing and registration but does not make the individual glyph shapes exact. The ministry support lines are also outlined Nimbus Sans. The forest-service oval lettering uses the PofBC Kabel reconstruction. These font differences remain the principal layout mismatch after registration correction.

## Files

- `poster-copy.json`: unchanged source wording with native-measured ink boxes.
- `panel-layout.json`: measured frames, caption exclusions, title background, divider and logo placements.
- `source-registration.json`: authoritative source crop mapping, supplied by the main builder.

Measurements are generally within about 0.2–0.4 poster units for the scanned rule centerlines. The original paper distortion and printing irregularities are retained only where source penwork is fitted; geometric rules are deliberately clean editable vectors.
