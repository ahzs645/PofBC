EB Garamond stands in for Adobe Garamond Pro, the serif the Province's published ministry marks
are lettered in. Adobe Garamond Pro is licensed and is not included; EB Garamond is a different
design (Georg Duffner and Octavio Pardo's revival of Claude Garamont's types), so editable
wording set in it will not match the published marks letter for letter. The published marks
themselves are in artwork/ministry-marks/, drawn by PofBC from the Province's own lettering.

Files: the Latin subsets of EB Garamond 400, 400 italic and 700 as published by Fontsource
(@fontsource/eb-garamond 5.2.5, https://fontsource.org/fonts/eb-garamond), unmodified.
